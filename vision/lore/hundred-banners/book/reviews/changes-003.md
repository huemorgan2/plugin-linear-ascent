## The two rulings that resolve group disagreements

- **The "untended" pair.** Group A prescribed keeping 12 + 33; group B
  prescribed 11 + 36. RULING: **12 and 33 keep the word.** 12's is the
  book's first full-dress lift and its quietest; 33's is spoken over
  the Welding Halls, where "untended" carries the most moral freight
  per syllable. 11 keeps the pattern's one self-explanation instead
  (the clause that teaches "the way it came apart on every freed
  floor") but loses the word; 36 gets Ede's ledger-register in its
  place; 13's clause is cut entire and 17 is thinned twice.
- **The Girdle-sea line (38).** Group B offered recast-as-testimony or
  outright cut. RULING: **keep the sentence and the image; anchor it
  as the world's report** — the fishermen would tell it for the rest
  of their lives, and the host had it from them after. The scram scene
  stays inside Vega's skull; the epilogue keeps the panorama's crown
  (the day with its own name, the no-courier close) and loses only the
  middle replay.
