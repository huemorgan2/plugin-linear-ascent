## 1. The Genre Faithful

**Verdict: LOVED.**

Cleaner than the draft I loved a round ago. The continuity noise is gone and
the payoffs now land on rested ears: Wat Kello is Wat Kello all the way to
the epilogue's raised banner, and the reader who wept at "Bread" in
`33-the-welded.md` now arrives at the Aldis salute in `39-last-door.md`
holding the right name. The K6 plant sits in Cindermaw's mouth exactly where
it belonged — "He built the one tower tall enough to see the whole world
from — and in eighty years he has never once looked" (`19-wyrms-pity.md`) —
so `38a-stolen-years.md`'s "the Wyrm had said it of him" is finally a memory
and not an invention. The forge-oath half-plant in `06-the-adit.md` ("Light
no fire I cannot quench; keep no fire past its work; and answer for —" she
said... and for once in her life did not finish it") is better than the fix
we asked for: the unfinished clause is the very clause `38-scram.md` turns
on. With the noise gone, what's left visible is smaller and older. Notes,
ranked:

1. **The banner wears two different devices, and one of them is the book's
   soul.** In `04-king-of-nothing.md` Nix hides behind "a device Nix had
   dusted a hundred times and never once asked about — a wall of squared
   stones, and above the wall an open hand," and asks after it later: "Wall
   and open hand." In `06-the-adit.md` he plants "the banner, the grey one,
   wall and open hand." From `08-what-the-freed-forgive.md` onward — "the
   sewn wall and its crooked star" — through the whole of
   `08a-first-banner.md` ("Her wall, her star. It came out crooked"), to
   Aldis herself in `39-last-door.md` ("I sewed the star crooked. It is
   still crooked"), the device is a wall and a crooked star, and the crooked
   star is a keystone. Two files carry the wrong emblem on the book's
   central object. Fix: in `04`, "a wall of squared stones, and above the
   wall a small star, sewn crooked" (and the later "Wall and open hand"
   line to match); in `06`, "wall and crooked star." Two lines. This is the
   single cheapest high-value repair on the table this round.

2. **Cindermaw's three armies and Dun's first climb still can't both be
   true, and the Champion chain deserves better.** `08a-first-banner.md`:
   Aldis's climb is *the* first — "first banners, first lifts opened, first
   names on the Stone, hers cut first of all." `19-wyrms-pity.md`:
   Cindermaw's "first army climbed angry. It reached the fortieth floor in
   its fury"; the *second* "climbed clever — quiet, patient, well-led. It
   got higher" — and `39-last-door.md` takes Aldis alive at floor 99, which
   is the second army's profile, not the first's. One clause in the wyrm's
   mouth closes it without touching Dun's telling: "The first climbed
   angry — a rabble of the early grief, no banners on it yet. It reached
   the fortieth floor in its fury..." Then Dun's "first climb" is the first
   climb of the banners, Cindermaw's ledger stands, and the reveal at `39`
   is untouched.

3. **Ulfar's gentle mathematics changes its own figure between its two
   great scenes.** `18-brother-and-sister.md`, three times: "Thirty-one
   years," "a generation, bearing the whole world down." Then at the
   scram-bar,
   `38-scram.md`: "Sixty years, Vega. One generation of a lit core," and
   the core's hum is "sixty years long." Same survey, same man, and Ulfar
   is precisely the character who would never round a figure. Pick
   thirty-one — it is the *counted* number, and it is his — and sweep `38`
   (three touches, one word each).

4. **On D14 (deferred docket): endorse the fix.** Vyx on her cocooned
   champion, `14-hostage.md`: "Taken in the Men's wars, four tiers up,
   ninety years gone." Ninety predates the Theft while "four tiers up" is
   tower grammar; the hard canon says tower-era spans read in decades. Make
   it "taken in the Men's wars, four tiers up, in the tower's first fury"
   — keeps her precision, loses the impossible number.

5. **On D5 (overturn).** The teenager wants two of Vega's three probes cut
   from `18-brother-and-sister.md`. Read again with fresh eyes: the three
   probes are thirteen lines *total* — couplings, seats, feed — and "Three
   ways she went at it, and three ways it held" is the sentence that makes
   Vega's "The figures are sound, brother. I cannot fault them" cost what
   it must cost. Cut two probes and her concession is taken on faith; the
   whole argument of the book's best two-hander goes soft. Overturn.

6. **Praise, filed so nobody regresses it:** the held-breath dedup made the
   Warden-beats sing again. `04`'s replacement — "split its seam with a
   sound like a stopper drawn from a year-sealed jug" — is scene-grown
   (Gnarl, cups, the last civilized object) and better than what it
   replaced. One straggler survived in `07-red-warcamp.md`; that is
   self-audit business and it is logged there.

**Do not touch:** the Berrin engine, still — and now specifically the trial
sentence in `36-malgrim.md` and the wordless delivery scene in
`41-epilogue.md` ("nobody spoke, and nobody helped, and nobody stopped
him"). Iteration 1's recast of the confession ("he had gone up the tower a

## 7. The Battle Choreographer

**Verdict: LIKED — five of seven set-pieces drawable from text alone, the
feast still the model; my whole budget goes to the two seams the first
panel already measured and one grounding line it missed.**

1. **D4 — ENDORSE, with the prescription sharpened to three cuts of the
   knife in `35-heralds-gate.md`.** (a) *The named soldier:* the steel hour
   is fought at arm's length ("She would remember very little of that
   walk"), and the hinge needs one close face. Use **Serle Danner** — the
   corporal who said "You sold us" across the fire in
   `08-what-the-freed-forgive.md` and stood at the line's end "daring her
   to send him anywhere but up." Fifteen to twenty lines of him anchoring
   the third rank at the Anvil, the best hedge-fighter in the second file
   doing lane-work under ballista sleet, closes an arc the book opened
   sixty files ago and gives the morning its Shieldwall face. (b) *The
   general's beat:* Ede's last action is at dawn ("went to her place under
   the hundred banners") and her next is receiving the Marshal's sword in
   the afternoon — in the book's Pelennor the commander has no on-page
   command between first charge and surrender. One paragraph at the Anvil:
   Ede reading the bow of the line, refusing to commit the reserve,
   choosing to let the bell's timetable spend her people — which also
   returns the chapter's bitter arithmetic to the person who owns it. (c)
   *The scale seam:* "the yards themselves ran beyond any seeing... wall to
   far wall a hard day's march" cannot contain "the shield-line bowed
   across the yards like a drawn bow." One clause localizes the battle to
   the gatehouse approach — one corner of a country-sized square — which
   also explains how the choir walks the Bell-Road from mid-morning to noon
   and arrives.

2. **D7 — ENDORSE, MODIFIED.** `07-red-warcamp.md` still delivers its
   emotional crest secondhand: "It was Nix who silenced it. Later she
   pieced it together from a dozen tellings." Ede is on the Paylane; the
   chapter's own geography says the drum-towers "stood over" the
   tent-lines; she can SEE the third tower. Prescription: cut the
   "pieced it together" frame, give her three live sentences — the small
   figure going up the lashed frame, the drums dying one frame at a time,
   the Break-collar working Vega had read off the pen-wall doing its
   costly work — and KEEP the "one picture outlived the rest" hearth-wall
   paragraph exactly where it is, because that one is explicitly the
   histories' image and earns its distance. Four lines swapped, the crest
   goes live, the myth-frame survives.

3. **D8 — ENDORSE.** `32-siege-of-outworks.md` briefs three steps — "Get
   the work-gangs clear. Fire the gas. Drop the King's first wall into the
   King's own hole" — and then executes five: *Unrivet* strips the rampart
   face, the collar-breakers assault INTO the breach, Rivetgrim
   counterattacks, THEN clear and fire. The breach-assault is the chapter's
   whole middle and it reads as improvisation. One briefing line where the
   plan is stated: "first the word would open the plate and the breakers
   would empty the breach of the driven — so the wall's garrison died
   freed instead of buried — then the gangs clear, then the gas." The
   battle becomes the executed plan it visibly wants to be.

4. **New find — the scattering still falls on an unplaced army.**
   `25-storm-queens-price.md`: when Zephyra spends her cellars, "down the
   long slopes, where the host lay camped by companies along the
   wind-shadows of the tier — down where the army was —" is the FIRST time
   the host's position is given, mid-catastrophe. One sentence during the
   embassy's climb ("Below and behind, the host strung its camps down the
   lee of the tier, company by company in the wind-shadows, waiting on the
   parley's word") converts the scattering from spectacle into witnessed
   geography. The first panel measured this and it fell off the budget;
   it should not fall off twice — the scattering drives three chapters.

5. **Verified clean, for the record:** the ladder-fixes introduced no
   choreography wobble — `25`'s vote arithmetic (27 + 27 + 4 = 58 with the
   general's fifty-ninth) still closes; `22-the-feast.md` remains the
   model and nobody has tightened it; the walk toward the horns in
   `28-the-coursing.md` now carries Ede's own banked coal ("walk to the
   horn and the horn falls silent" surfacing from the ash-lane) and the
   bravest walk in the book finally belongs to her memory, not Rhovan's
   lore. That fix reads as if it had always been there.

**Do not touch:** `28-the-coursing.md`, entire — the hunt that stays "one
glade behind" for three nights, the foxglove drift pulling Ede downhill
