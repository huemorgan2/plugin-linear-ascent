# AI-scent review — does the book sound like AI?

*Full pass over the final edition (59 files, ~164k words), 2026-08-13. Method:
a research-backed rubric of LLM stylistic tells, a mechanical vocabulary
sweep, then seven parallel close-reads covering every chapter and appendix.
File names below are final-edition file names, with line numbers approximate.*

## The rubric's sources

- Wikipedia, "Signs of AI writing" (WikiProject AI Cleanup) —
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing
- Juzek & Ward, "Why Does ChatGPT 'Delve' So Much?" (COLING 2025) —

- Kobak et al., "Delving into LLM-assisted writing… through excess

- "How to Identify AI-Written Web Fiction" — recordcrash.substack.com
- "Spotting machine-made prose" — carly.substack.com
- Julie Holmes, "AI Writing Tropes: The Complete List" — julieholmes.com
- "AI Writing Tropes to Avoid" — gist.github.com/ossa-ma (tropes.fyi)
- Steve Fenton, "The 6 Warning Signs of an AI-Generated Book" —
  stevefenton.co.uk

Core principle from all of them: no single tell proves anything; the signal
is density — three tells co-occurring in a passage, or one device firing
habitually.

## Verdict

The book does not read AI-written at the sentence level. The mechanical
sweep found **zero** AI-vocabulary hits ("delve" is diegetic miners' talk,
"realm" means kingdom); there is no brochure worldbuilding, almost no stock
emotional beats in 164k words (three or four total — remarkable), no synonym
cycling, ragged sentence music, and dialogue that interrupts and overlaps.
Chapter endings in the endgame are concrete, not uplift bows. The
concrete-object discipline (lead-strips, mortise-gates, sledge-shoes) is the
strongest anti-tell in the manuscript.

The exposure is **pattern-level**: five house devices fire on autopilot at a
frequency a suspicious reader can count. Each instance is craft; the
aggregate is a fingerprint.

## The five systemic habits, ranked

### 1. The negative-parallelism pivot — the book's #1 exposure

"Not X. Y." / "It was not X. It was Y." / "Not X, not Y. Z." — the single
most-cited LLM tell, and the book's default dramatic hinge. Counts per
reviewer batch: ~37 (files 00–07), ~45 (08–12a), ~40 (13–19), ~33
(19a–24), ~49 (25–31), ~41 (31a–37), ~38 (38–41). **Roughly 280 across the
book, ~5–6 per chapter, every chapter, every POV.** Nix, Ede, Vega, Rhovan,
and the narrator all fire it identically, which also flattens POV
differentiation.

Sub-tic: the **italicized corrective verb** at dramatic peaks — "not barred,
not chained. *Welded.*" / "did not break. It *woke*" / "not blown out;
*banked*" / "It had *risen*" / "not stormed but *woken*" / "I am
*invested*" — 15+ instances, always at the climax, italics doing the
lifting. Two of these are craft; fifteen are a template.

Worst concentrations: **12-out-mourned-king** (~9, four italicized, the
whole climax runs on the hinge), **35-heralds-gate** (~8),
**08a-first-banner** (~8), **07-red-warcamp** (~8–9), **40-the-throne**
(~9–10).

Fix: one global pass cutting roughly half, prioritizing narration over
dialogue (Vyx/Malgrim/Voss contract-registers legitimately antithesize).
Ration the italic-corrective to one per chapter and strip italics from half.

### 2. The "the way X does Y" simile scaffold

"the way you close a door on winter" / "the way a lie drops when the last
man stops telling it" / "the way you call someone home across fields at
dusk" — 3–8 per chapter, 150+ book-wide. It is the book's signature and
many instances are superb; the syntax never varies, and at this frequency
the template itself becomes visible (uniform-cadence tell). Also a repeated
mold within it: "[verb] the way you [domestic act]: [adv], [adv], all the
way to the [noun]" (files 06 and 07).

Fix: vary the connective in about a third of instances — bare metaphor,
apposition, or nothing.

### 3. Aphorism density over the book's own cap

The style guide budgets one keystone per chapter; actual counts run 3–5 in
at least a dozen chapters (05, 08, 08a, 09, 13-silk-terms/14-hostage batch,
17, 18, 19a, 21, 23, 25, 30, 31a, 35, 40a). Nearly every maxim is built on
the same antithesis chassis, and narrator and characters coin in one voice
("every paragraph resolves to a quotable"). This is the "false profundity /
asserted clarity" tell at manuscript scale. Related tic: the narrator
grading its own dialogue — "Rhovan put it best, as he put everything best"
(35-heralds-gate).

Fix: per chapter, keep the keystone, demote the rest to plain statement.
36-malgrim and the interludes show what the kept budget looks like.

### 4. The asserted-significance formula

"That was the cruelest part." / "That was the unbearable part." / "That was
what made it dreadful." / "That was the whole of the miracle." — ~13
instances book-wide (seven in files 25–31 alone). One shape, many costumes;
the most fingerprint-like single habit after the pivot. Cousin formula:
"did what she was for / what Berrin was for / what he was made for" — five
hits in four chapters (22–24).

Fix: keep two book-wide; elsewhere the shown detail already carries it.

### 5. Duplicated constructions and repeated theses (local-minimum drone)

Near-verbatim pairs a suspicious reader reads as model drone:

- "not burning, not breaking. Cutting" (14-hostage) vs "not breaking, not
  burning. Standing" (17-frozen-sea) — same verbs, same rhythm. **High.**
- Growing-army thesis fully stated twice: 32-siege-of-outworks ~L292 and
  35-heralds-gate ~L379 ("every other army got smaller by fighting").
  **High.**
- "The tower believes it is fighting an army…" thesis stated three times
  across 19a-what-roothollow-sends and 21a-the-wintering.
- Consecutive dragon-trailer chapter endings: 17-frozen-sea and
  18-brother-and-sister both close on "ahead… something older than the
  tower… waiting."
- "One step, roar. Next step, nothing." (26a-bottled-court / 27-the-eye).
- "wide enough to rest an army in" (26 / 26a, identical wording).
- "grief older than the griever" (25 / 26a).
- "knew before his/the mind did" (26a / 31-breaking-the-hunt).
- "the way quartermasters read: fast, and twice" (39-last-door /
  40a-the-trials).
- "argued from the morning horn to the dousing of the lamps" (21 / 21a).
- "like a fist unclenching" (03) vs "like a hand unclenching" (04).
- "Giants finish their sentences" surfaces in prose four times (21 ×2, 21a,
  24) — the style-bible note leaking into the text.
- "a nation moving" three times in three consecutive chapters (16–19 files);
  keep the 16-the-molt close.
- Both welded-drake Wardens in 24-anchor-chains die into the same
  release-shot image.
- Negated-light description three chapters running: "not white, not pale,
  but *absent*" (38) / "Not the Yoke's dead white. Not lamp-gold." (38a) /
  "Not the black of night… but the black of adamant" (39).
- Ch-ending climb-step ("…and climbed") closes at least three chapters
  (08, 12, 12a files).
- Three consecutive ominous-progressive closers: "The gathering had begun."
  (25) / "was already watching the gap" (26) / "the hunt was already
  moving." (27).

## The passages most likely to be screenshotted as "AI wrote this"

1. **35-heralds-gate ~L426–431** — closing sentence: participial
   "remembering," expanding-scope triad ("the tower's story too, and the
   world's…"), full uplift bow. The single most AI-flavored sentence in the
   book. End on the ticking brass.
2. **30-what-ede-carries ~L52–55 and ~L100–108** — aphorism stacked on
   run-on triad capped with a second aphorism; then pivot + anaphora triad +
   door aphorism in one paragraph. Three tells co-occurring, twice.
3. **12-out-mourned-king climax run** — eight-plus pivots, four italicized
   correctives, in one chapter. Keep *folded* and summons/lament; flatten
   the rest.
4. **11-banner-war ~L81–84** — "the whole floor seemed to hold its breath…
   as if the ground itself wanted to hear" — stock beat + dash-appositive
   drama + as-if significance in one sentence.
5. **40-the-throne ~L279–284** — anaphora + triple parallel negation +
   closing aphorism at the climax; the exact cadence LLMs default to at
   climaxes. Break the triple's symmetry.
6. **03-hedge-and-hollow ending** — "A hundred of them. All stolen. All
   waiting." Fragment-triad drum-roll bow.
7. **16-the-molt ending** — recap-inventory meditation capped by "It was
   the sound of a nation moving."
8. **18-brother-and-sister double ending** — two stacked trailers; cut one,
   end on "did not speak of the vellum again."
9. **09-the-quiet council scene** — five-plus maxims, every councillor
   exits on an epigram; give the quartermasters' "simply, bread" more room.
10. **20-long-stair ~L279** — "But that is the next part of the story." The
    one narrator wink at the camera in the book.

## Modern-idiom slips (banned register, reads 2020s)

- "load-bearing" — 01-dramatis-personae ~L173 ("Both facts are
  load-bearing.")

## Appendix findings

- **02-gazetteer** — all ~100 entries follow one four-sentence template,
  and nearly every closing line is a micro-uplift bow ("and it held," "at
  peace," "worth the fight"). Sampling ten entries in a row exposes the
  metronome. Break the template every 6–8 entries: one with no keeper named,
  one disputed, one that ends badly, one two sentences long.
- **01-dramatis-personae** — ~30 entries all close on the same
  antithetical-epigram snap ("Never became good; became invested"). Let a
  third end flat and factual.
- Charter, Vyx contract, Malgrim's ledger, on-the-yoke, interludes —
  clean; their registers are diegetic and the uniformity is the joke.

## Clean chapters — the models to thin toward

**36-malgrim** (best in book: dialogue-driven, one earned keystone,
parallelism lives in a designed register), **28-the-coursing**,
**20-long-stair**, **02-third-refusal**, **08x/16x/23x interludes**,
**03-charter-of-frosthall**.

## Recommended edit passes, in order of value

1. Global negative-parallelism thinning: cut ~half, narration first;
   ration italic-correctives to one per chapter. (Biggest single win.)
2. Dedupe the verbatim pairs and double-stated theses listed above.
3. Enforce the one-keystone-per-chapter cap the style guide already states.
4. Kill the "that was the X part" formula down to two instances book-wide.
5. Vary the simile connective in ~a third of "the way X does Y" instances.
6. Fix the three modern-idiom slips (five-minute job).
7. Break the gazetteer template and the personae epigram cadence.
8. Retouch the ten screenshot passages above.
