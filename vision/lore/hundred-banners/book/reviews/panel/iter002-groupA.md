## 1. The Genre Faithful

**Verdict: LOVED.**

so `38a-stolen-years.md`'s "the Wyrm had said it of him" is finally a memory
and not an invention. The forge-oath half-plant in `06-the-adit.md` ("Light
no fire I cannot quench; keep no fire past its work; and answer for —" she
said... and for once in her life did not finish it") is better than the fix
we asked for: the unfinished clause is the very clause `38-scram.md` turns
on. With the noise gone, what's left visible is smaller and older. Notes,
ranked:

1. **The banner wears two different devices, and one of them is the book's
   soul.** In `04-king-of-nothing.md` Nix hides behind "a device Nix had
   dusted a hundred times and never once asked about — a wall of squared
   stones, and above the wall an open hand," and asks after it later: "Wall
   and open hand." In `06-the-adit.md` he plants "the banner, the grey one,
   wall and open hand." From `08-what-the-freed-forgive.md` onward — "the
   sewn wall and its crooked star" — through the whole of
   `08a-first-banner.md` ("Her wall, her star. It came out crooked"), to
   Aldis herself in `39-last-door.md` ("I sewed the star crooked. It is
   still crooked"), the device is a wall and a crooked star, and the crooked
   star is a keystone. Two files carry the wrong emblem on the book's
   central object. Fix: in `04`, "a wall of squared stones, and above the
   wall a small star, sewn crooked" (and the later "Wall and open hand"
   line to match); in `06`, "wall and crooked star." Two lines. This is the
   single cheapest high-value repair on the table this round.

2. **Cindermaw's three armies and Dun's first climb still can't both be
   true, and the Champion chain deserves better.** `08a-first-banner.md`:
   Aldis's climb is *the* first — "first banners, first lifts opened, first
   names on the Stone, hers cut first of all." `19-wyrms-pity.md`:

   got higher" — and `39-last-door.md` takes Aldis alive at floor 99, which

   mouth closes it without touching Dun's telling: "The first climbed
   angry — a rabble of the early grief, no banners on it yet. It reached
   the fortieth floor in its fury..." Then Dun's "first climb" is the first
   climb of the banners, Cindermaw's ledger stands, and the reveal at `39`
   is untouched.

3. **Ulfar's gentle mathematics changes its own figure between its two
   great scenes.** `18-brother-and-sister.md`, three times: "Thirty-one
   years," "a generation, bearing the whole world down." Then at the

   the core's hum is "sixty years long." Same survey, same man, and Ulfar
   is precisely the character who would never round a figure. Pick
   thirty-one — it is the *counted* number, and it is his — and sweep `38`

4. **On D14 (deferred docket): endorse the fix.** Vyx on her cocooned

5. **On D5 (overturn).** The teenager wants two of Vega's three probes cut

6. **Praise, filed so nobody regresses it:** the held-breath dedup made the
   Warden-beats sing again. `04`'s replacement — "split its seam with a
   sound like a stopper drawn from a year-sealed jug" — is scene-grown

## 7. The Battle Choreographer

**Verdict: LIKED — five of seven set-pieces drawable from text alone, the
feast still the model; my whole budget goes to the two seams the first

1. **D4 — ENDORSE, with the prescription sharpened to three cuts of the
   knife in `35-heralds-gate.md`.** (a) *The named soldier:* the steel hour

   `08-what-the-freed-forgive.md` and stood at the line's end "daring her

   Ede reading the bow of the line, refusing to commit the reserve,

3. **D8 — ENDORSE.** `32-siege-of-outworks.md` briefs three steps — "Get

   plan is stated: "first the word would open the plate and the breakers

5. **Verified clean, for the record:** the ladder-fixes introduced no

**Do not touch:** `28-the-coursing.md`, entire — the hunt that stays "one
