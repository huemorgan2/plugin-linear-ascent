## Critic 2 — The Literary Critic

**Verdict: LIKED.**

The line surgery of two rounds has mostly taken. The lamp-gutter
dedup­lication produced replacements that belong to their floors better
than the tic ever did — `17-frozen-sea.md`'s Orla listens "for the
