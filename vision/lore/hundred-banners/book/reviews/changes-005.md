## Rulings made in application

- **Weck's simile is "easy as a posted tariff," not "easy as
  rent-day."** The review's proposed coin was checked against the live
  book before spending: `09-the-quiet.md`:145 already holds "methodical
  as rent-day" (the tower's answer coming down through the floors).
  Giving Weck "as rent-day" would have minted the exact defect class
  this finding kills — one simile worn by two registers. The
  replacement stays in Weck's own trade (they are standing at his
  toll-post; his warrants are "All stamped"), keeps the review's "easy"
  over the chapter's overworked "comfort," and quietly plants the
  tariff Rhovan later beats him with ("the tariff Voss believed and the
  tariff Voss was owed," same chapter). Rhovan keeps "mild as milk" at
  `02`:27 — the convening order left the wearer to the editor, and the
  panel's reasoning holds: "That's one," said again, mild as milk, is
  the book's best-earned use of the coin.
- **The triplet openings: two of three varied, per the convening
  order.** The review re-cut `24` alone; the order ruled two. `24`
  takes the review's wording as filed. `26` takes the smallest touch
  that de-rhymes the remaining pair: `23` and `26` shared the "[Rhovan]
  had spent a (long) life [gerund]" head, so `26` now opens "Rhovan had
  made his living finding things that did not want to be found. He had
  never hated the work until the things were his own." — the verb
  phrase swapped into Rhovan's coin-and-ledger register and the single
  breath split, so neither the head (against `23`) nor the one-sentence
  mold (against the new `24`) rhymes. `23` stands untouched, as the
  strongest of the three.
- **`28`'s six 79-column lines are not this round's.** The width sweep
  of every touched file turned up six lines at 79 columns in
  `28-the-coursing.md`, all outside the two repair sites and all
  pre-existing. Per the standing record, pre-existing long lines belong
  to the final-edition column sweep; entered as watched, not spent.
- **A fourth "four hundred and six" noted and left.** `08a`:26 ("—
  four hundred and six, four hundred and seven — you've gone back")
  is Nix counting through the number, not a count landing on it; it
  was outside the review's three and needs no cure. On the record so
  the final edition's counts-sweep does not rediscover it.
- **Keystone verification was run at the book's final wording.** The
  editorial-pass drafts were re-cut in earlier iterations per the
  anti-copy rule; the eight keystones were verified at their live
  sites (listed in the checklist below), and no edit this round
