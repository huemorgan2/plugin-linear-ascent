## The routed docket (from changes-004, dispositioned here)

**1. FIX — `38-scram.md`, the Girdle-sea clause (routed by C10 /
changes-004 refusals).** Read in place:

> "the fishermen would tell it for the rest of their lives, and the
> host had it from them after, as the world's report: the last veils
> dying together,"

The seam shows. "as the world's report" restates what "the host had it
from them after" has just done; the anchoring the iteration-3 ruling
wanted lives entirely in the fishermen-and-host frame, and the trailing
label is the one visible stitch. Fix: cut ", as the world's report" and
re-flow — "…and the host had it from them after: the last veils dying
together…" The money line and the grey-rung exception stand untouched.

**2. FIX — `05-ledger-of-gates.md`:78, "mild as milk" (routed
line-bundle).** "'The bread pays,' Weck observed, mild as milk" doubles
Rhovan's fingerprint from `02`:27 ("'That's one,' he said again, mild as
milk") — the book's two mildest threats wearing the same simile, forty
pages apart, on two different men. Fix: give Weck "easy as rent-day"
rather than the docket's "comfortable as rent-day" — the chapter already
has Weck saying things "comfortably" (line 56) and "the comfort leave
the toll-captain's face" (line 199); a third "comfort" would trade one
echo for another. Rhovan keeps the coin.

**3. FIX — `20-long-stair.md`, the counting-pair (routed line-bundle).**
Line 121: "The cord was for the dead but the counting was for
everything; counting was how he held the world still enough to look
at." Line 274: "because counting was how you held a thing still enough
to see what it really was." One image, cashed twice in one chapter.
Fix: end the first at "…but the counting was for everything." and keep
the chapter-closing instance, which is the load-bearing one.

**4. FIX — `32-siege-of-outworks.md`:45, "until the lamps guttered
twice" (routed, wording on file).** Guttering lamps already time seven
other chapters, including `19a`'s moot vote ("It took them until the
lamps guttered"), and this instance sits two floors from that one. Fix
as filed: "argued out over a light-table until the watch had twice
changed."

**5. FIX — Sliver's badge, trimmed at `15` and `21` (routed
line-bundle).** "I have carried forty-one climbers" and its variants
appear nine times (`02`, `03` K1, `04`, `08`, `09`, `15`, `21`, `39`,
appendix `01`). The choosing, the keystone, the Kingsfield verdict, the
Quiet's cold coda, and the Aldis reveal all earn it; `15`:212 and
`21`:34 are the two places it is only throat-clearing before the actual
sentence. Fix `15`: "*I will tell you the only thing four ages taught
me about hunting hidden things,*" the splinter said… Fix `21`: "*Not
one climber I have carried was ever asked for that before. I find I am
glad to have lasted.*" Seven instances remain, every one pulling
weight.

**6. FIX — `24-anchor-chains.md`:3, the twin opening (routed:
`23`/`24` templates).** `23` opens "Rhovan of the Grey Rides had spent
a long life sorting…"; `24` opens "Vega Coalbrow had drawn chain all
her life, and she had never feared it until…"; and the count is
actually three, because `26` opens "Rhovan had spent a life finding
things that did not want to be found, and he had never hated the work
until…" — the same "[Name] had [verb]ed X all their life / never…
until" mold three times in four chapters. Re-cutting `24` alone breaks
the drumbeat and leaves `23`/`26` non-adjacent. Fix: "Chain was Vega
Coalbrow's first trade, and she had never feared it until she walked
upon one above the clouds." Nothing else in the paragraph moves.
