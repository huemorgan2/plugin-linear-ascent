## Critic 1 — The Genre Faithful

**Verdict: LOVED.** This is now a book I would press on a friend with both
hands, and I reread the whole thing looking for reasons not to.

**Verification of iteration 3.** The summit stars land. "They crowded the
black from rim to rim, drifts and shoals of them" over "the last of the
air" is awe delivered at the right altitude, and the Nix beat is exactly
the right size — *"In the terms," Nix agreed* (`40-the-throne.md`), then he
loses the count on purpose and starts again. No violin. The plant (Dun over
the banner-hem in `08a-first-banner.md` — "The ground keeps thousands;
you'll see them when we've done the work — that's in the terms") pays
across seventy floors to the person it was said to, which is the Tolkien
trick done honestly. `40a-the-trials.md`'s "owed nobody a dawn" not only
survives the starry sky, it improves under it: no dawns, and stars anyway.
Gnarl's new veil-lift in `04-king-of-nothing.md` is the round's best single
addition — the dead air breaking, "every cloth on the Kingsfield lifted out
full together," closing on "applause at the end… The house was up" — it
turns a mercy-kill into a curtain call, which is what Gnarl was owed.

1. **The sixty thousand arrive unaudited.** `35-heralds-gate.md`: "the
   rising had marched to the Herald's Gate that dawn some sixty thousand
   strong." The last hard number this book gave me was about eleven
   thousand at the Eye (`27-the-eye.md`), and this book taught me to count
   — so I counted, and the jump rests on implication (the Bastion Line
   wheeling over in `34-viziers-floor.md`, the warren's four thousand)
   rather than on one auditable beat. Awe in this book has always been
   earned arithmetic; this is the one place it is asserted. Remedy: one
   clause at the `34` muster or `35`'s forming-up — the Bastion Line alone
   doubling the rolls — and sixty thousand becomes a payoff instead of a
   claim. **NICE.**

**Do not touch:** the filling of the hall and the banner-knock in
`40-the-throne.md`. "The tellers always spent their breath on the wrong
marvel" — the book spends it on the right one. That sequence is why this
book exists.

## Critic 2 — The Literary Critic

**Verdict: LOVED, with a pen out.** The prose has stopped needing
defending. Two lines want surgery.

**Verification of iteration 3.** The "untended" heptad is genuinely broken,
not merely thinned. `11-banner-war.md`'s tithe-country register ("The
floor's gloom had no collector now"), `13-silk-terms.md`'s wayshrine lamps
in "the first honest dark," `17-frozen-sea.md`'s shell that "cracked and
drained," `36-malgrim.md`'s ledger-register ("the ninetieth's veil coming
off the books with its keeper") — each lift now grows out of its floor's
own metaphor system. No NEW sameness: the recurring rim-inward motion
("rim-light walking in over the mounds," `12`; "peeling back from the far
rim inward," `20`; "let go from the rim inward," `31`) is the world's
physics, not a tic — light *does* come from the rim — and it reads as law,
not laziness. The two licensed "untended"s (`12`, `33`) and the two
licensed "held breath"s (`12`, `38a`) stand exactly where the ledger left
them; I grepped.

1. **An opaque phrase at the book's most-watched table.**
   `34-viziers-floor.md`: "The charter-table down the middle, the decade of
   it scarred and ringed." Opaque to the point of reading as a typo — the
   charter is a year old, the Frosthall plank is generations old, and "the
   decade of it" is neither; a close reader stops dead at the council the
   whole book has been climbing toward. Remedy: "the oak of it scarred and
   ringed," or "two years of councils scarred and ringed into it."
   **SHOULD.**
2. **One wrong word in the trophy hall.** `29-pale-court.md`: "trophies of
   the Master's freed peers, tokens of Skarn and Hrimgar and the Storm
   Queen." At this story-hour Skarn is dead in his collar and Zephyra is
   still crowned on her crag; "freed" is false of two of the three named,
   and it pre-spoils Zephyra's fate besides. Remedy: "the Master's crowned
   peers" (or "fellow lords"). **SHOULD.**

**Do not touch:** Vyx's second chapter in `14-hostage.md` — "I watched the
underside of a stolen country pass me in the dark. There were still lamps
burning in it." The best paragraph in the book; the panel should put a
fence around it.

## Critic 6 — The Character Surgeon

**Verdict: LOVED — and one alarm, rung loudly, because the last
substantive round is the last chance to ring it.**

The commissioned verification first. **Nix's summit stars
(`40-the-throne.md`): earned, not saccharine.** The beat is built from
Dun's exact words planted at the fire (`08a-first-banner.md`: "you'll
see them when we've done the work — that's in the terms"), it is paid
in Nix's own grammar ("counting — slowly, for once, the way you pay
out a debt you are glad of"), and it ends with him losing the count on
purpose and starting again, which is the character doing the weeping,
not the author. The restraint is total: no tears named, no Dun named.
Protect it. **Berrin's collar struck by hand (`36-malgrim.md`):**
"the freed clerks had struck it off by hand before they brought him"
is better than repair-grade — the tower's own clerks on the unmaking
is a character beat the physics fix bought for free, and "He had not
fought them... He had not anything" is the right amount of Berrin
left. **The Malgrim parley, the trial creed, and Ede's `40a`
self-entry stand untouched and complete.** No rollbacks proposed.

### Findings

**6.1 — MUST-FIX. The climax's one false figure, and both Coalbrows
out of character holding it.** `38-scram.md`: Ulfar's pitch at the
scram-bar now reads "Damp the coils to a tenth... And with the tenth
I keep the engines fed, and I set the floors down... Thirty-one
years." His own survey, checked by his own sister, closed that door
on-page in `18-brother-and-sister.md`: "could the core light the
engines at half-fire, quarter-fire, anything short of full? The
load-curves said what they said. The descent drew the core's full
breath for thirty-one years, no less, or the margins went to
nothing." In `18` the *tenth-damping* belongs to the BROADCAST — "the
broadcast can be throttled from the crown, we believe... a judgment,
not a figure" — never to the engines. As `38` stands, the man who has
never once got a figure wrong states the one arithmetic the book
ruled out, at the climax, and the sister who has never let a figure
stand answers the hours and lets it pass. Two sentences repair it and
nothing else moves: the tenth is the broadcast, throttled from the
Spire; the engines keep the core's full breath — e.g. *"Throttle the
broadcast to a tenth — the Yoke at a tenth is a headache, not a
madness — and the engines keep the core's full breath. Thirty-one
years, Vega."* Her hours-rebuttal and the forge-oath then stand
exactly as written, and both Coalbrows are themselves at the exact
beat the book needs them most. This is the only scene-level wound
left in the manuscript.

**6.2 — MUST. Vega's count of herself is wrong by ten floors.**
`28-the-coursing.md`: "I have been a debt on this climb since floor
six. I would rather end as a payment." She joined at the Gear
Galleries, having "carried up sixteen floors" of manifests
(`06-the-adit.md`). Vega counts exactly even at the edge of dying —
*especially* there; the covert speech is her arithmetic made creed.
One word: "since floor sixteen."

**6.3 — SHOULD. "A year hiding" overshoots Ede's own secret by three
