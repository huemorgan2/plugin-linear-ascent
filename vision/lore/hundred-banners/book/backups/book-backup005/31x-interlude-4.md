# From Malgrim's Ledger

Item: the Pale Court has fallen. Say rather: has been given away. The
Huntsman kept better books than the Queen — every course entered, every
quarry weighed, a thousand years in fair hand — and it availed him
nothing, because his ledger carried one bad clause from before our time,
and the woman found it and walked in through it. Both accounts are now
spent. The stolen realms are a closed column. I do not mourn. Assets
that were liabilities dressed in crowns.

Item: the pack is lost with its master. Enter under depreciation. A
hound that has heard the calloff cannot be re-fevered; the kennel-clerks
are not to waste the attempt.

Item: the rising musters below the outworks at a strength of seventy
banners and thirty-one peoples, under one charter. I have read the
charter. It is short, which is how I know it is dangerous. Long laws are
written to be escaped. Short ones are written to be kept.

Item: they now hold Unspring, Mark, and the calloff-word, which is to
say: our snares, our trackers, and our hunters are so much scrap. The
Made does not hunt and cannot be snared. Let them bring a wood's tricks
to an anvil.

Item: provision to the outworks doubled. The manufactory drums to beat
without interval; the garrisons above the eighty-fourth to be fed before
the garrisons below it. What the tower owns, the tower may spend, and I
intend to spend it by weight.

Item: our friend reports from the muster. The bread arrived before the
army did, as ever; his standing has never been higher; they gave his
wagons an escort of freed hounds, which he found — his word — merry. He
reports also that the woman no longer sleeps. Good. Sleepless generals
sign anything. Note to the willing below: press no advantage yet. A
tired hand signs; a suspicious one checks the figures twice. Let her
tire further.

Item: the hounds took no notice of him. I record this because the
kennel-clerks assure me a hound cannot be deceived about a man, and he
has now walked through ten thousand of them unremarked. Either the
clerks flatter their stock, or my friend is by now so wholly the thing
he pretends that there is nothing false left in him for a dog to find.
I collared him for exactly this quality. On quiet evenings I confess it
unsettles even me.

Item: the siege begins when they touch the first rampart. Open a new
page. Head it: expenditures.
