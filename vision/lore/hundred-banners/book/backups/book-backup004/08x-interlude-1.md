# From Malgrim's Ledger

Item: the Warcamp is spent. Write off the garrison, the drums, and the
Warlord entire. Skarn priced his collar as a rank and defended it as one;
this was always the flaw in him, and I enter it here so the flaw is not
repeated: never issue a collar to a creature that wants it. Wanting it is
the beginning of accounts we cannot audit.

Item: the woman climbs.

Item: sixty-three units of aged inventory have been recovered by the
enemy at floor twenty. I decline to enter this as a loss. Grief is a bond
that matures; every cage she opens, she carries. Let her portage.

Item: our gate-toll investment matures. The Tollmaster's chains have
taught three factions to hate one another at their own expense, which is
the cheapest garrison I have ever kept on the books. Recommend no
interference. Men quarreling over a gate will guard it for nothing.

Item: a goblin has been observed at the head of parleys. Entered under
sundries. Small things are not small costs — I know this better than most
— but there is no line in any ledger for what he is spending, and a
currency we cannot mint we also cannot counterfeit. Watch him.

Item: on the question of reprisal against the low floors — denied, and I
will have this minuted, because below they think me soft for it. Reprisal
is spending fear to buy fear you already own. The quiet floors are quiet
because they remember; a memory is a standing asset; burn a village and
you convert it, at a loss, into a grievance. We do not buy what we hold.

Item: the King's peace is misunderstood by its beneficiaries, which is the
usual fate of sound administration. A hundred floors, a hundred quarrels
kept apart, and not one war among them in an age — the woman below is
climbing to repeal the only peace that has ever balanced. I keep its books
gladly, and I will keep hers when she is entered in them.

Note to the willing below: feed her the trail slowly. Small findings,
honestly come by, at decent intervals — a mark here, a manifest there.
Nothing that cannot bear her checking, for she will check. A hope led is a
hope steered; it is the hauling of it that keeps her ours. And see to it
you grieve well when the trail warms. You have always grieved beautifully.
It is why I collared you.
