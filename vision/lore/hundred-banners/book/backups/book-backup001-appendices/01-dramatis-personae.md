# The Banners, and Who Carried Them

*Compiled at Roothollow after the fall, from the rebuilt muster-roll,
the census-wall, the tally-slates, the Herald's ledger, and the mouths
of the living. One line to a name; the clerks were told the paper was
short and the grieving was not, and to let the line do both jobs.*

## The rising's core

- **Ede Stonhal** — of Men, of Roothollow. Cut thirty names on a stone
  and freed all thirty. Wanted to die making amends; sentenced by
  victory to live being followed. Drills recruits in the market square
  now, and climbs with them.
- **Rhovan of the Grey Rides** — elf, archer, formerly of the Wake.
  Told the truth in fractions for forty years; the sum, audited at the
  end, came out whole. Overpays a spider annually and calls it
  interest.
- **Vega Coalbrow** — giant, forge-matron. Put her mark into
  collar-steel young and spent a hundred floors buying it back; the
  receipt was a bell.
- **Nix** — goblin. Picked up a fallen standard to hide behind and was
  never afterward found hiding. Counts things; weeps in numbers; holds
  the age's one parole, and counts slow.
- **Sliver** — a shardmind. Has carried forty-one climbers and chosen
  nine; last light of its kind; a poor liar, that being a fault of
  crystal.
- **Berrin Or** — Roothollow's provisioner. Fed the rising for two
  years and had sold it for twenty. Keeps books still, by sentence of
  the thirty — the open kind only, and a wagon, and a list longer than
  his life.
- **Mistress Calyne** — spymaster of the Wake. Suspected to her face
  for three parts of a climb; right about everything, thanked for none
  of it, dead proving the case.
- **Gilder Voss** — Tollmaster-General, then quartermaster-general at a
  fixed honest fee. Never became good; became invested. Is in roads
  now; roads, he will tell you unasked, go both ways.
- **Thane Ulfar Coalbrow** — of the Forge-Court. Climbed to inherit the
  tower warm; his forty sheets of vellum were right about everything
  except the hours. Put out the world's stolen fire with his own hand.

## The Shieldwall

- **Aldis Bright** — founder of the first banner; stood in the Last
  Door an age. Asked who commands, and, told, saluted.
- **Dun Harrow** — standard-bearer, one-armed. The record shows no
  instance of his wavering. Held the north door; the lintel says so,
  without titles.
- **Marta Reed** — senior after Dun. Called the roll until all thirty
  answered, the quick and the dead; voted rope; was outvoted; enforces
  the better sentence to the letter.
- **Sefwin** — welded, iron from the jaw down, every word paid for.
  His first in twenty years was the courier's name; his next four were
  the sentence: let him feed them back.
- **Marn** — of the thirty, freed at the Welding Halls; carried the
  last banner in the world through the Last Door one-handed. The iron,
  for one hour, weighed nothing at all.
- **Tam** — of the thirty. Wept when the Champion said his name;
  raised the banner at Roothollow with both hands, having kept them.
- **Dara Shieldwall** — turned the keys of the twentieth floor's
  cages. Counts the traitor's sacks twice at the granary door, and
  says: same time next season.
- **Corran Vael** — mourned by name at the black moat. Did more work
  dead than most swords manage living.

## The keepers

- **Hobb Fennick** — the Fencerows, floor one of the world. Kept
  counts that did not match for a lifetime, climbed a hundred floors
  to watch them made to, and checked once more.
- **Bruna Hearthold** — hall-steward of the Frosthall. Hid the true
  songs eleven years and out-sang a skald in its own hall; ordered two
  lintels cut, without titles, a door being a door.
- **Mereth Wakesend** — keeper of the funeral-forms. Taught an army to
  mourn in good order, which the enemy entered as a weapon.
- **Wend Threadneedle** — thread-cutter of the fortieth floor. Nine
  years of cocoons, one strand at a time; both forearms say so.
- **Aline Bridlerest** — last ride-master of the Grey Rides. Recalled
  a hundred-year hunt to its own supper.
- **Wend Latchlight** — the Trapdoor Fields. Walked an army across a
  country that was one trap, saying, in the main, "slowly."
- **Bruna Coldquay** — the Frozen Sea. Taught the drivers Kindle and
  roaded the ice for a fleet's worth of runners.
- **Ferro of Coreside** — keeper of the scram-lore. Kept one lesson
  his whole life, said it once, to the right people, and wept without
  sound, and needed nobody.
- **Hollen** — Lastward, floor ninety-nine. Kept where a name was
  buried, under the sill; laughed last, helplessly, and first.
- **Bly, Cord, Elys, Vess, and Nib** — keepers of the throne-tiers: a
  Silence, a chisel, a garden, an armory, and eighty years of petitions,
  all walked up through the Last Door at the head of the queue.

## The Wake

Founded after the Theft by three lamplighter houses in the only grove
still lit; climbed to make the wood whole, whatever it let its
hirelings believe. Its two great agents are entered above, and both
were right.

## The Broodthrone and the Warrens

- **Matriarch Vyx** — freeholder of the fortieth floor by right of
  survival. Would not fight, that being arithmetic and not mercy;
  contracted, and kept every clause to its final letter. Regards a
  countersigned rate-board as the age's one compliment to her.
- **Wend Silkstead** — the Broodwarrens, which yielded whole to a
  goblin's word and was betrayed by doctrine. Buried none of the
  Cullers on her floor. Her one condition.

## The Cullers

- **Serjeant Hale** — taught the general to fight and a generation to
  kill the surrendered. Broken by his own doctrine at the warrens;
  carried out his own dead; asked, once, how the goblin does it. Held
  the south door.

## The Hunt

- **The Pale Huntsman** — Warden of the eightieth. Never spoke, which
  was worse. Kept eighty years of fair-hand books and was walked
  in through one bad clause.
- **Nael Hornsend** — horn-caller. Kept the calloff-lore against the
  day somebody would need a hunt unsounded, and lived to teach it.
- **The freed hounds** — took no notice of the one man they should
  have; carry a pennon sewn as a joke and borne like a relic.

## The Storm Court

- **Zephyra** — Storm Queen, Warden of the seventieth. Offered the sky
  at the price of a throne; refused, scattered a third of the host.
  Paid her treaty-debt in full, one noon late, and wheeled above the
  finished field awaiting applause.
- **Her bottled court** — kept in storm-glass as the tower's sureties;
  freed unthanked. A queen schedules her own debts.

## The tower's own

- **Vharuk** — King of the Ascent, architect of the Theft. Portioned a
  world and mistook the portioning for godhood; spent his last hour
  listening for a tower that no longer answered.
- **Malgrim** — Herald of the King, floor ninety. Freed once on the
  forty-third and starved on it; chose the collar back and kept honest
  books for a dishonest house. Heard the Break-word said over his own
  steel and was not changed, which was the whole of him.
- **The Marshal** — Malgrim's second; the collar had grown a man
  around it, his men said. Reached last of all; carried his sword
  across the yards flat on two palms.
- **Gnarl** — the Goblin King, floor ten. Crowned as a joke at his own
  people's expense, and knew it; refused the Break-collar for the
  crown and died a king of nothing.
- **Warlord Skarn** — the Red Orc, floor twenty. Priced his collar as
  a rank and defended it as one.
- **The Barrow King** — a drowned king's mound given voice, floor
  thirty. Could not be out-fought and was out-mourned; conceded the
  deeper grief and fell honestly.
- **Cindermaw** — the Wyrm, floor fifty. Older than the Theft; the
  only being in the tower that pitied Vharuk. Smelled one climber
  twice, and said so.
- **Jarl Hrimgar** — Warden of the sixtieth, welded at his own high
  seat, absent from none of the wrong feast's eleven years. Freed, he
  set the crown down like a debt.
- **Halflight** — the core's demon; burned at half a sun and could not
  die while it fed. Starved standing; remains a cinder in the shape of
  a kneeling man.
- **The Heralds** — the collared willing. Forty servers and twelve
  bows at one feast, spent to the last man and entered as worth it.
- **Dara Lastmuster** — muster-captain of the freed, floor ninety.
  Struck thousands of names from the conscript-rolls one stroke each,
  would give the work to no one, and finished it.
- **Bastionheart and Fluegrim** — a drill-master and a warren-captain
  of the Made; the tower's discipline and the tower's livery, both
  changed employers intact.
- **The Kettle** — looms. Carries two bowls. Both facts are
  load-bearing.
