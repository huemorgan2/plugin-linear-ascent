# The Hundred Banners

**Final edition** — the shipping text, cut 2026-08-04 after the
reckoning (`reviews/reckoning.md`).

## The cycle

Five editorial iterations were run against the virgin draft, each with
a two-group critical panel, a convening order, and a change ledger
(`reviews/changes-001` through `changes-005`), with a backup taken per
iteration. Iteration 1 spent its budget on continuity keystones;
iteration 2 was the structural round; mid-cycle, plan 011 re-grounded
the tower's world-construction (the Strand, the veils, the ladder — in
place of the virgin draft's grav-engine canon), and iteration 3
verified it and worked the seams; iteration 4 closed the last hard
numbers and the climax's remaining wound; iteration 5 was line-polish
only. The reckoning then audited the whole cycle against the virgin
draft, restored three lines the iterations had over-trimmed (Hobb's
tricolon in the epilogue; "the willing signed nothing" and the old
digger in the trials), ran the final mechanical sweep (the six
79-column lines in `28-the-coursing` re-flowed word-identical, plus
one 80-column straggler in `40-the-throne`; numbering, emphasis,
scene-breaks, and spacing verified clean), and cut this edition.

Printed numbering: Prologue; Chapters 1–48, strictly sequential; four
unnumbered interludes from Malgrim's ledger; Epilogue. Six appendices.
