# Construction cards — per-floor assignments (plan 011)

Key (see `world-construction.md`, §§3–5, 7, 10, 11):
- **Sky** — the sky-sentence, in miles of air: *low* ≈ 1 ("the lid presses"), *standard* ≈ 3–5 ("room for real weather"), *cathedral* ≈ 6–8 ("Vharuk liked his trophies lit and airy"). No correlation with tier — "a low-vaulted floor at 71 is scarier than any pattern."
- **Veil** — the Warden's habit: *heavy* where the gloom is policy pressed hard, *thin* where the lore already reads open and bright. "The gloom is not weather; it is policy."
- **Rimlands** — how wide the living band runs, "two miles on the cruel floors, ten on the kind ones," plus what the rim band is like there.
- **Deep Middle** — the one anchor the interior hides: "mine country, wild country, fever country … the best ore in the tower is at the bottom of it." One anchor noun per floor.
- **Gates** — where the up-gate stands: *rim* (the norm — "light, towns, trade"), *inland* (an exception the floor file already wrote), *deep-middle* (the terror floors — "Vharuk chose this for the realms he wanted no one to leave"). "(as written)" = the floor file already places the stair-lift there; existing text wins.
- **Strings** — any Sunskirt, Undershadow, bead-town, stay-road, or knot-garden the floor owns (§4d, §7). Most floors own none.
- Floors 81–100 are the Made: "perfect circles … the shape itself is the warning" — noted as *round tray* where it matters.
- Terror-gate floors and string floors carry a short justification in the cell.

*These cards are applied to the floor files by plan 011 phase 4; this table is the source of record until then.*
