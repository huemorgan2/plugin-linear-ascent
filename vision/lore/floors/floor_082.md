# Floor 82 — The Chain Yards (Made)

> *"They forge the chains here that they'll bind the next floor's slaves with. We forge our own shackles, link by link, and hate every one." — Chain-slave Wend Linkside*

**The Made:** the tower's chain-forging yards · **Gate-town:** Linkside (a captive forge-camp) · **Warden:** Anchorwrath

## The land
The chain-yards of the Made — vast forging-floors slung over glowing quench-
pits, where slave-gangs hammer and quench the tower's endless chains: the
shackles, hoist-chains, and drag-lines that bind every floor above and below.
Red chain runs on overhead drums into black quench-water that boils and
hisses; the din of hammer on link never stops; the air is iron-smoke and
steam. Nothing lives here but forge-vermin. It is a floor whose only product is
bondage, worked by the bound.

## "Flora" — the Making's growth
- **Canopy:** none — chain-drums and gantry.
- **Ground growth:** quench-scum on the black pits, forge-moss on the cold
  links, and chain-rust flowering orange on the drums.
- **How the Making twists it:** the chain-rust binds a hand to a link it
  touches; the quench-scum hides the pit-edge; the forge-moss re-heats cold
  chain to searing.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Quench-Pit Undercroft.** The cistern-cavern beneath the
  forging-floor where the quench-water is drawn. *Seed: the master drain is
  here — flood or drain the pits and the whole forge stops.*
- **The peak — the Gantry-Crane.** The great chain-hoist over the yards; the
  works read from it. *Seed: master it and every chain on the floor answers —
  including the ones on the slaves.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Chain Gate.** The stair-lift is chained
  shut behind a curtain of hanging links. *Seed: Anchorwrath binds it.*

## The people — who you are freeing
Chain-slaves — the re-enslaved of the freed floors, chained to the forges and
made to forge the very chains that bind the next captives. Their own shackles
are their finest work, and they know it. They love a chain broken, a shift
survived, and a hammer swung at an overseer instead of an anvil. They fear the
quench-pits and being welded into the drag-lines.
**The keeper:** Wend Linkside, chain-master among the slaves — keeps the master-
drain's secret and files at his own shackle in the dark.

## The six
1. **Quench-pit eel** (N) — *was:* a cistern eel. *now:* boil-scarred,
   lunging from the black quench-water. *Cured, it sinks into the cistern.*
2. **Forge bat** (N) — *was:* a yard roost-bat. *now:* smoke-mad, fouling the
   forge-lamps. *Cured, it flits into the gantry.*
3. **Slag-rat** (N) — *was:* a forge-floor rat. *now:* iron-toothed, gnawing
   the cold links. *Cured, it bolts into a slag-heap.*
4. **Chain-gang imp** (P) — *was:* a captured soul forged into a chain-hauling
   drudge, collar-bound. *now:* a chain-fisted imp that lashes with living
   links. *Pressed — Break-collar frees it; else a real death.*
5. **Quench knight** (P) — *was:* a captured soul welded into quench-hardened
   plate, collared to the yards. *now:* a steaming knight that fights from the
   pit-edges. *Pressed — the collar breaks; freed, a heavy fighter for the
   rising.*
6. **Living chain** (W) — *was:* slaves worked to death and welded into the
   drag-lines, aether pooled in the links. *now:* a serpent of animate chain
   that whips and binds the living. *Broken, it falls to dead links and the
   dead in it are freed.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Break-chain — spell.** Snaps any chain, shackle, or binding at a word —
  the slaves' most-longed-for art, and the literal freeing of the Made's
  bound. *Seed: learn it to strike the shackles off every captive above.*
- **Quench-steel chain — material.** The tower's own hardened chain, turned to
  the rising's grapples, flails, and binding-gear. *Seed: cut a fresh run.*
- **Slave's-ease — potion.** A labor-camp brew that numbs the pain and
  bone-weariness of forced work; keeps a broken body moving.

## The Warden — Anchorwrath
A living-chain construct grown vast — the drag-lines of the whole yard welded
into a thrashing anchor-serpent, hung with the bones of the slaves forged into
it. A pure construct of the Making, it fights by binding and hauling climbers
into the quench-pits. Its end is an *eviction:* Break-chain and Unrivet it, and
the links fall dead, the drowned slaves in it freed to rest. The Chain Gate's
chains fall slack.

## When it falls
Linkside drains the quench-pits and the forges die cold; the chain-slaves
strike their own shackles off with the master's file and the freed **Break-
chain**. The freed chain-slaves climb as the host's chain-breakers, carrying
quench-steel and the one spell the Made fears most — the word that unbinds —
into the King's works above.
