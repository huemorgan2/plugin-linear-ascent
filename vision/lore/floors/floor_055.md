# Floor 55 — The Troll Bridges (Giants)

> *"Snow-bridges over drops you can't see the bottom of. The trolls built them, the trolls guard them, and the trolls charge a toll no coin can pay." — Bridge-keeper Orla Bridgeward*

**Cut from:** the Giants' high cold country — a network of glacier-crossings · **Gate-town:** Bridgeward · **Warden:** Spanbreak

## The land
A maze of snow-bridges arcing over bottomless crevasses — spans of wind-packed
snow and blue ice thrown across the glacier's cracks by the mountain-trolls,
narrow and groaning and hung with icicle-fringe. Gorge-ravens haunt the spans;
the wind moans up the crevasses; a bridge that held yesterday may be gone
today. The trolls built these crossings and hold them like toll-gates, and the
fever has made them jealous. Crossing the floor means trusting a bridge of
snow over a drop with no floor, with a troll at the far end. The stair-lift
stands in an ice-cliff at the far end of a great span; Spanbreak holds the
bridge before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — snow-spans and ice-cliffs.
- **Ground & water plants:** ice-lichen fringing the spans, crevasse-moss on
  the shaded walls, and the pale snow-bloom that marks firm snow (usually).
- **Under the Yoke:** the snow-bloom now marks rotten snow as if it were firm;
  the ice-lichen weakens the spans it grows on; the crevasse-moss lures with
  warmth over the drop.

## Places of interest
- **The High Span.** The longest, highest snow-bridge, arcing over the deepest
  crevasse — the one crossing the trolls never let fall; the whole network
  reads from its crown. *Seed: securing it opens the one line across the floor
  that can't be cut.*
- **The Troll-Piling.** A great bridge-footing the mountain-trolls raised,
  hollowed into shelter out of the crevasse-wind and carved over with their
  span-marks; the keepers' span-charts hang inside. *Seed: the charts here
  show which crossings still hold.*
- **The Groaning Chasm.** The deepest crevasse of all, unbridged, where the
  wind moans up in a voice the keepers read for the weather. *Seed: its
  blue-ice walls hold a still-frozen caravan — and the span-iron cable it was
  carrying.*

## The people
Bridge-keepers — giant span-wrights and delver-crossing-crews who packed and
tended the snow-bridges, cautious folk who sound every span before they trust
it. They shelter in Bridgeward in the footing-caves, keeping the safe
crossings marked as the trolls cut the rest. They love a firm span, a sounded
bridge, and a crossing made safe. They fear the collapsing bridges and the
bridge-trolls' toll.
**The keeper:** Orla Bridgeward, bridge-keeper — keeps the span-charts and
sounds each bridge with her ice-staff, reading the snow by its crunch.

## The six
1. **Gorge raven** (N) — *was:* a crevasse raven. *now:* massing off the
   spans, mobbing crossers toward the edge. *Cured, the flock lifts into the
   wind.*
2. **Crevasse-climber** (N) — *was:* an ice-clinging lizard grown large.
   *now:* hauling up the crevasse-walls onto the spans to ambush. *Cured, it
   scuttles back down into the blue dark.*
3. **Toll-runner wolf** (N) — *was:* a glacier wolf. *now:* pack-coursing the
   bridges, herding crossers onto rotten snow. *Cured, it lopes off across a
   firm span.*
4. **Snow-hare** (N) — *was:* a glacier hare. *now:* bolting underfoot on the
   narrow spans to trip crossers. *Cured, it's a white blur and gone.*
5. **Bridge-troll** (N) — *was:* a mountain-troll — troll-kind is Deep-born,
   but these were drawn up out of the delvings long ago to throw the
   snow-bridges over crevasses no giant could span, and stayed as the
   crossings' builders. *now:* fever-jealous, holding the spans they made and
   hurling ice-blocks at any who cross unpaid. *Cured, the slow giant goes back
   to mending bridges instead of breaking crossers.*
6. **Span-wight** (W) — *was:* a keeper who fell when a bridge gave, aether in
   the failed span. *now:* a rime-shrouded figure that walks the bridges
   collapsing snow under the living. *Broken, it falls into the crevasse and
   drains away.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Spanhold — spell.** Freezes a snow-bridge firm or throws a span of ice
  across a gap for a spell; the keepers' art, charted in the Troll-Piling.
  *Seed: learn it to cross the bridges and bridge any gap the sky-tiers
  open.*
- **Span-iron — metal.** The cold-forged bridge-cable iron of the crossings,
  drawn into span-cable and grapples for the whole climb. *Seed: the frozen
  caravan in the Groaning Chasm still carries a cable-hoard of it.*
- **Orla's span-charts — knowledge.** The bridge-keeper's map of every crossing
  and which snow will hold; she sounds each span by its crunch. *Seed: win her
  trust and no rotten bridge takes you.*
- **Surefoot draught — potion.** A crosser's brew that grips any surface; a
  climber cannot slip on ice, snow, or narrow stone for a spell.

## The Warden — Spanbreak
A bridge-troll grown vast and jealous, welded with ice-block fists and a hide
of packed snow — it holds the High Span and fights by collapsing the bridges
under climbers and hurling ice, dropping the fight toward the crevasse. Break
it and the trolls mend rather than break; the spans hold; the stair-lift opens
at the far end.

## When it falls
Bridgeward secures the High Span and the trolls set to mending the crossings
they once cut; the network holds firm. The bridge-keepers climb as the host's
span-wrights and crossing-crews, carrying **Spanhold** and span-iron — the
folk who throw a bridge across any crevasse the tower cracks open toward the
throne.
