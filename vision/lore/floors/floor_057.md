# Floor 57 — The Shade Walks (Elves)

> *"Keep a lamp between you and your own shadow. On the Walks, a shadow that gets behind you doesn't come back to your feet." — Lamp-keeper Illa Candlerow*

**Cut from:** the Elves' lamp-woods — the lantern-boulevards of a wood-city · **Gate-town:** Candlerow · **Warden:** Palewick
**Sky:** standard — three miles of air the city has never seen.
**Veil:** heavy — dark laid on dusk, lamp by murdered lamp.
**Land:** Rimlands six miles wide — the still-lit boulevards; the deep middle
holds the dead palace-lamps.
**Gates:** the down-gate at the rim among the lit avenues; the up-gate in
the deep middle, where the last lamp died.

## The land
Grand lamplit avenues of an elf wood-city — boulevards of pale stone lined
with lantern-trees and everlasting candle-lamps, elegant and endless. But the
lamps have failed one by one, and where the light dies the *shadows won't hold
to their casters:* they peel loose and wander, hungry for the warmth and the
light they've lost. In the lit stretches all is graceful and calm; step into a
dark one and your own shadow may leave you. It is a floor of light and dark in
strict avenues, safe in the glow and lethal in the gaps. The city never
knew a sky: a wider country roofs it over, and the elves answered that long
Undershadow dusk with lamps — a realm that made its own daylight, which is
exactly what the Warden came to murder. The living boulevards keep to the
rim, where the slot-light helps the lamps; the avenues run down and inward
through longer and longer dark to the dead palace-lamps at the floor's
heart — and the stair-lift stands there, in the deep middle, at the inmost
avenue's end where the last lamp died. The only way up is a walk into the
oldest dark on the floor, relighting as you go, to an arch that is a dead
lamp-arch; Palewick pools the black before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** lantern-oak and candle-birch lining the walks, their
  lamps guttering.
- **Ground & water plants:** lamp-lily in the avenue-pools, glow-ivy on the
  lit walls, and the pale gloom-moss that spreads where lamps die.
- **Under the Yoke:** the gloom-moss — the Warden's own weed — snuffs the
  lamps it reaches; the glow-ivy hoards its light and starves the walk; the
  lamp-lilies drink the glow and darken.

## Places of interest
- **The Great Lamp.** The central lamp-tower that once lit the whole
  wood-city; from its gallery the lit and dark avenues read like a map.
  *Seed: relit, it fixes every shadow back to its caster at once.*
- **The Wick-Vault.** The candle-cellar beneath the walks where the city's
  everlasting wicks and lamp-oil are stored — and the master wick, the light
  that anchors every shadow on the floor, gutters low. *Seed: keep the master
  wick alight and the wandering shadows cannot peel free.*
- **The Drowned Lantern-Court.** A grand plaza whose fountain-pools swallowed
  their own lamps when the dark came, now a black mirror of quenched lights
  where the peeled shadows gather thickest. *Seed: rekindle its ring of lamps
  and reclaim the city's heart from the shade.*

## The people
Lamp-keepers — elves whose calling is the light: wick-trimmers, oil-wrights,
and lantern-wardens who kept the wood-city aglow. Now they fight a losing war
with the dark, and shelter in Candlerow's few still-lit squares, guarding the
last flames. They love a trimmed wick, a lit avenue, and a shadow that stays
at its feet. They fear the wandering shadows and the shade that steals the
light itself.
**The keeper:** Illa Candlerow, lamp-keeper — keeps the master wick and walks
the lit lines relighting what she can, a lantern always in hand.

## The six
1. **Avenue hound** (N) — *was:* a city watch-hound. *now:* prowling the dark
   stretches, hunting the warmth of the living. *Cured, it pads into a lit
   square and lies down.*
2. **Avenue rat** (N) — *was:* a lamp-oil rat. *now:* gnawing the wicks and
   spreading the dark, massing in the gaps. *Cured, it bolts for a shadow-
   crack.*
3. **Lamp-moth** (N) — *was:* a lantern-moth. *now:* huge and greedy,
   smothering the flames it swarms. *Cured, it drifts to a steady lamp.*
4. **Shade-cat** (N) — *was:* a boulevard cat. *now:* half-shadow itself,
   melting between the dark stretches to pounce. *Cured, it steps into the
   light, solid, and stretches.*
5. **Unfixed shadow** (W) — *was:* nothing — a shadow peeled loose from a dead
   lamp's last caster, aether-quickened. *now:* a flat dark shape that flows
   the walls and tries to take a living body's shadow — and its warmth.
   *Broken (or lit), it thins to nothing and drains into the dark.*
6. **Candle-thief shade** (W) — *was:* a lamp-keeper who died in the dark,
   aether in his snuffed lantern. *now:* a grey figure that snuffs lamps as
   it passes to spread the dark ahead of the living. *Broken, its lantern
   gutters out and it drains away.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Lightbind — spell.** Fixes a wandering shadow back to its caster, kindles a
  dead lamp, and banishes shadow-things in a burst of light; the keepers' art.
  *Seed: learn it to relight the Walks and, above, to break the tower's
  darkness.*
- **Candle-silver — material.** The light-holding silver of the everlasting
  lamps, worked into gear that sheds its own glow and wards shadow. *Seed: the
  Wick-Vault's stores.*
- **The master wick — relic.** The everlasting wick that anchors every shadow
  on the floor, kept guttering in the Wick-Vault. *Seed: carry it lit and no
  shadow — yours or another's — can wander from its feet.*
- **Brightdraught — potion.** A keeper's brew that lets the eye see true in any
  dark and wards the warmth-draining shade for a spell.

## The Warden — Palewick
An unfixed shadow grown vast and given form, welded to the city's greatest
snuffed lamp — a shape of living dark that snuffs the lamps around it and sets
the loose shadows on the living, fighting from the pooling black. It is a
darkness the tower quickened, so its end is an *eviction:* flood it with light
(relight the Great Lamp) and the borrowed aether is driven out, the shadows
fix back to their casters, and the stair-lift opens in the glow.

## When it falls
Candlerow relights the Great Lamp and every shadow on the Walks snaps back to
its owner; the avenues glow whole again. The Warden's laid darkness unravels
in a day; the overhang keeps the city's old dusk, but it is the honest dusk
the lamps were made for, and the gloom-moss withers avenue by avenue as the
light walks inland. The lamp-keepers climb as the host's
light-bearers and shadow-banishers, carrying **Lightbind** and candle-silver —
the folk who bring light into the tower's darkest floors and keep the army's
own shadows from turning on it.
