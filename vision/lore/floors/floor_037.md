# Floor 37 — The Broken Doors (Giants)

> *"Every blast-door in the mountain was made to hold an age. The tower breached them all in a season. That should frighten you more than the crows." — Doorward Bruna Lastlantern*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — its great blast-door bulkheads · **Gate-town:** Last Lantern · **Warden:** Gatebone

## The land
A tier of colossal breached blast-doors — the giants' bulkhead-gates that once
sealed the deep-forge against flood and fire, now blown off their hinges and
lying in rubble, the corridors between them choked with debris and loud with
nesting crows. Scorch-marks streak the walls where the tower's charges cut
through. Draughts howl through the breaches. It was the mountain's armor, and
its breaking is written plain in every twisted hinge — proof that nothing the
old world built kept the tower out.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — steel bulkheads and blast-rubble.
- **Ground & water plants:** breach-moss on the scorched steel, crow-nettle in
  the rubble, and the scorch-lichen ringing the blast-marks.
- **Under the Yoke:** the crow-nettle grows razor-leaved in the debris; the
  scorch-lichen re-ignites old blast-residue at a spark.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Sally-Port.** A giant's side-door undercroft that survived
  the breaching, its mechanism intact. *Seed: it holds the door-charts — the
  key to every bulkhead in the tower, breached or whole.*
- **The peak — the Gatehouse.** The high control-gallery over the greatest
  door, its levers dead. *Seed: restore it and every surviving bulkhead on
  the floor answers — a fortress the rising could hold.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Last Door.** The stair-lift is the one
  unbreached bulkhead, still sealed. *Seed: only Gatebone's fall unbars it.*

## The people
Doorwards — giant gate-wrights and delver-mechanists who built and manned the
bulkheads, keepers of the mountain's locks, shamed that their doors failed.
They shelter in Last Lantern behind the sally-port, holding the one door left.
They love a door that holds, a true-set bar, and a bulkhead sealed against the
dark. They fear the door-breaker orcs and the doorward-remnant that haunts the
breaches.
**The keeper:** Bruna Lastlantern, chief doorward — keeps the door-charts and
the last working bulkhead, and means to make it hold this time.

## The six
1. **Breach crow** (N) — *was:* a bulkhead-roosting crow. *now:* massing in
   the breaches, mobbing anything through a doorway. *Cured, the flock lifts
   into the girders.*
2. **Scorch rat** (N) — *was:* a corridor rat. *now:* burn-scarred and bold,
   nesting in the warm blast-rubble. *Cured, it bolts through a breach.*
3. **Rubble-hound** (N) — *was:* a gate-guard dog. *now:* debris-caked,
   ambushing from the blast-piles. *Cured, it shakes off dust and pads away.*
4. **Door-breaker orc** (P) — *was:* a pressed sapper from Skarn's camp set to
   blow the bulkheads. *now:* collared, charge-laden, breaching walls to reach
   the living. *Pressed — a real death; the deserters know how to seal doors
   as well as break them.*
5. **Powder kobold** (P) — *was:* a first-taken conscript set to carry blasting-
   charges. *now:* collared, flinging mining-charges by the handful. *A real
   death; freeing the collar is the mercy — and stops the blast.*
6. **Doorward remnant** (W) — *was:* a doorward killed holding a door that
   failed, aether in the broken hinge. *now:* a hinge-and-plate figure that
   walks the corridors slamming breached doors on the living. *Broken, the
   plate falls and it drains into the steel.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Barway — spell.** Bars any door shut or breaks any bar open at a word; the
  doorwards' craft, etched on the sally-port mechanism. *Seed: learn it to
  reach the Gatehouse and, above, to open the tower's sealed ways.*
- **Blast-plate — metal.** The layered door-steel of the bulkheads, forged
  into the heaviest shields and armor in the host. *Seed: strip a blown door.*
- **Steadyhand tonic — potion.** A sapper's brew against blast-shock and
  deafness; steadies the hands and clears the head after a concussion.

## The Warden — Gatebone
A doorward remnant welded into a walking bulkhead — a slab of blast-plate on
hinge-legs that slams shut on climbers and blows its own breaches to flank —
it fights in the choke-points between doors, where there's nowhere to dodge a
closing slab. Break it and the doors fall still; the Gatehouse can be
restored; the Last Door unbars on the floor above.

## When it falls
Last Lantern restores the Gatehouse and seals the surviving bulkheads — a
fortress-floor the rising can hold at its back. The doorwards climb as the
host's breachers and gate-wrights, carrying **Barway** and blast-plate — the
folk who open the tower's locked ways and shut its doors behind the army.
