# Floor 17 — The Glass Flats (Waste)

> *"Everything you see out there is a lie the heat tells. Everything you don't see is the ogres." — Sira Shardside*

**Cut from:** the burned borderlands — the Waste · **Gate-town:** Shardside · **Warden:** Glassjaw

## The land
A plain of fulgurite — desert fused to glass by ancient lightning — stretching
flat and mirror-bright to a horizon that shimmers with mirage. The heat lies
in sheets; false lakes and false towers hang in the air; every distance lies.
Underfoot the glass rings and cracks, and the cracks are edged like knives.
It was borderland no crown claimed, and it kept its secrets by making anyone
who crossed it doubt their own eyes. The mirages are worse now. The stair-lift
is set in a fulgurite bluff so bright it cannot be looked at directly; Glassjaw
hides among mirror-images of itself before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — glass, sand, and heat-shimmer.
- **Ground & water plants:** glasswort in the cracks, the deep-rooted
  mirage-lily (that blooms only in a real oasis), and salt-crust moss.
- **Under the Yoke:** the mirages have thickened into near-solid illusions,
  and the glasswort's edges have turned to razor-glass that reaches for
  shade-casting warmth.

## Places of interest
- **The Shard Hollow.** The gleaners' shelter — a glass-walled dry-hollow the
  mirages can't reach, cool and shaded under the flats. *Seed: its back wall is
  a natural lens, and it focuses on a buried true oasis somewhere out on the
  glare.*
- **The Mirror Tor.** A monolith of black glass that throws back the whole
  flat like a standing mirror; before it, real and mirage can finally be told
  apart. *Seed: a signal struck against its face cuts clean through every false
  image on the plain.*
- **The Fused Caravan.** A merchant train caught on the flats when the
  lightning-glass formed, drivers and wagons and beasts all fused mid-flight
  into black glass. *Seed: its strongbox and sealed water-casks survived under
  the glass — and its route-map still marks the true fords across the flats.*

## The people
Glass-gleaners — borderland folk who harvest fulgurite and read the true
path across the mirage by lore passed mouth to mouth. They live in the Shard
Hollow's shade, rationing water and truth. They love a real oasis, a clean-
struck blade of glass, and a horizon that means what it shows. They fear the
mirages that walk now, and the shard-picker ogres.
**The keeper:** Sira Shardside, gleaner-elder — walks the flats blindfolded
by choice, trusting her feet over her lying eyes.

## The six
1. **Glass salamander** (N) — *was:* a heat-basking salamander. *now:* skin
   fused to glass scale, mirror-bright, striking from a reflection. *Cured,
   it dulls and slips into a crack.*
2. **Flat-skitter lizard** (N) — *was:* a sand-runner. *now:* skating the
   glass at blurring speed, hard to track against the glare. *Cured, it
   freezes, then is gone.*
3. **Mirage-hare** (N) — *was:* a desert hare. *now:* it casts false doubles
   as it runs, three hares where there's one. *Cured, the doubles vanish and
   the real one bolts.*
4. **Glass-vulture** (N) — *was:* a carrion bird of the flats. *now:* glass-
   feathered, stooping out of the mirror-glare unseen. *Cured, it wheels off
   into the shimmer.*
5. **Shard-picker ogre** (P) — *a border-ogre, press-ganged.* Ogres roamed the
   Waste's edges before the Theft; this one was collared when the flats were
   taken and kept out here to cut fulgurite for the lenses the tower grinds
   above. *was:* a Waste-born conscript set to harvest glass. *now:* collared
   and glass-scarred, hurling razor-shards by the armful for a wage of nothing.
   *Pressed — a real death; the few who throw the collar down know the true
   paths across the flats.*
6. **Heat-ghost** (W) — *was:* a gleaner who died of thirst chasing a mirage,
   aether caught in the shimmer. *now:* a walking mirage that shows a climber
   water and leads them onto the razor-glass. *Broken, the image collapses
   and the shimmer clears.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Truesight — spell.** Strips illusion and mirage from a stretch of ground,
  the gleaners' survival-charm · learned from Sira out on the flats · *Seed:
  cross the flats now, and see through the tower's mirror-tricks later.*
- **Fulgurite lens — material.** A struck glass lens that focuses light to a
  cutting point, the seed of aether-optics and beam-work · cut from the Shard
  Hollow's wall · *Seed: forge the rising's first light-weapons.*
- **The caravan's route-map — knowledge.** The fused merchants' chart of true
  fords and real water across the lying flats · sealed under the glass of the
  Fused Caravan · *Seed: walk the flats without trusting your own eyes.*

## The Warden — Glassjaw
A glass salamander of the flats, welded huge and mirror-scaled, wreathed in
its own mirages so it fights from a dozen false positions at once. Its bite
is edged glass. Break the real one (not its reflections) and the mirages
shatter; the flats stop lying; the stair-lift dims enough to enter.

## When it falls
Shardside reaches the buried oasis and the flats bloom with mirage-lily that,
for once, marks real water. The gleaners climb as the host's illusion-
breakers and lens-smiths, carrying **Truesight** into the mirror galleries
and false-faced floors near the throne.
