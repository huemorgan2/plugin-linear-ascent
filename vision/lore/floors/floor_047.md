# Floor 47 — The Doorstone Moor (Men)

> *"The old folk raised the standing stones to keep something shut. The fever cracked the seal. Now the moor has a door open, and it doesn't lead anywhere good." — Stone-warden Bran Stonewake*

**Cut from:** the old megalith-country of Men — the dolmen moor · **Gate-town:** Stonewake · **Warden:** Doorstone

## The land
A high heather moor studded with standing stones — menhirs, stone rows, and
great dolmens raised by Men older than the realms, ward-stones set to seal
what the ancestors feared. Under the Yoke one dolmen has cracked open: a
portal-tomb the old folk sealed for good reason, now gaping, breathing a cold
wrong wind across the heather. The moor is wide and wind-scoured and silent
but for curlew-cry, and every stone hums faintly with old warding — except the
one that no longer holds.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — heather, gorse, and grey stone.
- **Ground & water plants:** ling-heather, bog-cotton in the flushes, and the
  ward-lichen that only grows on true sealing-stones.
- **Under the Yoke:** the heather round the open dolmen has blackened; the
  ward-lichen is peeling from the cracked stone; the gorse points its thorns
  toward the breach.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Portal Dolmen.** The opened tomb-chamber itself, the
  breach in the moor's old seal. *Seed: the sealing-rite that shut it once is
  carved inside — and can be carved again, if the Warden lets you reach it.*
- **The peak — the Ring.** The great stone circle on the moor's crown, the
  master ward that anchors all the lesser stones. *Seed: re-hallow the Ring
  and every ward-stone on the moor answers, sealing the breach.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Doorstone Gate.** The stair-lift is set
  in a lintel of three standing stones. *Seed: Doorstone bars it as it bars
  all the moor's true doors.*

## The people
Moor-folk — Men of the megalith-country: stone-wardens and ward-keepers whose
inherited charge is to keep the sealing-stones sound and never open what's
shut. They shelter in Stonewake within a lesser stone-ring, appalled that a
dolmen has failed on their watch. They love a sound ward, a kept seal, and an
old door left shut. They fear the open dolmen and whatever the wind off it is
waking.
**The keeper:** Bran Stonewake, stone-warden — keeps the sealing-rite and
tends the Ring, and means to shut the breach his forebears trusted him to
guard.

## The six
1. **Moor hound** (N) — *was:* a moor-herding dog. *now:* heather-lean,
   coursing the flushes in packs, spooked toward the breach. *Cured, it heels
   to a whistle.*
2. **Moor-hare** (N) — *was:* a heather hare. *now:* moon-mad and bolting,
   drawing coursers across the bogs. *Cured, it stills, then lopes off.*
3. **Curlew** (N) — *was:* the moor's wading bird. *now:* massed and crying a
   wail that unnerves and misleads. *Cured, the flock rises calling true.*
4. **Moor adder** (N) — *was:* a basking adder. *now:* fever-bold in the ling,
   striking at a striding boot. *Cured, it pours into the heather.*
5. **Something unsealed** (W) — *was:* the old wrong thing the ancestors shut
   in the dolmen — never a beast, only a hunger — now aether-fed and loose.
   *now:* a cold half-seen shape that drifts from the breach, aging and
   sapping what it touches. *Broken (and the seal re-carved), it is forced
   back and shut in for good.*
6. **Moor ghoul** (W) — *was:* a ward-keeper who died shutting a stone, aether
   in the heather. *now:* a peat-black figure that drags the living toward the
   open dolmen. *Broken, it sinks into the flush and drains down.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Wardstone — spell.** Seals a portal, door, or breach with old ward-lore,
  and binds what tries to pass; carved in the Portal Dolmen. *Seed: learn it
  to re-seal the breach and, above, to shut the tower's summoning-ways.*
- **Doorstone granite — material.** Ward-carved menhir-stone that will not
  break and turns aether aside; the finest shield-core and seal-stone in the
  tower. *Seed: quarry a fallen menhir.*
- **Heather-mead — potion.** A moor-brew of ling-honey; steels the heart and
  clears the head against dread and old fear.

## The Warden — Doorstone
The moor's ancient stone sentinel, roused wrong — a dolmen-golem of standing-
stone that the ancestors set to *guard* the seal, now fever-turned to hold
every door on the moor *open* instead of shut. It fights before the Portal
Dolmen, raising ward-stones as walls and weapons. It is a construct woken to
the wrong purpose, so its end is an *eviction:* break its fever-hold and the
old guardian-will returns — it seals the breach itself and stands down. The
Doorstone Gate unbars.

## When it falls
Stonewake re-hallows the Ring, the breach seals, and the wrong thing is shut
in for good behind sound stone. The moor-folk climb as the host's warders and
seal-carvers, carrying **Wardstone** and doorstone-granite — the folk who shut
the doors the tower opens and keep the throne from summoning worse.
