# Floor 28 — The Smelterworks (Giants)

> *"We pour the mountain's blood. It used to make tools. Now it just makes more of the tower." — Crucible-master Bruna Tapfire*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — its smelting-floor · **Gate-town:** Crucible Gate · **Warden:** Smeltjaw

## The land
The smelting-floor: banks of furnaces still roaring on captured aether,
runnels of molten metal threading the stone in glowing rivers, and the great
crucibles tipping their fire into moulds that cast nothing anymore — or cast
tower-parts on their own. The air shimmers with heat; the light is furnace-
orange; the runnels must be crossed on narrow tap-bridges. It is the loudest,
hottest, most dangerous floor of the mine-tier, and the metal never stops
pouring. The stair-lift stands behind a wall of moulded fire at the far
end of the pour, its arch cast as a crucible's lip; Smeltjaw pours before
it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — furnace-banks and glowing channels.
- **Ground & water plants:** slag-moss on the cooler stone, fire-lichen at
  the runnel-edges, and the crucible-fungus fruiting on flux-heaps.
- **Under the Yoke:** the fire-lichen flares to flame at a breath of warmth;
  the crucible-fungus spits sparks; the runnels overflow their channels to
  chase heat.

## Places of interest
- **The Tap-Hole Undercroft.** The channel-cave beneath the furnaces where
  the molten runnels are tapped and gathered. *Seed: the master tap-plug is
  down here — pull it and every furnace on the floor drains cold.*
- **The Tap-Bridges.** The web of narrow stone spans crossing the glowing
  runnels, the only footing above the metal rivers. *Seed: hold or drop a
  span and you choose where the fight over the fire is fought.*
- **The Mould-Yard.** Rows on rows of casting-moulds the crucibles now fill
  on their own, turning out tower-parts with no hand to shape them. *Seed:
  slip a saved mould among them and the runaway pour casts a clean weapon
  instead of a chain.*

## The people
Smelters — giant crucible-masters and delver-tappers who turn ore to metal,
scorched folk who read a melt by its color and never turn their backs on a
runnel. They shelter in Crucible Gate's cooler galleries, pouring metal that
feeds the tower they hate. They love a clean pour, a true cast, and a
furnace banked safe at shift's end. They fear the overflowing runnels and the
heat-haunts that walk the fire.
**The keeper:** Bruna Tapfire, crucible-master — knows every furnace's temper
and holds the master tap-plug against the day it's worth pulling.

## The six
1. **Slag rat** (N) — *was:* a flux-heap rat. *now:* heat-hardened, nesting
   at the runnel-edges. *Cured, it bolts into a cool crack.*
2. **Runnel salamander** (N) — *fire-bred, and not first from here.*
   Salamanders are Waste creatures, hatched in burning ground; these crept
   up the tapped runnels into the giants' heat and bred in the metal rivers
   until the smelters counted them native. *was:* a molten-channel
   salamander. *now:* swimming the rivers, lashing molten drops. *Cured, it
   sinks into a cooling runnel.*
3. **Crucible-moth** (N) — *was:* a furnace-drawn moth. *now:* fireproof and
   swarming, diving into eyes at the tap-bridges. *Cured, it drifts off,
   singed but whole.*
4. **Smelter-boss orc** (P) — *warcamp stock, not smelter-born.* Orcs
   belong to Skarn's Waste warbands; this foreman was marched up as pressed
   garrison labor to drive the giants' captured smelter, set over the
   tappers with a lash he never asked to hold. *was:* a pressed foreman set
   over the tappers. *now:* collared, driving the melt. *A real death; the
   ones who drop the lash know the furnace-controls.*
5. **Ladle kobold** (P) — *dragged up from the low warrens.* Kobolds are
   first-taken conscripts like the goblins far below, marched all the way up
   here to fetch and carry molten ladles — small enough to send between the
   furnaces and, to the tower, worth nothing to spend. *was:* a conscript
   set to carry ladles. *now:* collared, burn-scarred, flinging molten shot.
   *A real death; freeing the collar is the only kindness left.*
6. **Heat-haunt** (W) — *was:* a tapper who fell into a runnel, aether caught
   in the metal. *now:* a walking figure of living molten metal that flows
   across the tap-bridges. *Broken, it hardens to a cooling statue and drains
   down.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Pour — spell.** Commands a body of molten metal to flow, hold, or part;
  the tappers' art · scored on the master tap-plug · *learn it to cross the
  runnels and bank the furnaces.*
- **Crucible steel — metal.** The purest melt the smelters can pour, cast
  into master-blades and armor-cores · caught from a clean pour in a saved
  mould · *set a mould in the Mould-Yard and take the cast.*
- **The master tap-plug — tool.** The single great plug that drains every
  furnace at once, held back for the day it's worth pulling · in the Tap-
  Hole Undercroft, in Bruna's keeping · *pull it and the endless pour stops
  cold.*
- **Furnace-draught — brew.** A smelter's tonic against the heat; wards the
  body from fire and exhaustion by the runnels for a spell.

## The Warden — Smeltjaw
A runnel salamander grown vast, welded into a mould-plated hulk that carries
its own furnace in its chest — it fights on the tap-bridges, flooding the
runnels to sweep footing and spitting molten metal. Break it and the furnaces
can be banked; the runnels cool to hard channels; the stair-lift's fire-
wall sets solid enough to pass.

## When it falls
Bruna pulls the master tap and the endless pour finally stops; the smelters
cast for the rising instead of the tower. They climb as the host's foundry-
masters, carrying **Pour** and crucible steel — the folk who turn the freed
mountain's ore into the weapons that will breach the throne.
