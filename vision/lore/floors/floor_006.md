# Floor 6 — The Threshold Dark (Deep)

> *"This is where the light ends. Everything past here, you carry yourself." — Sable Lastlamp*

**Cut from:** the shared under-caverns — the Deep belongs to no one people · **Gate-town:** Lastlight · **Warden:** Duskspin

## The land
The first true dark of the Ascent: a cave-mouth cavern where the tower's
floodlights fail entirely, the floor thick with the guano of a thousand
years of roosts. Here the stolen surface finally gives out and the
under-world begins — a vault of dripping stone, silk in the high corners,
and a cold that has never known a season. Bring your own light or bring
nothing. Lastlight is the last lamp before the deep, and its people named it
as a warning.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none. Stone teeth above, stone teeth below.
- **Ground & water plants:** cave-lichen, threshold moss at the light's dead
  edge, and pale root-hair where surface trees once reached down.
- **Under the Yoke:** the threshold moss has turned lightless-hungry, and
  the silk in the corners grows faster than any spider could spin it.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Guano Vault.** The main cavern, floor soft and deep with
  droppings, roof alive with roosts. *Seed: under the guano lies a pre-Theft
  delver-cache, sealed when the light went.*
- **The peak — the Chimney.** A natural shaft climbing toward a last grey
  smear of borrowed daylight — the highest a climber can stand here. *Seed:
  from its top the whole vault can be lit at once, if there were a flame big
  enough.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Dark Gate.** The stair-lift is cut into
  the deepest wall, its arch webbed shut. *Seed: Duskspin's silk seals it.*

## The people
Delvers — of every race, born to the deep, who read stone by echo and never
feared the dark until the dark changed. They live by shielded lamp and
knotted rope, mapping the vault by touch, and they love a true echo, a dry
ledge, and a lamp that will not blow. Now they huddle at Lastlight's edge,
because past it the silk is spreading.
**The keeper:** Sable Lastlamp, echo-reader — knows the vault blind, and
will not step past the last lit stone until it is safe.

## The six
1. **Grave-moth** (N) — *was:* a pallid cave-moth. *now:* palm-broad, drawn
   to breath, it powders the eyes with scale-dust. *Cured, it drifts back
   into the dark.*
2. **Threshold beetle** (N) — *was:* a blind carrion beetle of the guano.
   *now:* armored and swarming, tide-deep across the floor. *Cured, the tide
   parts and pours into the cracks.*
3. **Cave broodling** (N) — *was:* a spiderling of the corner-silk. *now:*
   dog-sized and hunting in threads, the first of many broods above. *Cured,
   it scuttles up its own line and is gone.*
4. **Guano vole** (N) — *was:* a blind vole of the roost-floor. *now:*
   bloated, bold, gnawing at boots and rope. *Cured, it burrows into the
   soft floor.*
5. **Sentinel spider** (N) — *was:* the vault's big ambush-weaver. *now:*
   fever-quick, dropping on a shielded lamp from the roof. *Cured, it hauls
   itself back to a high corner.*
6. **Silk-wrapped husk** (W) — *was:* a delver lost to the silk, aether
   pooled in the bundle. *now:* a walking cocoon that mimics a caught man's
   voice to draw lamps close. *Broken, the silk falls empty and drains
   downward.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Echo-sight — spell.** Reads the shape of a dark room from a single
  clap; the delvers' oldest art, half-forgotten. *Seed: learn it from Sable
  to map the silk-choked floors above.*
- **Threshold spar — ore.** A dark crystal that holds a charge of light,
  mined only at the edge of the true dark. *Seed: it could feed the Chimney
  beacon.*
- **Deep-draught — potion.** A delver's tonic against the cold and the fear
  of the lightless — steadies a climber in any dark.

## The Warden — Duskspin
A sentinel spider of the vault, welded huge with spinnerets of aether-wire
that lay cable, not thread — it walls the Dark Gate behind steel silk and
drops from the unseen roof. It fights by the dark it owns, cutting lamps
first. Break it and the wire-silk parts, the Dark Gate clears, and the
Chimney can be lit to give the vault its first light in an age.

## When it falls
Lastlight's lamps burn steady past their old edge; the delvers map the
freed dark and open the sealed caches. They climb with rope, shielded
lamps, and echo-sight — the guides the whole host will follow through every
Deep floor above, where no surface-folk can find the way.
