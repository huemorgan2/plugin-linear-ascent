# Floor 60 — The Frosthall (Giants) · Warden

> *"He was the best of us — the jarl who'd give his own seat to a cold stranger. The tower froze that open hand into a fist and set him feasting on his own folk." — Hall-steward Bruna Hearthold*

**Cut from:** the Giants' high country — the great mead-hall of a jarldom · **Gate-town:** Hearthold · **Warden:** **Jarl Hrimgar**

## The land
A colossal timber mead-hall under a snowbound sky — the seat of a giant
jarldom, all carved rafters, roaring hearth-fires, long tables, and the din of
an endless feast that no longer feeds anyone. Warm firelight spills from the
doors into the snow; a skald's song never stops; horns are forever raised. But
the warmth is a lie the fever tells: the feast is a war-feast now, the jarl's
open hand frozen to a fist, and his hall-thanes drink and fight and die and
rise to drink again. It is the last floor of the sixth tier, and the first
*hall* the climb must take — warm, welcoming, and deadly.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** the hall's own carved roof-trees, and the frost-birch
  of the yard.
- **Ground & water plants:** hearth-moss on the warm stones, mead-mould in
  the spill, and the frost-holly hung for the feast.
- **Under the Yoke:** the frost-holly's berries burst to ice-shot; the mead-
  mould's fumes deepen a reckless feast-fury; the hearth-moss burns hot
  enough to spread the hearth-fires.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Undercroft Cellar.** The mead-vaults beneath the hall,
  racked with the jarldom's ale and its oldest cask. *Seed: the Mead of
  Memory — the draught that could wake the jarl to who he was — is cellared
  here.*
- **The peak — the High Seat.** The jarl's throne-dais under the smoke-hole,
  the hall's high place. *Seed: the fight ends here; from the roof-hole the
  whole tier reads, freed.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Frosthall Gate.** The stair-lift is set
  behind the jarl's own seat, hung with frozen war-shields. *Seed: only
  Hrimgar's freeing opens it — the gate to the seventh tier.*

## The people
Hall-folk of Hearthold — the jarldom's giant household: stewards, cup-bearers,
skalds, and thanes' families, warm generous folk whose whole life was the
open hall. Now they serve a war-feast at spear-point, drinking to a fury they
hate. They love a shared horn, a true song, a guest made welcome, and a jarl
who remembers mercy. They fear the endless feast and the jarl their good lord
has become.
**The keeper:** Bruna Hearthold, hall-steward — keeps the Mead of Memory
hidden and the true songs alive, waiting for a hand strong enough to break the
feast.

## The six
1. **Hearth-wolf** (N) — *was:* the hall's fireside hounds. *now:* fire-
   maddened, coursing the tables and the yard. *Cured, it flops down by the
   hearth and sleeps.*
2. **Hall-raven** (N) — *was:* the jarl's thought-ravens. *now:* a black storm
   in the rafters, crying the war-feast. *Cured, they settle quiet on the
   roof-trees.*
3. **Hall-boar** (N) — *was:* the feast-boar on the spit. *now:* risen off the
   spit half-carved and furious, charging the benches. *Cured, it drops and
   is only meat again.*
4. **Mead-bearer troll** (N) — *was:* a cellar-troll set to haul the casks.
   *now:* fever-drunk, hurling barrels down the hall. *Cured, the slow giant
   sets down its cask and lumbers to the cellar.*
5. **Hall-thane** (P) — *was:* one of the jarl's sworn warriors, first-taken
   and collared to the feast. *now:* drinking, fighting, and rising again,
   bound to serve the war-feast forever. *Pressed — a real death unless the
   collar is broken; the freed thanes are the hall's fiercest for the
   rising.*
6. **Ice skald** (W) — *was:* the hall's greatest singer, dead at his song,
   aether in the frozen harp. *now:* a rime-shrouded bard whose war-song
   drives the feast-fury and compels the living to fight. *Broken (or sung
   down), the song ends and it drains into the hearth.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Hearthfire — spell.** A rallying warmth that heals and heartens every
  ally in earshot and turns fear to courage; the true hall's song, not the
  war-song. *Seed: learn it from Bruna to counter the ice skald and hearten
  the host below the throne.*
- **Hall-gold — material.** The jarldom's feast-gold and shield-silver,
  aether-warm from a thousand years of song; the richest morale-metal in the
  tower. *Seed: the High Seat's hoard.*
- **Mead of Memory — potion.** The oldest cask; a draught that restores what
  the fever stole — memory, self, and heart. *Seed: it is what wakes Jarl
  Hrimgar; drunk by a climber, it heals mind as well as body.*

## The Warden — Jarl Hrimgar
A giant jarl — himself first-taken, and the hardest-collared soul on the tier
— welded into rime-frost war-plate and set at the head of his own hall's
endless war-feast. Like Warlord Skarn far below, he is a good lord made into a
warden and turned against his own folk; and like Skarn, his ending is a
*freeing,* not a slaughter. He fights with hearth-fire and frost, rallying his
collared thanes and driven by the ice skald's war-song. Break his collar (and
give him the Mead of Memory) and the jarl wakes to who he was — the open hand
returns, the feast becomes true welcome, and the Frosthall Gate opens on the
seventh tier.

## When it falls
Hrimgar wakes, throws down his frost-crown, and turns his freed hall to the
rising; the war-feast becomes a true feast, and the hall-folk sing songs that
mean something again. This is the second great *freeing* of a ruler on the
climb — after Skarn, before the throne — and it teaches the host that even the
tower's wardens can be won back. The hall-folk climb as the rising's own
heart: chieftains, skalds, and thanes carrying **Hearthfire** and the Mead of
Memory. The sixth tier is freed; the seventh opens.
