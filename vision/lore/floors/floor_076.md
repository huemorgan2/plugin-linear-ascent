# Floor 76 — The Quarry Runs (Elves)

> *"This is where the hunt ran its quarry down. The runs remember the chase. Step onto them and the wood decides you're the quarry now." — Quarry-warden Nael Bayhollow*

**Cut from:** the Elves' lamp-woods — the hunt's coursing-runs (kin to floors 31, 66) · **Gate-town:** Bayhollow · **Warden:** Foxglove

## The land
The hunting-runs of the elf-realm — a wood laid out for the chase, with driven
coursing-lanes, quarry-coverts, viewing-stands, and blind-hides, all crow-
watched from the high branches. The runs still *remember the chase:* set foot
on a coursing-lane and the whole wood turns on you as quarry, driving you down
the runs toward the killing-ground. The coverts hide teeth; the stands hold
watchers; the crows call the course. It is a floor where the hunt is real and
you are what's hunted, unless you learn to hunt it back.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** run-oak and covert-thorn walling the coursing-lanes,
  crow-nested above.
- **Ground & water plants:** run-grass streaming down the lanes, covert-
  bramble in the hides, and the pale foxglove that lines the killing-ground.
- **Under the Yoke:** the covert-bramble drives quarry back onto the lanes;
  the run-grass shows a false clear path to the killing-ground; the foxglove's
  pollen slows a fleeing heart.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Covert.** The quarry's deepest hiding-thicket cave, where
  the hunted go to ground. *Seed: the run-lore — how to read the coursing-
  lanes and turn hunter into hunted — is kept in the covert.*
- **The peak — the Vantage Stand.** The hunt's high viewing-mound over all
  the runs; the whole coursing-ground reads from it. *Seed: from it the runs
  can be re-set so the hunt no longer turns on travelers.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Run Gate.** The stair-lift is set at a
  coursing-lane's end, its arch a hunting-arch. *Seed: Foxglove courses the
  runs before it.*

## The people
Quarry-folk — elves of the coursing-runs: beaters, run-wardens, and quarry-
keepers who ran and read the hunt. Now the runs hunt them, and they shelter in
Bayhollow's coverts, learning to move as quarry without being caught. They
love a well-run course, a covert kept safe, and a chase ended clean. They fear
the turning runs and the course-steward's shade.
**The keeper:** Nael Bayhollow, quarry-warden — keeps the run-lore and moves
the runs like a ghost, and teaches the hunted how to double back on the hunt.

## The six
1. **Pacing hound** (N) — *was:* a coursing-hound. *now:* pacing the lanes
   to drive quarry toward the killing-ground. *Cured, it breaks pace and
   heels to a hand.*
2. **Stand crow** (N) — *was:* the hunt's watch-crows. *now:* massed on the
   stands, crying the course to turn the runs on the living. *Cured, they
   lift off the branches, quiet.*
3. **Run-hare** (N) — *was:* the quarry-hare. *now:* bolting the lanes to
   spring the coverts on a pursuer. *Cured, it doubles into the bramble and
   is gone.*
4. **Covert-boar** (N) — *was:* a covert-lurking boar. *now:* bursting from
   the hides onto the driven quarry. *Cured, it snorts off into the thorn.*
5. **Rival quarry-beast** (N) — *was:* the runs' great fox-wolf, the apex
   quarry the hunt most prized. *now:* fever-turned hunter, coursing the
   living down the lanes, its bite foxglove-venomed. *Cured, the great beast
   slips into the covert, quarry once more.*
6. **Course steward** (W) — *was:* the hunt's steward, dead at the killing-
   ground, aether in his hunting-horn. *now:* a grey figure that blows the
   course to turn the whole wood on travelers. *Broken (or out-run), the horn
   falls silent and it drains into the run.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Mark — spell.** Marks a quarry so the caster can track and find it
  unerringly through any cover — the hunt's true art, turned to the climber's
  hand. *Seed: learn it from the run-lore to hunt the tower's hidden
  wardens above.*
- **Quarry-bone — material.** The keen worked bone-and-silver of the hunt's
  gear, and covert-silver beside it; light fast weapons for a runner. *Seed:
  the Covert's cache.*
- **Coursing-draught — potion.** A runner's brew of speed and wind; a climber
  runs down or outruns anything for a spell.

## The Warden — Foxglove
The rival quarry-beast grown vast — a great fox-wolf welded with venom-glands
and coursing-harness, the run's apex quarry made into its master hunter — it
fights by driving climbers down the coursing-lanes into its killing-ground and
striking with a foxglove-venom bite that slows the heart. Cure it (out-course
and break it) and the great beast is quarry once more; the runs stop turning;
the Run Gate opens.

## When it falls
Bayhollow re-sets the runs from the Vantage Stand and the wood stops hunting
travelers; the chase is sport again, not slaughter. The quarry-folk climb as
the host's trackers and coursers, carrying **Mark** and quarry-bone — the folk
who run down whatever the Making hides and hunt the tower's own hunters on the
floors ahead.
