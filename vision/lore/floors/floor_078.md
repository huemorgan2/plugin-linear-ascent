# Floor 78 — The Eye Wall (Men)

> *"The calm is the trap. Out there the wind would kill you in a breath — but in here, you just... stop. Sit down. Rest your eyes. And never get up again." — Eye-dweller Mera Lullside*

**Cut from:** the sky-realm of Men — the still eye of the storm-queen's tempest (ringing floor 70) · **Gate-town:** Lullside · **Warden:** Stillmark

## The land
The uncanny calm eye of the great tempest — a wide bowl of dead-still air
ringed by the roaring eye-wall of Zephyra's storm, where the rain hangs
suspended, the light is soft and gold, and a warm, drowsy peace lies over
everything like a drug. After the screaming gales of the outer floors, the
stillness is bliss — and lethal, because the eye lulls the will to move, to
climb, to live, and those who sit to rest in it never rise. It is a floor
whose danger is not fury but a peace that kills by kindness. The stair-lift
stands in a crag at the eye-wall's foot, on the edge of the roar; Stillmark
holds the Still Point before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** still-cypress standing motionless in the dead air, and
  the drowsing lull-willow at the eye's center.
- **Ground & water plants:** hush-grass that never stirs, suspended rain-lily
  hanging in the still air, and the pale lull-poppy of the calm.
- **Under the Yoke:** the lull-poppy's scent deepens the killing peace; the
  hush-grass muffles all urgency; the rain-lilies hang a drowsing mist that
  slows the heart.

## Places of interest
- **The Barometer Vault.** The pressure-readers' undercroft, the one built
  place the lull can't reach; the eye-lore — how to keep moving in the calm —
  is kept here, with the master barometer that reads the storm. *Seed: hold the
  vault and you hold the art of staying awake in the killing peace.*
- **The Sitting Garden.** A becalmed pleasure-garden where the eye's first
  captives sat to rest a thousand years ago and never rose — folk still seated
  among the suspended rain, breathing, dreaming, and the pressure-ghosts
  thickest around them. *Seed: rouse the seated here and you wake a whole
  gentle crowd to the rising at a stroke.*
- **The Still Point.** The dead center of the eye, stillest and most lethal;
  the whole storm reads from it. *Seed: it is the calm's heart — break the lull
  here and the eye lets its captives go.*

## The people
Eye-dwellers — Men caught in the calm when the tempest formed: weather-readers,
rain-wrights, and lull-wardens who fight the drowse by will and habit. They
shelter in Lullside near the eye-wall's roar, using the noise to stay awake,
rousing those who sit too long. They love a task to keep the hands busy, a
read barometer, and a companion kept awake. They fear the lull-poppy peace and
the pressure-ghosts that welcome you to rest.
**The keeper:** Mera Lullside, eye-warden — keeps the eye-lore and the master
barometer, and walks the calm shaking the drowsing awake before the peace
takes them.

## The six
1. **Eye-wall harpy** (N) — *was:* an eye-wall raptor. *now:* fever-drawn
   half-human, riding the calm's edge, stooping on the drowsing. *Cured, it
   folds to a plain gull and drifts in the still air.*
2. **Rain hound** (N) — *was:* a storm-country hound. *now:* padding the calm
   silently, lying down beside the resting to keep them still. *Cured, it
   nudges a sleeper awake and heels.*
3. **Calm-crow** (N) — *was:* an eye-dwelling crow. *now:* massed and
   motionless, waiting on the still to fail. *Cured, they lift, breaking the
   hush.*
4. **Pressure-adder** (N) — *was:* a hush-grass adder. *now:* slow and heavy,
   striking a drowsing ankle. *Cured, it slides into the still grass.*
5. **Lull courtier** (P) — *was:* one of Zephyra's court, first-taken and
   collared, left in the eye. *now:* a serene compelled figure that invites
   the living to sit and rest, forever. *Pressed — a real death unless the
   collar is broken; the freed courtiers know the way through the eye-wall.*
6. **Pressure-ghost** (W) — *was:* an eye-dweller who lay down to rest and
   never rose, aether in the killing calm. *now:* a peaceful drifting figure
   that beckons the living to lie beside it. *Broken (or roused), the peace
   cracks and it drains into the still air.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Lull — spell.** Imposes or lifts a deep calm — pacifies a raging foe, or
  rouses the drugged and panicked; the eye-wardens' double-edged art. *Seed:
  learn it to break the killing calm and to steady the host near the throne.*
- **Pressure-crystal — material.** The eye-glass grown in the dead-still air,
  a barometer-stone and a calm-charm; steadies whatever it's set in. *Seed:
  the Still Point.*
- **The master barometer — tool.** Mera's great storm-glass, kept in the
  Barometer Vault; it reads the eye-wall's turning and shows the one safe
  moment to cross into the roar. *Seed: win it to time the crossing and to read
  the throne's own storms above.*
- **Sharpwake draught — brew.** An eye-dweller's draught against drowse and
  compulsion; keeps a climber alert and moving through any lull or lulling
  spell.

## The Warden — Stillmark
A pressure-ghost grown vast and welded at the Still Point, the embodiment of
the killing calm — it fights not with fury but with peace, deepening the lull
until limbs go heavy and the will to fight drains away, welcoming climbers to
lie down and rest. It is a stillness the tower quickened, so its end is an
*eviction:* rouse the eye and break the calm at the Still Point and the
borrowed peace disperses; the captives wake; the stair-lift opens.

## When it falls
Lullside breaks the killing calm at the Still Point and the eye lets its
drowsing captives go — folk who sat down a thousand years ago rise and walk.
The eye-dwellers climb as the host's wake-keepers and calmers, carrying
**Lull** and pressure-crystal — the folk who keep an army awake through the
tower's drugging peace and pacify what can't be fought on the Making-floors
ahead.
