# Floor 5 — The Flooded Mine (Giants)

> *"We built the pumps to keep the deep dry. Now the pumps are drowned too." — Durn Sumphand*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — the working galleries below the adit · **Gate-town:** Pumpstead · **Warden:** Sumplock

## The land
The mine's true depth: gallery after gallery sunk into black water, the
great pumps that held it back now silent and half-submerged. Drowned tools
hang on drowned pegs. The water is dead-flat and dead-black, and it goes
down further than any lamp will show. Where the Rustwater Adit above only
wept, this floor is the flood the weeping fed — a giant industry gone under,
one lift below its own mine-mouth.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — worked stone and drowned pit-props.
- **Ground & water plants:** sump-weed in pale rafts, blind-cave lichen, and
  the ghost-fungus that fruits on the rotting props above the waterline.
- **Under the Yoke:** the sump-weed has turned carnivorous, closing on
  anything that stirs the surface, and the ghost-fungus glows to lure it.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Sump Galleries.** The drowned deep itself, a maze of
  flooded drifts where the water hides old shafts. *Seed: the deepest
  gallery holds the master pump-valve, and the crews sealed in with it.*
- **The peak — the Pump-Head.** The great broken pumping-engine tower rising
  from the flood, its walking-beam frozen mid-stroke. *Seed: restarted, it
  could drain the whole floor.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Drain Gate.** The stair-lift sits behind
  a dry adit carved high up a rock face, reachable only when the water drops.
  *Seed: it cannot be opened until the Pump-Head runs.*

## The people
Pump-crews and drift-miners — giants and delver-folk who kept the deep dry
for generations, proud of a floor you could walk without wet feet. Now they
cling to the few dry galleries, rationing air and lamp-oil, listening to
their own drowned machines. They love a pump that runs clean, a dry seam,
and a shift-song. They fear the water finding the last dry room.
**The keeper:** Durn Sumphand, master of pumps — stands watch on the Pump-
Head's walking-beam, willing it to move.

## The six
1. **Sump eel** (N) — *was:* a blind drift-eel of the deep water. *now:*
   fever-long, it strikes up from the flat black at a lamp's reflection.
   *Cured, it sinks back into the dark water.*
2. **Blind cave-fish** (N) — *was:* pallid schooling fish. *now:* massed and
   biting, they boil the surface where warmth touches it. *Cured, the shoal
   scatters into the deep.*
3. **Gallery newt** (N) — *was:* a hand-length wall-newt. *now:* grown to an
   arm, clinging to the ceilings and dropping. *Cured, it clings and stays.*
4. **Sump-crawler** (N) — *was:* a cave-crab of the drifts. *now:* shield-
   backed, many-legged, scuttling the flooded floors underfoot. *Cured, it
   sidles into a crack.*
5. **Bilge kobold** (P) — *was:* a first-taken conscript, set to bail the
   deep by hand. *now:* collared, wet to the bone, swinging a bailing-hook
   at any light. *A real death; the mercy is the collar, not the body.*
6. **Drowned-miner husk** (W) — *was:* a drift-crew's lost lamp and gear,
   aether pooled in the flood over them. *now:* a lamp-lit shape that wades
   the galleries in the shape of a working man. *Broken, the lamp gutters
   and it drains into the sump.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Dryfoot — ward (spell).** Lets a climber cross deep water on its skin
  for a few strides; carved into the Pump-Head's beam. *Seed: reach the
  master valve across the deepest gallery.*
- **Sumpsteel — metal.** Drowned steel that took no rust in the black water;
  the finest tools in the low tower are forged from it. *Seed: salvage the
  drowned tool-store.*
- **Diver's breath — potion.** A crew's draught for the long bail; a lungful
  that lasts underwater far past reason.

## The Warden — Sumplock
A giant sump-eel of the deep, welded through with a pump-valve maw — it takes
water in and jets it out hard enough to sweep a climber off their feet, then
strikes from the wave. It rules the flooded galleries where its own element
hides it. Break it and the master valve turns, the Pump-Head shudders back
to life, and the Drain Gate at last comes clear of the flood.

## When it falls
The walking-beam strokes; the water falls; drowned galleries drain into
drier dark. Durn's crews come up the lift with pump-iron and long
experience of black water — the folk the host will want most on the drowned
and flooded floors still to come. Pumpstead lights its shift-lamps and, for
the first time in memory, keeps its feet dry.
