# Floor 62 — The Reactor Scar (Waste)

> *"There's a fire here you can't see and can't feel until you're already dying of it. The old world built a sun in a box, and the box broke." — Scar-warden Grint Scarwatch*

**Cut from:** the burned borderlands — the Waste, around a melted-down aether-reactor · **Gate-town:** Scarwatch · **Warden:** Palescorch

## The land
A vast blast-crater of fused colorless glass — the scar left when one of the
old world's great aether-reactors melted down, burning still with a colorless
flame that sheds no light and casts no shadow, felt only as a sick prickling
in the blood. Nothing grows in the crater; the air shimmers with an invisible
heat; the ground is scar-glass that rings underfoot. It is the deepest wound
in the stolen world, a place the fever itself seems to pour from — and the few
who survive here do so by lore, luck, and the pale water that cleanses the
sickness.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — colorless glass and shimmering air.
- **Ground & water plants:** scar-lichen (the only thing that eats the
  colorless flame), pale-fern at the crater's cool edge, and the glow-less
  null-moss.
- **Under the Yoke:** the scar-lichen flares the invisible flame where it's
  trodden; the null-moss drinks warmth and light both; the pale-fern's spores
  carry the sickness.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Core Pit.** The reactor's exposed heart at the crater's
  center, where the colorless fire burns fiercest. *Seed: a single intact
  fuel-slug there could power the rising's greatest work — or kill everyone
  who carries it wrong.*
- **The peak — the Crater Rim.** The high glassy lip ringing the scar; the
  whole wound reads from it, and the safe cool edge from the killing center.
  *Seed: a warded beacon here maps the survivable ground.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Scar Gate.** The stair-lift is set in the
  crater wall behind a curtain of colorless flame. *Seed: Palescorch burns
  before it, unseen.*

## The people
Scar-survivors — Waste-folk who live at the crater's cool rim, cleansing the
sickness with pale-water and reading the invisible fire by the prickle in
their skin. Hollow-cheeked and careful, they salvage what the old world's sun-
in-a-box left behind. They love a cool day, a cleansing draught, and ground
that doesn't prickle. They fear the colorless flame and the canyon-hermit
ogres driven mad by it.
**The keeper:** Grint Scarwatch, scar-warden — reads the invisible fire like a
map and keeps the pale-water flowing, and glows faintly himself from too many
years too close.

## The six
1. **Scar salamander** (N) — *was:* a crater salamander. *now:* colorless-
   flame-skinned, invisible in the shimmer until it strikes. *Cured, it dims
   to a plain lizard and slips into a cool crack.*
2. **Scar rat** (N) — *was:* a crater-edge rat. *now:* glow-sick and bold,
   nesting in the warm null-moss. *Cured, it bolts for the cool rim.*
3. **Glass-vulture** (N) — *was:* a scar scavenger. *now:* glass-feathered,
   stooping through the shimmer unseen. *Cured, it wheels off over the rim.*
4. **Ash-serpent** (N) — *was:* a crater adder. *now:* long and null-scaled,
   striking from the colorless haze. *Cured, it pours into the scar-glass.*
5. **Canyon-hermit ogre** (P) — *was:* a Waste-born conscript stationed to
   guard the Core Pit, alone, for too long. *now:* collared, glow-mad and
   ranting, hurling scar-glass. *Pressed — a real death, and a pitying one;
   the freed hermits know the safe paths through the scar.*
6. **Colorless flame** (W) — *was:* nothing — raw aether burning loose from
   the broken core. *now:* an unseen fire in a walking shape, felt only as
   the sickness deepening, that drifts to cling to living warmth. *Broken (or
   warded), it earths out and drains into the Core Pit.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Colorward — spell.** Reveals the colorless flame as visible light and
  shields the caster from its sickness for a spell; the survivors' one true
  defense. *Seed: learn it to cross the scar and to face the reactor-lit
  floors near the throne.*
- **Scar-glass — material.** The blast-fused glass of the crater, aether-
  charged and keen; the core of the tower's deadliest aether-weapons — and
  the rising's. *Seed: cut it from the Core Pit's edge.*
- **Palewater tonic — potion.** The survivors' cleansing draught; purges
  aether-sickness and glow-poisoning outright — the master cure for the
  radiant floors ahead. *Seed: drawn from the rim's pale springs.*

## The Warden — Palescorch
A scar salamander grown vast and welded around a shard of the broken core, so
it burns with colorless flame that no eye can see and no armor turns — it
fights invisible in the shimmer, its every touch deepening the sickness,
raising the colorless flame in walls across the crater. Break it (with
Colorward's sight) and the invisible fire dims to something that can be fought
and quenched; the Scar Gate's flame-curtain shows and parts.

## When it falls
Scarwatch draws an intact fuel-slug from the Core Pit under full ward and the
colorless fire is banked; the crater's sickness eases at the rim. The scar-
survivors climb as the host's flame-seers and cleansers, carrying **Colorward**,
scar-glass, and casks of palewater — the folk who reveal the tower's unseen
fires and cure the army of the sickness the throne pours down.
