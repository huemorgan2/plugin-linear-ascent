# Floor 44 — The Ogre Steps (Waste)

> *"Terrace on terrace of kilns, all still firing, all still empty. The goats graze the heat like grass. The ogres tend a bake that's a thousand years done." — Kiln-master Grint Kilnrest*

**Cut from:** the burned borderlands — the Waste, worked into a kiln-country · **Gate-town:** Kilnrest · **Warden:** Kilnfist

## The land
A great hillside cut into terraced kiln-steps — hundreds of lime- and brick-
kilns stacked in tiers up the slope, all still firing on captured heat, the
air rippling and the whole hill glowing orange in the dead dark. Fire-goats
graze the hot terraces; ash drifts like grey snow. The Waste-folk fired lime
and brick here for the realms above, and the kilns never cooled. Climbing the
Steps means climbing terrace by terrace through baking heat, past ovens that
would cook a careless climber in a breath. The stair-lift stands behind a
glowing kiln-arch on the mid-slope, its stones fired to glass; Kilnfist
stokes the fire before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — kiln-stacks and terrace-walls.
- **Ground & water plants:** kiln-moss on the cooler steps, fire-thistle in
  the ash-drifts, and the heat-loving ember-poppy on the terrace-tops.
- **Under the Yoke:** the fire-thistle spits sparks; the ember-poppy's pollen
  bursts into flame; the kiln-moss hides draw-holes that vent oven-heat.

## Places of interest
- **The Draw-Hole.** The stoke-cellar beneath the greatest kiln, where the
  terrace-folk fed and drew the fire and where the master draught-valve stands.
  *Seed: choke the valve and the whole hillside's kilns can be banked to a
  safe, crossable heat.*
- **The Top Terrace.** The highest and hottest kiln, crowning the hill, that
  the Steps read from. *Seed: its firebox can bake the rising's own ceramic-
  plate — armor lighter and tougher than steel.*
- **The Slip-Incline.** The great gravity-tramway of scorched rails and
  counterweights that once ran fired brick down the slope, now seized and
  half-buried in ash. *Seed: free it and the host can haul heavy gear — plate,
  engines, stores — straight up the burning tier.*

## The people
Terrace-folk — Waste-born kiln-masters and stokers who fire lime and brick,
heat-hardened folk who read a bake by the glow and never touch a kiln bare-
handed. They shelter in Kilnrest on the cool lower steps, tending fires for a
realm that's gone. They love a clean bake, a true-fired brick, and a kiln
banked safe. They fear the runaway kilns and the terrace-ogres.
**The keeper:** Grint Kilnrest, kiln-master — knows every kiln's temper by
its glow and holds the master draught-valve.

## The six
1. **Kiln salamander** (N) — *was:* a firebox salamander. *now:* glowing,
   denning in the ovens, lashing at stokers. *Cured, it cools and slips into
   a draw-hole.*
2. **Kiln goat** (N) — *was:* a terrace-grazing goat. *now:* fire-fleeced,
   butting climbers off the narrow steps into the ovens. *Cured, it picks off
   up the terraces.*
3. **Kiln vulture** (N) — *was:* an ash-sky scavenger. *now:* heat-riding,
   stooping through the shimmer unseen. *Cured, it wheels off into the ash.*
4. **Kiln-beetle** (N) — *was:* a stoke-cellar beetle. *now:* ember-shelled,
   massing in the draw-holes. *Cured, it trundles into a cool crack.*
5. **Terrace ogre** (P) — *was:* a Waste-born conscript set to stoke the
   kilns. *now:* collared, brick-fisted, hurling fired brick and hot ash.
   *Pressed — a real death; the deserters know the terrace-paths and the
   valves.*
6. **Ash-wight** (W) — *was:* a stoker who fell into a firebox, aether in the
   ash. *now:* a drifting ash-figure that reignites the ember-poppies around
   the living. *Broken, it scatters to cold ash and drains down.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Fireclay — spell.** Shapes and fires clay to stone-hardness in an instant
  — a wall, a shield, a seal; the kiln-masters' art. *Seed: learn it to climb
  the Steps and bake ceramic-plate at the Top Terrace.*
- **Ogre-ware plate — material.** The terrace-folk's fired ceramic armor,
  lighter than steel and shrugging off both blade and flame. *Seed: bake it
  in the Top Terrace's firebox.*
- **Fireproofing draught — tonic.** A stoker's brew that steels flesh against
  heat and flame for a spell — essential armor for the fire-floors above;
  drawn in Kilnrest. *Seed: carry a flask before the first firebox.*

## The Warden — Kilnfist
A terrace ogre grown vast, welded into a kiln-oven body that carries its own
firebox — its fists are fired brick, its breath is oven-heat, and it fights by
opening the terrace draw-holes to vent scalding blasts across the steps. Break
it and the kilns can be banked; the Steps cool enough to climb; the stair-lift
opens.

## When it falls
Kilnrest banks the runaway kilns and fires ceramic-plate at the Top Terrace
for the rising. The terrace-folk climb as the host's kiln-wrights and armorers,
carrying **Fireclay** and ogre-ware plate — the folk who bake an army's armor
and raise fired-clay walls against the fire-floors and the throne's own heat.
