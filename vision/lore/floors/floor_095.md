# Floor 95 — The Trophy Vaults (Made)

> *"Everything he ever took, he kept. Every hero's sword, every kingdom's crown, every liberator who came before you — they're all in the vaults, labelled. He wanted you to see your own name on a shelf before you reached him." — Hoardside curator Vess*

**The Made — throne-tiers:** the King's hoard of stolen glory · **Gate-town:** Hoardside · **Warden:** Curator

## The land
The trophy-vaults of the Made — the King's vast hoard-galleries where every
spoil of the Theft and every relic of the taken is displayed on endless
shelves: crowns of drowned kingdoms, the arms of fallen heroes, the standards
of a hundred freed floors re-captured, and — grimly labelled — the gear of
every climber who came before and failed. It is Vharuk's museum of his own
conquest, curated by collared thralls, a monument to the vanity of a King who
keeps proof of everything he's unmade. The relics here are the richest arsenal
in the tower — and the most haunted.

## "Flora" — the Making's growth
- **Canopy:** none — vaulted shelving and dust-light.
- **Ground growth:** dust-lichen on the cases, hoard-moss in the plinth-seams,
  and the grey label-fungus that fruits with the name of what it grows on.
- **How the Making twists it:** the label-fungus curses a relic to be taken;
  the hoard-moss glues a spoil to its shelf; the dust-lichen wakes an idle
  trophy to defend itself.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Deep Vault.** The lowest, oldest hoard, where the first
  spoils of the Theft are kept. *Seed: the regalia of Aldervale-that-was — the
  crowns and seals that would legitimize a restored realm — lie here.*
- **The peak — the Display Hall.** The great gallery where the King's proudest
  trophies stand; the vaults read from it. *Seed: the fight ends here, among
  the arms of fallen liberators.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Vault Gate.** The stair-lift is behind the
  hoard's adamant door. *Seed: the Curator holds the keys.*

## The people — who you are freeing
Hoardside curators — collared archivists made to dust and catalogue the proof
of their own world's ruin, keepers of a museum of grief. They love a relic
returned to its owner, a label torn off, and a shelf emptied. They fear the
waking trophies and being catalogued themselves as the latest failed climber.
**The keeper:** Vess of Hoardside — keeps the provenance-lore, knows whose
every relic was, and can put a hero's arms back in a hero's hand.

## The six
1. **Vault rat** (N) — *was:* a hoard rat. *now:* dust-grey, nesting in the
   regalia. *Cured, it slips behind a plinth.*
2. **Case-moth** (N) — *was:* a gallery moth. *now:* devouring the standards.
   *Cured, it drifts off.*
3. **Collared curator** (P) — *was:* a captured archivist collared to guard
   the hoard. *now:* a key-fisted drudge that sics the trophies on thieves.
   *Pressed — Break-collar frees it, and it opens the cases.*
4. **Trophy-wight** (W) — *was:* a fallen liberator, aether left in the arms
   the King displayed. *now:* a grey figure that takes up its old sword to
   fight the living once more. *Broken, the hero drains free and its arms fall
   to the climber's hand.*
5. **Animate relic** (W) — *was:* nothing — a spoil quickened by hoard-aether.
   *now:* a floating blade, crown, or standard that defends its shelf.
   *Broken, it clatters dead to the floor and can be claimed.*
6. **Regalia-thrall** (W) — *was:* a king of a drowned realm, aether bound
   into his displayed crown. *now:* a crowned husk that guards the Deep Vault's
   regalia. *Broken, the old king drains to rest and his crown comes free.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Repossess — spell.** Seizes a stolen object back to its rightful owner and
  disarms relic-traps; the curators' restoring-art. *Seed: learn it to reclaim
  the hoard — arming the whole rising from the King's own museum.*
- **Hero-steel — material.** The reclaimed arms of fallen liberators and the
  regalia of drowned realms — the finest weapons and armor in the tower, each
  with a name. *Seed: empty the shelves.*
- **Owner's draught — potion.** A curator's brew that lets a claimed relic
  answer to a new hand without the old ghost's grudge.

## The Warden — Curator
A vast construct accreted from a thousand trophies — a figure of welded crowns,
swords, and standards, the hoard itself risen to guard its glory, studded with
the arms of every hero it has shelved. A construct of the Making, it fights by
turning the fallen liberators' own famous weapons against the climber. Its end
is an *eviction:* Repossess and break it, and the borrowed aether drains, the
trophies clatter dead, and the arms of the fallen fall free to be carried
onward. The Vault Gate opens.

## When it falls
Vess's curators throw open the vaults, and the rising arms itself from the
King's own museum — the crowns of Aldervale, the swords of a hundred fallen
liberators, all going up the stair at last in living hands. The failed climbers
of ages are laid to rest and avenged. The freed Hoardside curators climb as the
host's quartermasters, carrying **Repossess** and hero-steel to the throne.
