# Floor 16 — The Hollow Lanes (Men)

> *"You can't see over the banks and you can't see round the bends. You just walk and hope the lane's still yours." — Drover Cobb Hedgeharbor*

**Cut from:** the droving country of Men · **Gate-town:** Hedgeharbor · **Warden:** Thornhide

## The land
A country of sunken droving-lanes — holloways worn shoulder-deep by
centuries of driven cattle, running between hedgebanks grown so high and
thick they meet overhead in a green tunnel. The floodlight barely reaches
the lane-floor; the light comes down in coins. It is a maze with no map, all
blind bends and hidden gates, taken with the drove still on the road. You can
walk it for an hour and never see the sky, and never be sure you're alone.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** the hedgebanks themselves — hazel, thorn, and holly
  grown to a roof, laced with old man's beard.
- **Ground & water plants:** hart's-tongue fern and moss on the lane-walls,
  and the deep mud of a road that never dried.
- **Under the Yoke:** the holly has grown swords, the hedges close behind a
  traveler, and the old man's beard reaches down for warmth.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Sunken Meet.** The deepest holloway, where four lanes
  meet under an earth-roof of grown roots. *Seed: the drovers cached their
  toll-hoard and their true lane-map here before the banks closed.*
- **The peak — the Bank-Top.** A single high hedgebank a climber can breach
  to see over the whole maze. *Seed: from it the true lanes read clear —
  worth a map for the whole floor.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Drove Gate.** The stair-lift is set where
  a lane cuts into a rock spur, its arch worn smooth by cattle-horn. *Seed:
  Thornhide blocks the meet before it.*

## The people
Drovers and lane-wrights — Men of the road who moved the realm's herds along
these lanes and know every bend by the feel of the mud. Cut off mid-drove,
they shelter in Hedgeharbor, penned by their own closing hedges. They love an
open lane, a quiet herd, and an inn at the end of a long drove. They fear the
lanes that rearrange and the wolves that hunt the blind bends.
**The keeper:** Cobb Hedgeharbor, head drover — carries a lane-staff notched
with every turning, and no longer trusts the notches.

## The six
1. **Bank marten** (N) — *was:* a hedgebank marten. *now:* long and bold,
   dropping from the green roof onto necks. *Cured, it flows back into the
   bank.*
2. **Lane bull-boar** (N) — *was:* a drove-following boar. *now:* huge in
   the narrow lane where there's no room to dodge. *Cured, it snorts past
   and is gone round the bend.*
3. **Hedge-blind wolf** (N) — *was:* a lane-hunting wolf. *now:* it uses the
   blind bends, striking from a lane you didn't know joined yours. *Cured, it
   trots off down a side-lane.*
4. **Drove-ox** (N) — *was:* a herd-ox of the last drove. *now:* riderless
   and panicked, stampeding the narrow lanes. *Cured, it calms and stands
   chewing.*
5. **Lane crow** (N) — *was:* a drove-following crow. *now:* nesting in the
   green roof, mobbing anything that looks up. *Cured, the mob lifts and
   scatters.*
6. **Hollow-wight** (W) — *was:* a drover lost when the lanes closed, aether
   in the deep mud. *now:* a mud-figure that calls a herd-cry round a blind
   bend to lead climbers into a dead-end. *Broken, it slumps into the mud.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Wayfind — spell.** Points true north and holds a path in the mind through
  any maze; cut into the Sunken Meet's roof-root. *Seed: learn it to map the
  Hollow Lanes and never be turned around again.*
- **Drove-iron — metal.** The tough ox-shoe iron of the lanes, forged into
  gear that takes long punishment. *Seed: the toll-hoard's cache.*
- **Drover's brew — potion.** A road-draught of the long lanes; steadies a
  climber for a long push without rest.

## The Warden — Thornhide
A lane bull-boar, welded under a hide of thorn-iron and living holly that
regrows as it's cut — it fights in the narrow meet where there's no room to
flank, and the closing hedges are its ally. Break it and the hedges fall
still; the lanes stop rearranging; the Drove Gate opens.

## When it falls
Hedgeharbor's lanes hold their shape and open end to end; the last drove
comes home. The drovers climb as the host's pathfinders, carrying **Wayfind**
— the folk who will keep an army from getting lost in the mazework floors
still ahead.
