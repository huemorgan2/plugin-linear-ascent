# Floor 49 — The Rime Forest (Giants)

> *"The whole forest froze standing. Touch a tree and it rings. The stags ring loudest — their antlers are glass now." — Forester Hune Firwatch*

**Cut from:** the Giants' high cold country — a great conifer forest · **Gate-town:** Firwatch · **Warden:** Rimehide

## The land
A vast conifer forest flash-frozen where it stood — firs and pines sheathed in
clear rime-glass, every needle a glittering blade, the whole wood ringing like
struck crystal when the cold wind moves through it. Snow lies deep and blue;
frozen stags with antlers of clear glass step between the glass trees; the
light comes silver-white. It was the giants' timber-forest, taken and frozen in
a single killing night, and it is achingly beautiful and lethal in equal
measure — one careless swing and a whole tree of glass needles comes down.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** rime-glazed fir and pine, glass-sheathed and razor-
  needled, and the ancient bell-cedar that rings the whole wood.
- **Ground & water plants:** frost-fern under the snow, glass-holly, and the
  chime-moss that rings when trodden.
- **Under the Yoke:** the glass-needles shed like falling knives at a loud
  sound; the glass-holly cuts; the chime-moss rings alarms to the herds.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Deadfall Hollow.** A shelter under a jam of frozen fallen
  timber, out of the ringing wind. *Seed: the foresters' fire-hearth and the
  thawing-lore that could unfreeze the wood are kept here.*
- **The peak — the Watch-Fir.** The tallest bell-cedar, the foresters'
  lookout; the wood reads from its crown. *Seed: rung true, it sets the whole
  forest chiming in a harmony that shatters the fever-glass gently.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Rime Gate.** The stair-lift is set in a
  glass-sheathed scarp, its arch hung with icicle-glass. *Seed: Rimehide dens
  in the deadfall before it.*

## The people
Foresters — giant timber-wrights and delver-woodfolk who felled and hauled the
forest, careful quiet folk who now move in silence lest the glass come down.
They shelter in Firwatch in the deadfall hollows, keeping fires small and
voices low. They love a straight-grown fir, a clean fell, and a fire that
holds against the cold. They fear the shedding glass and the chime-sprites in
the ringing wood.
**The keeper:** Hune Firwatch, head forester — keeps the thawing-lore and the
fire-hearth, and reads the wood's danger by the pitch of its ringing.

## The six
1. **Glass-antlered stag** (N) — *was:* a forest stag. *now:* antlers grown to
   clear glass blades, goring and shattering. *Cured, the glass melts to
   velvet and it steps into the trees.*
2. **Bell-wise wolf** (N) — *was:* a timber-wolf. *now:* hunting by the wood's
   chimes, striking where the ringing hides its approach. *Cured, it lopes
   off silent over the snow.*
3. **Frost-owl** (N) — *was:* a fir-roosting owl. *now:* silent and white,
   stooping through the glass canopy. *Cured, it wings up into a rimed
   crown.*
4. **Ice-boar** (N) — *was:* a mast-rooting boar. *now:* rime-plated,
   charging through the glass trees and bringing needles down. *Cured, it
   crashes off into the deadfall.*
5. **Hammer-logger troll** (N) — *was:* a cold-forest troll that felled timber
   for its den. *now:* swinging a frozen log like a maul, shattering the
   glass wood as it fights. *Cured, the slow giant shoulders its log and
   lumbers off to fell in peace.*
6. **Chime sprite** (W) — *was:* nothing — aether ringing loose in the frozen
   bell-cedar. *now:* a shard of singing light that shatters glass-needles
   down onto the living with its ringing. *Broken, its note cracks off and it
   drains into a tree.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Shatter — spell.** Rings a killing resonance that bursts ice, glass, and
  brittle stone; learned from the Watch-Fir's chime. *Seed: master it to clear
  the glass wood and, above, to shatter the throne's crystal defenses.*
- **Rime-glass — material.** The clear rime-glass of the frozen wood and the
  shed stag-antlers — cut into blades keener than any steel, and lenses for
  aether-optics. *Seed: harvest shed antler-glass.*
- **Warmth-mead — potion.** A forester's mulled brew; wards the killing cold
  and lifts the heart with real warmth for a spell.

## The Warden — Rimehide
A hammer-logger troll grown vast, welded under a hide of rime-glass that
regrows from the cold — it fights by felling the glass trees onto climbers and
setting the whole wood ringing to bring the needle-canopy down. Break it (and
ring the Watch-Fir true) and the wood chimes into gentle thaw; the shedding
stops; the Rime Gate's glass melts enough to pass.

## When it falls
Firwatch rings the Watch-Fir and the forest thaws in a slow silver harmony,
the glass returning to needle and sap. The foresters climb as the host's
timber-wrights and glass-cutters, carrying **Shatter** and rime-glass blades —
the folk who can bring down any frozen or crystal barrier the tower raises
toward the throne.
