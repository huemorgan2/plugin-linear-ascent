# Floor 23 — The Anvil Commons (Giants)

> *"A hundred anvils and one fire. That's how a commons forges. The tower cut the fire and left the anvils ringing anyway." — Old Hammersmith Dwald*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — a communal forge-floor · **Gate-town:** Hammerhome · **Warden:** Anvilback

## The land
A vast communal forge-hall where the giant-smiths worked shoulder to
shoulder — a floor of a hundred anvils around one great shared hearth, bellows
the size of sails, and slack-tubs like ponds. The hearth is cold now, but the
anvils *ring* on their own, struck by nothing, a maddening chorus that never
rests. Coal-dust hangs black in the floodlight. It was the beating heart of
the giants' craft, and it beats still, on empty. The stair-lift stands
in a rock face behind the great cold hearth, its arch cut as an anvil's
silhouette; Anvilback holds the hearth before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — stone, iron, and cold coal.
- **Ground & water plants:** clinker-moss on the slag-heaps, slack-tub weed
  in the quench-ponds, and coal-fungus fruiting in the dust.
- **Under the Yoke:** the coal-fungus smoulders and chokes; the slack-tub
  weed scalds, holding the quench-water at a boil that never cools.

## Places of interest
- **The Slack-Tub Undercroft.** The flooded quench-cellar beneath the
  anvils, its water held forever at a boil by the fevered slack-weed.
  *Seed: the master-smiths sank their finest work here to keep it from the
  tower — reachable only by someone who can stand the scald.*
- **The Great Chimney.** The shared stack over the central hearth, wide as
  a road and climbing into the dark. *Seed: draw it and the cold hearth can
  be relit — the forge that could arm the whole rising.*
- **The Hundred Anvils.** The commons floor itself, a hundred giant anvils
  ringed around the dead hearth, each ringing on its own under no hand — a
  chorus that never rests. *Seed: quiet one anvil and it holds the tune of
  the smith who last owned it; a hundred lost names lie waiting in the
  noise.*

## The people
Smiths — giant hammermen and their delver-strikers, a craft-commons where no
one owns the fire and everyone owns the work. They shelter in Hammerhome
around dead forges, deafened by the ringing anvils, hoarding coal they can't
light. They love a true weld, a shared hearth, and a tool that outlasts its
maker. They fear the armorer-orcs from the warcamp and the half-forged things
the tower leaves behind.
**The keeper:** Dwald, old hammersmith — keeps one banked ember alive in a
clay pot, waiting for a hearth worth lighting.

## The six
1. **Bellows hound** (N) — *was:* a forge watch-dog. *now:* barrel-chested,
   breathing forge-heat, charging in a gust of sparks. *Cured, it pants,
   cools, and lies down by a cold anvil.*
2. **Forge-cricket** (N) — *was:* a hearth-cricket. *now:* iron-shelled,
   massed, their chirr matching the phantom anvil-ring. *Cured, they fall
   silent and scatter.*
3. **Coal-rat** (N) — *was:* a coal-store rat. *now:* soot-black and bold,
   nesting in the warm slag. *Cured, it bolts into a clinker-heap.*
4. **Spark-moth** (N) — *was:* an ember-drawn moth. *now:* alight at the
   wings, settling on skin to burn. *Cured, it drifts off, dark.*
5. **Red Orc armorer** (P) — *no native of this hall.* Red Orcs are
   warcamp stock from the Waste; this one was marched up from Skarn's camp
   as pressed garrison labor to run the giants' captured forge — the tower
   needed hands that could stand a giant's fire and did not care whose.
   *was:* a pressed smith set to forge collars and warframes. *now:*
   collared to an anvil, hammering under the lash. *A real death; the ones
   who down the hammer know how to un-make a collar.*
6. **Half-forged thing** (W) — *was:* a warframe abandoned unfinished, aether
   in the cooling iron. *now:* a lopsided hammer-armed shape that finishes
   itself on whatever it kills. *Broken, it falls to unworked scrap and
   drains down.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Temper — spell.** Heats or hardens metal at a touch — mends a blade
  mid-fight or seizes an enemy's; the smiths' first art · learned at
  Dwald's banked ember · *learn it to relight the Great Chimney's hearth.*
- **Hammerhome steel — metal.** The commons' finest weld-steel, the
  standard every good blade in the tower is measured against · sunk in the
  Slack-Tub Undercroft · *raise the drowned work to claim it.*
- **A mastersmith's hammer — tool.** One of the great named hammers left
  hanging when the fire died, still true and still ringing its owner's
  note · on its anvil in the Hundred Anvils · *match a climber to the
  hammer whose tune they can quiet, and it is theirs.*
- **Smith's draught — brew.** A hammerman's tonic of strength and stamina;
  a swallow puts a giant's weight behind a climber's swing.

## The Warden — Anvilback
A bellows hound of the commons, welded onto an anvil-and-hammer frame so its
back is a striking-face and its forelimbs are hammers — it fights on the
ringing chorus, each blow tuned to the phantom anvils, and mends its own
plate at any hot slag-heap. Break it and the ringing stops; the hearth can be
lit; the stair-lift swings open on a forge that could arm a war.

## When it falls
Dwald lights the Great Chimney and the shared hearth roars for the first time
since the Theft; the anvils ring to real hammers again. The smiths climb as
the host's armorers, carrying **Temper** and Hammerhome steel — the folk who
will forge the rising's weapons for every floor still above.
