# Floor 67 — The Coolant Falls (Giants)

> *"The forges' cold outflow poured off this cliff for a thousand years and never froze. The day the forges died, it froze mid-fall — a whole white river, stopped in the air." — Falls-warden Bruna Milkwater*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — its coolant outfall · **Gate-town:** Milkwater · **Warden:** Whitefall

## The land
A colossal frozen waterfall — the deep-forge's coolant outflow, once a
thundering milk-white river pouring off a mile-high cliff, now flash-frozen
into a cataract of pale ice caught in the very act of falling, with caverns
and ledges behind the frozen curtain. The light through the ice is a soft
milky glow; the cold is absolute; martens run the frozen spray-ledges. It was
a place the falls-folk held half sacred, and pilgrims came to the white water.
Now it is a cliff of stopped river, climbed behind and through the frozen
fall. The stair-lift is set in the cliff behind the frozen curtain, low where
the fall once pooled; Whitefall calves the ice before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** frost-birch on the spray-ledges, and the ancient falls-
  cedar at the head.
- **Ground & water plants:** milk-ice lichen glazing the frozen curtain,
  spray-moss on the ledges, and the pale falls-fern in the cataract-caverns.
- **Under the Yoke:** the spray-moss re-freezes to glaze the handholds; the
  milk-ice lichen weakens the frozen curtain to calve blocks; the falls-fern
  breathes a numbing cold-fog.

## Places of interest
- **The Cataract Cave.** The great hollow behind the frozen fall where the
  falls-folk shelter in the soft milk-light, the only warm dark on the cliff.
  *Seed: the coolant-source controls are here — reopened, the falls could roar
  and flow again and free what the freeze holds.*
- **The Falls-Head.** The top of the frozen cataract where the white river
  stopped mid-pour, the whole cliff readable from its brink. *Seed: from here
  the frozen river can be thawed from the top down, safely, without calving
  the whole curtain at once.*
- **The Pilgrims' Stair.** The ice-cut pilgrim way up the fall-face, where the
  folk who came to the sacred white water froze mid-climb and still stand in
  the curtain, clear and pale. *Seed: free the frozen pilgrims and their old
  offerings — and the shrine-lore lost with them — come back to light.*

## The people
Falls-folk — giant coolant-wardens and delver-spray-crews who tended the
outflow and guided the pilgrims, quiet reverent folk who read the falls by its
roar (now its silence). They shelter in the Cataract Cave in the milk-light,
keeping the source-shrine and mourning the stopped river. They love the roar of
the falls, a safe ledge, and a pilgrim seen through. They fear the calving ice
and the frozen pilgrims in the curtain.
**The keeper:** Bruna Milkwater, falls-warden — keeps the source-controls and
the pilgrim-lore, and listens for the day the river might roar again.

## The six
1. **Ice marten** (N) — *was:* a spray-ledge marten. *now:* frost-furred and
   bold, dropping onto climbers from the frozen curtain. *Cured, it flows
   into a spray-crack.*
2. **Glass-coat wolf** (N) — *was:* a falls-country wolf. *now:* ice-glazed,
   coursing the frozen ledges silently. *Cured, it shakes off the glaze and
   lopes into a cavern.*
3. **Falls-bat** (N) — *was:* a cataract-cave bat. *now:* massing in the
   milky dark, blundering the ledges. *Cured, it flits up behind the
   curtain.*
4. **Frost-char** (N) — *was:* a coolant-river fish frozen in the fall.
   *now:* thrashing free of the ice to flop and bite on the wet ledges.
   *Cured, it slips into a melt-pool, calm.*
5. **Falls troll** (N) — *was:* a Deep troll brought up from the under-caverns
   to labor in the coolant-caves and left behind when the forges died. *now:*
   ice-hided, calving blocks of the frozen curtain down onto climbers. *Cured,
   the slow giant lumbers off behind the fall toward the down-stair and the
   Deep it came from.*
6. **White pilgrim** (W) — *was:* a pilgrim who froze at the sacred falls,
   aether locked in the milk-ice. *now:* a pale figure that walks out of the
   frozen curtain to draw the living into the killing cold. *Broken (or
   thawed), it melts to still water and drains away.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Thaw — spell.** Melts ice and frees what's frozen in it — a path, a
  handhold, or a trapped soul; the falls-folk's art. *Seed: learn it to climb
  the frozen fall and free the frozen on every ice-floor.*
- **Milk-ice crystal — material.** The pale glowing ice-crystal of the falls,
  a soft-light lens and cold-forged blade-core; and the falls-silver beside
  it. *Seed: cut the Falls-Head curtain.*
- **Meltwater tonic — tonic.** A spray-crew's brew that revives the frozen and
  cold-struck; brings warmth and life back to a hypothermic climber. *Seed:
  drawn from the cavern's melt-pools.*
- **The pilgrim-lore — knowledge.** The source-shrine's lost rites for reading
  the falls and tending the white water, recovered by thawing the frozen
  pilgrims who carried them. *Seed: free the Pilgrims' Stair to win it back.*

## The Warden — Whitefall
A falls troll grown vast, welded into the frozen cataract so it wears the
white river as armor — it fights by calving great blocks of the frozen curtain
down onto climbers and freezing the spray to ice their holds. Break it and the
curtain stops calving; the frozen river can be thawed; the stair-lift opens
behind it.

## When it falls
Milkwater reopens the coolant-source and the white river thaws and *roars*
again for the first time in an age, giving up its frozen dead. The falls-folk
climb as the host's thaw-wrights and cold-rescuers, carrying **Thaw** and
milk-ice crystal — the folk who free the frozen and melt any barrier of ice
the tower raises on the way to the throne.
