# Floor 19 — The Fusion Vaults (Giants)

> *"We split the fire of the earth and kept it in jars. The jars are still warm. Don't ask what's grown around them." — Vault-Warden Hesk Half-Light*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — its aether-fuel vaults · **Gate-town:** Half-Light · **Warden:** Coreburn

## The land
The deep-forge's fuel-store: vaults of glowing aether-rods racked floor to
ceiling, each rod a captured sliver of the earth's fire, shedding a sick
half-light that never fully dims or brightens. The air is warm and faintly
wrong; shadows have hard edges and hum. This is where the giants kept the
power that fed every forge above — and the tower kept it lit, so the vaults
glow on in a perpetual, radiant dusk that has done strange things to
everything living in it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — racked rods and lead-shielded stone.
- **Ground & water plants:** glow-fungus (fat on stray radiance), lead-moss
  that shields against the rods, and the pale rod-lichen that creeps the
  racks.
- **Under the Yoke:** the glow-fungus has grown luminous and lures; the rod-
  lichen concentrates the rods' fire and burns at a touch.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Shielded Vault.** The deepest, lead-lined store where the
  hottest rods are racked and the half-light is fiercest. *Seed: one full-
  charge rod there could power a great work — or cook the careless.*
- **The peak — the Cooling Stack.** The tall vent-tower that bled the vaults'
  heat to the deep sky. *Seed: reopened, it could vent the fever-heat and
  cool the whole floor.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Core Gate.** The stair-lift is set behind
  a lead blast-door in the rock, its seams glowing. *Seed: Coreburn's heat
  keeps it sealed shut.*

## The people
Vault-crews — giants and delver-handlers trained to move the rods behind lead
and never linger, careful folk who count their years by exposure. They shelter
in Half-Light's shielded galleries, sickening slowly, guarding a power they
dare not use. They love a cold rod safely racked, a thick lead wall, and a
shift survived. They fear the glow-sickness and the things it grows.
**The keeper:** Hesk Half-Light, vault-warden — lead-aproned and grey-faced,
counts the crews' exposures and his own.

## The six
1. **Luminous cave-spider** (N) — *was:* a vault-corner spider. *now:* glowing
   from within, weaving hot-wire webs across the racks. *Cured, its light
   fades and it slips into shadow.*
2. **Glow-newt** (N) — *was:* a damp-wall newt. *now:* radiant and swollen,
   leaving burning trails on the stone. *Cured, it cools and clings still.*
3. **Rod-moth** (N) — *was:* a warmth-seeking moth. *now:* alight from within,
   swarming the rods and any lamp. *Cured, it drifts off, dim.*
4. **Glow-sick kobold** (P) — *was:* a first-taken conscript set to handle
   bare rods without shielding. *now:* collared, luminous, dying and
   dangerous, flinging hot shot. *A real death, and a pitiful one — freeing
   the collar is all the mercy left.*
5. **Rod-wisp** (W) — *was:* nothing — aether bled from a cracked rod. *now:* a
   drifting mote of core-fire that seeks and clings and burns. *Broken, it
   guts and drains into the shielding.*
6. **Forge remnant** (W) — *was:* a forge-crew fused to a rod-rack in a
   breach, aether caught in the slag. *now:* a half-melted figure of glowing
   iron that walks the vaults radiating heat. *Broken, it slumps to cooling
   slag and drains down.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Ward-cold — spell.** Sheds heat and radiance off the caster for a spell;
  scored inside the lead vault. *Seed: learn it to reach the Shielded Vault
  and the full-charge rod safely.*
- **Core-rod — material.** A captured sliver of earth-fire; the single richest
  power-source in the tower, and the most dangerous to carry. *Seed: the whole
  floor is a heist to claim one intact.*
- **Lead-draught — potion.** A crew's tonic against glow-sickness; wards the
  body from aether-burn for a spell — vital for the reactor floors near the
  top.

## The Warden — Coreburn
A forge-remnant grown vast, welded around a cracked core-rod so it burns from
the inside — every blow it lands scorches, and it radiates a heat that blisters
at range. It seals the Core Gate with its own fire. Break it and the core-rod
can be drawn and shielded; the vaults' heat drops; the Core Gate cools and
opens on the last floor before the warcamp.

## When it falls
Half-Light vents the Cooling Stack and the sick glow steadies; the crews
shield the loose rods at last. They climb as the host's power-handlers,
carrying **Ward-cold**, lead-draughts, and — if the heist went well — a core-
rod to light the rising's greatest works against the manufactured floors above.
