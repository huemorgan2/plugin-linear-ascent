# Floor 70 — The Tempest Court (Men) · Warden

> *"She was our sky-queen, and a good one — she read weather like scripture and never lost a flyer. The tower crowned her in a storm that never ends, and now the wind is her law and her prison both." — Court-steward Cole Galesend*

**Cut from:** the sky-realm of Men — the storm-queen's high court · **Gate-town:** Galesend · **Warden:** **Zephyra, the Storm Queen**

## The land
A sky-court built on the highest crag of the sky-realm — an open-air palace of
wind-worn towers and perch-balconies wreathed in a permanent, roaring tempest
of "unbottled gales," the storms the court once caught, stored, and loosed for
weather. The gales scream through the colonnades; lightning stitches the
clouds below; courtiers cling to their perches in the wind. It was the seat of
the sky-realm's storm-queen, taken and crowned into a tyrant of tempest, and
it is the last floor of the seventh tier — a whole court held captive in an
endless hurricane.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — wind-scoured stone and screaming air; one
  storm-cypress bent flat on the highest perch.
- **Ground & water plants:** gale-lichen scoured into the stone, perch-moss in
  the wind-shadows, and the pale storm-orchid that blooms only in the eye.
- **Under the Yoke:** the perch-moss peels loose to fling as debris; the gale-
  lichen shrieks a wind that unbalances; the storm-orchid's scent draws the
  unbottled gales to a warm body.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Windward Undercroft.** The storm-cellar beneath the court,
  where the gales were bottled in storm-glass and kept. *Seed: the queen's
  own storm-crown — and the bottling-lore to unmake it — are cellared here.*
- **The peak — the Storm Throne.** Zephyra's high sky-dais in the tempest's
  eye; the whole sky-realm reads from it. *Seed: the fight ends here; the eye
  is the one still place in the gale.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Tempest Gate.** The stair-lift is set in
  the crag behind a wall of screaming wind. *Seed: only Zephyra's freeing
  stills the gale enough to enter — the gate to the eighth tier.*

## The people
Court-captives of Galesend — the sky-realm's courtiers, weather-wrights, and
perch-squires, held in the tempest as Zephyra's unwilling court, bowing at
the wind's command. They shelter in the wind-shadows, tying themselves to the
stone, keeping the bottling-lore alive. They love a read sky, a bottled gale,
a calm eye, and the queen they remember. They fear the unbottled gales and
what their good queen has been made into.
**The keeper:** Cole Galesend, court-steward — keeps the bottling-lore and the
way to the storm-crown, and waits for a climber who can reach the eye.

## The six
1. **Champion drake** (N) — *was:* the court's storm-riding champions. *now:*
   the queen's fever-driven air-guard, strafing the perches. *Cured, it
   glides down out of the gale, calm.*
2. **Court-guard harpy** (N) — *was:* the court's raptor-sentries. *now:*
   fever-drawn half-human, riding the gale to fling climbers off the perches.
   *Cured, it folds to a plain eagle and settles.*
3. **Perch squire** (P) — *was:* a court page, first-taken and collared to
   serve the storm-queen. *now:* compelled to guard the perches and spring
   the wind-traps. *Pressed — a real death unless the collar is broken; the
   freed squires know the court's every hidden stair.*
4. **Storm-hawk** (N) — *was:* a court messenger-hawk. *now:* massing in the
   gale, mobbing anything crossing the balconies. *Cured, the flock wheels off
   down the wind.*
5. **Unbottled gale** (W) — *was:* nothing — a stored storm loosed from a
   shattered storm-glass, aether-quickened. *now:* a walking wind in a
   half-seen shape that tears climbers off their footing and hurls debris.
   *Broken (or re-bottled), it disperses to still air and drains away.*
6. **Gale-wraith** (W) — *was:* a weather-wright torn from a perch in the
   tempest, aether in the screaming wind. *now:* a wind-shredded figure that
   rides the gale, cutting tie-lines and crying false wind-warnings. *Broken,
   it's scattered on the wind and drains down.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Stillstorm — spell.** Calms and bottles a gale, opening a moment's eye of
  quiet in any tempest; the court's founding art, cellared in the Undercroft.
  *Seed: learn it to cross the Tempest Court and to still the storm-works near
  the throne.*
- **Storm-glass — material.** The gale-bottling crystal of the court, holding
  a caught storm like lightning in a jar — the material of wind-weapons and
  the queen's own crown. *Seed: the Windward Undercroft.*
- **Eye-of-storm draught — potion.** A weather-wright's brew that wraps a
  climber in a calm the wind can't touch; wards against gale, buffet, and the
  fear they carry.

## The Warden — Zephyra, the Storm Queen
The sky-realm's queen — first-taken, and crowned by the Yoke into a tyrant of
tempest, wreathed in the unbottled gales she once kept for her people. Like
Skarn and Hrimgar below her, she is a good ruler made a warden and turned
against her own; and like them, her ending is a *freeing,* not a killing. She
fights from the eye of the storm, hurling gales, loosing her air-guard, and
riding the wind out of reach. Reach the Storm Throne and break the storm-crown
(with Stillstorm) and the borrowed tempest disperses — the queen wakes to
herself, the gales fall to a breeze, and the Tempest Gate opens on the eighth
tier of stolen realms.

## When it falls
Zephyra throws down the storm-crown and the endless tempest calms to clear
sky; her freed court comes down off the perches into still air. This is the
third ruler won back on the climb — after Skarn and Hrimgar — proof that even a
crowned tyrant is a captive underneath. The sky-folk climb as the rising's
storm-fleet: weather-wrights and drake-riders carrying **Stillstorm** and
storm-glass. The seventh tier is freed; the eighth opens above — the last of
the stolen realms before the tower's own Making begins.
