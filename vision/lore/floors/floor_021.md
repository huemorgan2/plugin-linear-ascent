# Floor 21 — The Burnt Common (Men)

> *"The fire went through the year they took us. Green's come back through the black. That's the only good news on this floor." — Goodwife Ash Ashfold*

**Cut from:** the common-grazing heath of Men · **Gate-town:** Ashfold · **Warden:** Greycinder

## The land
A wide common heath caught by wildfire the season it was stolen — a black
country of burnt gorse and standing char, with a haze of new green bracken
pushing up through the ash. The floodlights lie grey on the cinders. It is a
place mid-healing and stopped there: the char never washed away, the green
never grew past knee-high, the whole common held forever at the moment after
the burn. Underfoot the ash is soft and still faintly warm.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** char-standing birch and gorse-skeletons, and the one
  fire-oak that survived, scorched but leafing.
- **Ground & water plants:** new bracken, fireweed (rosebay), and the first
  green of heather returning through the black.
- **Under the Yoke:** the fireweed smoulders at the root and reseeds in
  little bursts of ember; the char-birch drops burning bark.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Cinder Delf.** A burnt-out badger sett under the fire-oak,
  its walls glazed by old heat. *Seed: the commoners hid the parish seed-
  store here to save it from the burn.*
- **The peak — the Burnt Tor.** The heath's one rise, crowned by the old
  fire-beacon the commoners lit for warning. *Seed: relit, it warns the
  freed floors of a fire the Warden could still spread.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Ash Gate.** The stair-lift is set in a
  fire-cracked bluff, its arch soot-black. *Seed: Greycinder beds in the warm
  ash before it.*

## The people
Commoners — Men of the shared heath: turf-cutters, bee-keepers, gorse-
gatherers, plain folk with common rights and no lord. They shelter in Ashfold
among the char, guarding the seed-store and the returning green. They love a
grazed common, a gorse-fire in its place, and honey off the heath. They fear
the ember-things and a second burn they couldn't outrun.
**The keeper:** Goodwife Ash Ashfold, common-reeve — keeps the seed-store and
reads the green's return like a calendar of hope.

## The six
1. **Cinder wolf** (N) — *was:* a heath wolf. *now:* ash-grey and ember-eyed,
   hunting the smoke-hazed black. *Cured, it shakes off cinder and lopes to
   the green edge.*
2. **Char-tusk boar** (N) — *was:* a common-grazing boar. *now:* hide baked to
   black plate, tusks glowing at the tips. *Cured, the char flakes off and a
   plain boar trots away.*
3. **Fire-beetle** (N) — *was:* an ash-burrowing beetle. *now:* carapace
   glowing, swarming warmth and igniting dry gorse. *Cured, it cools and
   burrows into cold ash.*
4. **Ash-hare** (N) — *was:* a heath hare. *now:* soot-white, bolting in
   puffs of ash straight underfoot. *Cured, it's a grey blur and gone.*
5. **Smoke-crow** (N) — *was:* a carrion crow of the burn. *now:* trailing
   smoke, mobbing in the haze, dropping embers. *Cured, the flock lifts,
   clean-feathered.*
6. **Ember shade** (W) — *was:* a commoner who died in the burn, aether caught
   in the smouldering root. *now:* a walking coal in a human shape that sets
   the fireweed alight where it passes. *Broken, it collapses to cold ash and
   drains down.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Quench — spell.** Snuffs fire in a circle and cools scorched ground; cut
  into the Cinder Delf's glazed wall. *Seed: learn it to cross the ember-heath
  and, later, to face the forge and reactor floors above.*
- **Cinder-iron — metal.** Fire-hardened bog-iron from the burn, tough and
  heat-proof. *Seed: dig the tor's old beacon-ironwork.*
- **Greenshoot tonic — potion.** Brewed from the new bracken; a healing
  draught that closes wounds like the green closing over the black.

## The Warden — Greycinder
A char-tusk boar of the common, welded under a shell of ember-iron that
glows hotter as it fights — it charges through the ash in a trail of fire,
setting the fireweed alight to hem a climber in. Break it and its fire dies;
the ember-things go cold; the Ash Gate opens, and the green is left to finish
what the burn began.

## When it falls
Ashfold plants the saved seed-store across the cooled black, and the common
greens for real. The commoners climb as the host's foragers and fire-wardens,
carrying **Quench** and greenshoot tonic — plain folk who know how to bring
life back to burned ground, which the tower's forge-floors will need badly.
