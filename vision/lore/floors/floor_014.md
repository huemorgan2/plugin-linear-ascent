# Floor 14 — The Gear Galleries (Giants)

> *"The belts still run. Nothing's on them. They just carry the dark from one end of the hall to the other." — Grib Sprocket*

**Cut from:** the Giants' deep-forge realm — its machine-halls · **Gate-town:** Sprocket Row · **Warden:** Gearhide

## The land
Machine-halls the size of towns: belt-drives and flywheels the height of
houses, all still turning on captured aether, driving nothing. The floor
shakes with the endless rumble of empty machinery; grease-smoke hangs under
the floodlights; belts run overhead carrying nothing from nowhere. The
giants built this to move the mountain's ore in rivers. Now it moves only
noise, and the noise never stops. The stair-lift is set in a rock face behind
a wall of meshing cogs that part only on the right cadence; Gearhide sets the
wrong one, and holds the mesh before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — steel and stone and turning brass.
- **Ground & water plants:** grease-mould in the drip-trays, oil-lichen on
  cold iron, and the cotton-fungus that packs itself into bearings.
- **Under the Yoke:** the cotton-fungus jams and un-jams the gears at random,
  and the oil-lichen sparks where the belts rub.

## Places of interest
- **The Flywheel Hall.** The great flywheel-tower storing the captured aether,
  a wheel the height of a keep spinning silver-fast and near-silent. *Seed:
  its stored charge could power the rising's own works — if it can be tapped
  without tearing the tower apart.*
- **The Grinding Pit.** A belt-fed arena of stacked gear-teeth where the
  garrison drives the pressed pit-fighters to brawl for the shift's sport, the
  floor of it dark with old blood. *Seed: free the pit and its fighters, and
  the camp's own enforcers may turn on their keepers.*
- **The Master Clutch.** The control-house sunk in the belt-undercroft, where
  one great lever throws every drive on the floor into gear or out of it.
  *Seed: reach it and you can stop — or free — every machine in the galleries
  at once.*

## The people
Machinists and belt-wrights — giants and delver-mechanics who ran the ore-
rivers, folk who love a true-running machine more than sleep. They live in
Sprocket Row, oiling machines that carry nothing because stopping feels like
dying. They love a smooth drive, a balanced wheel, and a shift that ends
with everything still turning. They fear the runaway gears and the press-
gangs from the warcamp above.
**The keeper:** Grib Sprocket, master machinist — keeps the great flywheel
balanced by ear, because an unbalanced wheel would tear the hall apart.

## The six
1. **Belt-runner rodent** (N) — *was:* a grease-eating rat. *now:* riding
   the belts in packs, dropping on warmth below. *Cured, it scurries off a
   belt-end.*
2. **Grease-moth** (N) — *was:* an oil-drinking moth. *now:* smoke-winged,
   swarming lamps and fouling eyes with grease. *Cured, it drifts to a drip-
   tray.*
3. **Flywheel bat** (N) — *was:* a roost-bat of the high halls. *now:*
   riding the wheel-wind, stooping fast as the rim. *Cured, it flaps up into
   the girders.*
4. **Gear-thief kobold** (P) — *no kin to giant-work.* Kobolds are warren-
   delvers from far below; this one was marched up in a scrap-gang and posted
   to the galleries to strip the drives for the small parts the tower hoards
   above. *was:* a first-taken conscript. *now:* collared and wrench-armed,
   stealing the very cogs it hides among, guarding a spoil-heap nobody will
   ever come to collect. *A real death; breaking the collar is the mercy, and
   a cornered scrap-hand may simply walk away.*
5. **Red Orc pit-fighter** (P) — *sent down, not born here.* No orc belongs in
   a giants' gear-hall; these were driven down from Skarn's warcamp on the
   tier's last floor to garrison the works and cow the conscript gangs, made
   to brawl in the Grinding Pit for a purse no one pays. *now:* scarred and
   chained-fisted, fighting because the collar leaves no other move. *Pressed
   — killing costs; the deserters make grim allies.*
6. **The runaway** (W) — *was:* a loosed flywheel that killed a crew, aether
   caught in its spin. *now:* a rolling wheel of cold force that follows the
   belt-lines and flattens what it meets. *Broken, the spin earths out and
   it drains into the undercroft.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Freewheel — spell.** Frees a jammed mechanism — or seizes a running one;
  the machinists' trick · etched on the Master Clutch's lever · *Seed: learn
  it to throw the clutch and still the halls.*
- **Geartooth steel — metal.** The hardened tooth-steel of the drives, forged
  into weapons that bite and hold · stripped from a dead gear-train · *Seed:
  arm the host with edges that never dull.*
- **Tapped flywheel-charge — power source.** Stored aether drawn safely off
  the great wheel into a carry-cell, portable power for the rising's works ·
  drawn at the Flywheel Hall · *Seed: tap the wheel without letting it tear
  the hall down.*

## The Warden — Gearhide
A pit-fighter's fury welded onto a frame of meshing cogs and belt-drive — it
fights *on rhythm*, blows falling on the cadence of the hall, and it can seize
a climber's own gear mid-swing. It sets the stair-lift's cogs to the wrong
cadence to keep it shut. Break its rhythm and Gearhide grinds to a halt; the
cogs fall into true; the stair-lift meshes open.

## When it falls
Grib's crews throw the master clutch and the endless rumble *stops* — the
first silence in the halls since the Theft — then set the machines to real
work. The machinists climb as the host's engineers, carrying **Freewheel**
and tapped flywheel-charge to break the forge-works and war-engines of the
floors above.
