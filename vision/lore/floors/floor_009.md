# Floor 9 — The Beacon Field (Men)

> *"The beacons were how we called each other across the dark. Now they call the moths." — Wick Harlow*

**Cut from:** the signal-heaths of Men · **Gate-town:** Pylon Rest · **Warden:** Glarefang

## The land
A night signal-heath — a moor of humming aether-pylons that the sky-sailing
Men once lit to guide harbor-craft and call the fairs. The pylons still hum
and flicker, casting long knife-edged shadows that slide across the heather,
and the floodlights above war with them until the whole field strobes. It is
a floor of false signals: every light says *come here*, and none of them means
safety. The last honest beacon is dark. The stair-lift is cut into a granite
tor ringed by pylons, its arch inlaid with beacon-code; Glarefang hunts the
strobing shadows before it.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** wind-bent rowan and gorse in the pylon-lee.
- **Ground & water plants:** ling heather, bog-cotton, and the glow-lichen
  that crept up the pylons for the warmth.
- **Under the Yoke:** the glow-lichen pulses in false code, and the gorse
  has gone to spines that catch the strobing light like teeth.

## Places of interest
- **The Cable Vault.** A buried junction-chamber where the pylons'
  aether-cables meet, still live and sparking in the dark. *Seed: from here
  every pylon on the floor could be silenced — or turned true.*
- **The Old Beacon.** The tallest pylon, the original beacon-mast, now dark.
  *Seed: relight it true and the sky-harbors above would see it burn.*
- **The Grounded Skiff.** A small sky-sailing harbor-craft that read a false
  beacon and came down in the heather, hull split, crew long gone. *Seed:
  its light-charts are still aboard, and they map the whole signal-net.*

## The people
Beacon-keepers and pylon-wrights — Men of the signal-line, who lived by
reading light across distance and kept the network burning through every
night. Now they hide in Pylon Rest with the shutters shut against the
strobe, half-blind and jumping at shadows. They love a clear signal, a
still night, and a message that arrives. They fear the false lights, and
the shadow-line wolves that hunt inside the flicker.
**The keeper:** Wick Harlow, chief keeper — half-deaf from the pylon-hum,
reads the field by the pattern of its false flashes.

## The six
1. **Glare moth** (N) — *was:* a heath-moth drawn to the beacons. *now:*
   plate-sized, blinding-pale, it swarms the eyes and smothers lamps.
   *Cured, it drifts off toward the true dark.*
2. **Night-hawk** (N) — *was:* a moth-hunting nightjar. *now:* over-large,
   stooping silent out of the strobe. *Cured, it flits back into the gorse.*
3. **Shadow-line wolf** (N) — *was:* a heath wolf. *now:* it hunts *inside*
   the pylon-shadows, unseen until it crosses a light. *Cured, it steps into
   the open, ordinary, and lopes away.*
4. **Bog-cotton hare** (N) — *was:* a moor hare. *now:* strobe-maddened,
   bolting in blind zigzags. *Cured, it stills and is gone.*
5. **Pylon adder** (N) — *was:* a warmth-seeking adder that basked on the
   cables. *now:* charged and fever-bold, striking from the cable-runs.
   *Cured, it uncoils and slips away.*
6. **Flicker-wight** (W) — *was:* a keeper electrocuted at a junction, aether
   caught in the arc. *now:* a stuttering man-shape that appears only in the
   dark between flashes. *Broken, the arc earths out and it drains down the
   cables.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Truelight — spell.** Cuts a steady beam through strobe and glare that
  false lights can't fake; scored into the Cable Vault. *Seed: learn it to
  cross the strobing heath and reach the Old Beacon.*
- **Beacon-copper — metal.** The pure signal-copper of the cables, prized for
  aether-work. *Seed: strip a dead pylon-run.*
- **The signal-cipher — knowledge.** The beacon-keepers' code for reading and
  sending true light across the dark, held by Wick Harlow and half in the
  Grounded Skiff's charts. *Seed: learn it to signal the freed floors below
  and read the tower's false beacons above.*

## The Warden — Glarefang
A shadow-line wolf of the heath, welded with a mane of pylon-lamps that
strobe blinding-bright — it hunts in its own flicker, seen only in the
dark between its own flashes. It rushes when the field goes black. Break it
and the false beacons die; the Old Beacon can be lit true; the stair-lift
opens.

## When it falls
The pylons are silenced or turned honest; Pylon Rest opens its shutters to a
steady dark; the Old Beacon burns true and, for the first time since the
Theft, the freed floors below can be signalled at once. The keepers climb as
the host's signalers — the eyes that will read the tower floor to floor.
