# Floor 77 — The Egg Galleries (Deep)

> *"The warrens spin the silk. The galleries grow the spinners. Rank on rank of sacs, all glowing, all waiting to open. We count them so we know how bad it's getting." — Watcher Wend Palewatch*

**Cut from:** the shared under-caverns — the Deep, its spider-hatcheries · **Gate-town:** Palewatch · **Warden:** Husklight

## The land
The hatchery-galleries of the Deep spiders — vast pale caverns racked with
glowing egg-sacs in their tens of thousands, each a soft lantern of quickening
life, so the whole gallery glows a sick pale gold and hums with the stir of
things about to hatch. This is where the brood that fills the warrens and
vaults is grown, tended by sentinel-spiders and warm with incubating life. The
fever has quickened the hatch to a flood, and sacs burst constantly, spilling
broods into the dark. It is a floor lit by the light of its own dreadful
fertility.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — sac-racks and silk-vaulting.
- **Ground & water plants:** sac-lichen glowing on the racks, incubator-moss
  in the warm hollows, and the pale husk-fungus on spent sacs.
- **Under the Yoke:** the incubator-moss quickens the sacs to burst early; the
  husk-fungus lures with warmth-scent; the sac-lichen's glow hides how many
  are about to open.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Incubatory.** The deepest, warmest hatch-chamber, where the
  mother-sacs are tended. *Seed: chilling it (or holding it in stasis) halts
  the whole floor's hatch — the source of the brood-flood above.*
- **The peak — the Sac-Spire.** The tallest egg-cluster, a glowing spire to
  the vault; the galleries read from it. *Seed: from it the hatch-tempo can be
  read and slowed.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Hatch Gate.** The stair-lift is set behind
  a wall of glowing sacs. *Seed: Husklight guards the Incubatory before it.*

## The people
Watchers of Palewatch — Deep-folk who monitor the hatcheries and cull the
overflow, grim tallying folk whose whole task is counting how fast the brood
grows. They shelter among the cold racks, chilling sacs to slow the hatch.
They love a slowed hatch, a chilled gallery, and a tally that isn't rising.
They fear the bursting sacs and the sac-lights that walk.
**The keeper:** Wend Palewatch, chief watcher — keeps the hatch-tally and the
chilling-tools, and knows to the hour when a gallery will flood.

## The six
1. **Gallery-bat** (N) — *was:* a hatchery roost-bat. *now:* sac-glow-mad,
   swarming the racks and fouling the tally-lamps. *Cured, it flits off into a
   cold gallery.*
2. **Sentinel spider** (N) — *was:* the hatchery's sac-guards. *now:* fever-
   fierce, defending the racks and spitting sac-silk. *Cured, it returns to
   tending the sacs, ignoring the lamp.*
3. **Gallery-tick** (N) — *was:* a sac-parasite. *now:* fist-sized, dropping
   from the racks to burrow. *Cured, it drops off into the moss.*
4. **Brood-fed troll** (N) — *was:* a Deep troll that fed on the sacs. *now:*
   glow-bloated and strong, hurling burst sacs. *Cured, the slow giant
   lumbers off to a cold cavern.*
5. **Hatching sac** (W) — *was:* nothing — aether pooled in a mother-sac that
   died quickening. *now:* a great glowing sac that walks on the broodlings
   within, bursting to spill them on the living. *Broken (or stilled), it
   goes dark and drains into the rack.*
6. **Sac-light** (W) — *was:* a watcher who died among the sacs, aether in the
   glow. *now:* a drifting pale light that lures the living deep into the
   quickening racks. *Broken, the glow guts and drains away.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Stasis — spell.** Halts a thing's motion and growth for a spell — freezes
  a hatch, a swarm, or a foe mid-strike; the watchers' hatch-halting art.
  *Seed: learn it to still the Incubatory and to freeze the tower's engines
  above.*
- **Sac-glass — material.** The glowing quickened crystal the sacs are grown
  in, a soft-light lens and a life-charged enchanting-stone. *Seed: harvest
  the Sac-Spire.*
- **Hatchling-tonic — potion.** A tonic of the sacs' quickening humor;
  restores a climber with the raw vitality of new life, closing wounds fast.

## The Warden — Husklight
A sentinel spider grown vast, welded around a cluster of mother-sacs so it
glows from within and births broods as it fights — it fills the galleries with
hatching swarms and lures with its own light. Break it and the glow dies; the
hatch halts across the floor; the Incubatory can be stilled and the Hatch Gate
reached.

## When it falls
Palewatch stills the Incubatory and the brood-flood stops at its source; the
galleries dim to a cold, countable quiet. The watchers climb as the host's
brood-cullers and stasis-wrights, carrying **Stasis** and sac-glass — the folk
who freeze the tower's swarms and engines dead on the Making-floors ahead.
