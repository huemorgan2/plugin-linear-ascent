# Floor 90 — The Herald's Gate (Made) · Warden

> *"Malgrim was a man once, they say, freed on some floor far below — and then re-taken, and welded, and promoted, until he loved the King who unmade him. He is what the tower wants us all to become. He is the one we couldn't save." — Muster-captive Dara Lastmuster*

**The Made:** Malgrim's muster-gate, the last great gate below the throne-
tiers · **Gate-town:** Lastmuster (a captive muster-yard) · **Warden:**
**Malgrim, Herald of the King**

## The land
The Herald's Gate of the Made — the vast drum-driven muster-gate where Vharuk's
army is assembled and sent out, a fortress-square of iron mustering-yards under
a colossal gatehouse, ranked with forming legions and thundering with the
muster-drums that never stop beating men into soldiers. Banners of the King
wall the yards; the drums drive the tempo of the whole Made; slave clerks tally
the endless conscript-rolls. It is the ninth tier's climax and the last floor
before the throne-tiers — the gate through which the tower's whole strength has
always poured.

## "Flora" — the Making's growth
- **Canopy:** the gatehouse's iron vault and the King's mustering-banners.
- **Ground growth:** muster-moss beaten into the yards, drum-lichen on the
  skins, and roll-fungus in the record-vaults.
- **How the Making twists it:** the drum-lichen sets the muster-drums beating
  on their own; the muster-moss deadens a deserter's footfall to betray it;
  the roll-fungus re-inks a struck name back onto the conscript-rolls.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Muster-Rolls Vault.** The record-crypt where every
  conscript in the tower is tallied. *Seed: strike the rolls here and the
  Made cannot lawfully re-muster its freed — the paperwork of tyranny
  undone.*
- **The peak — the Drum-Throne.** Malgrim's high drum-dais over the yards;
  the muster reads from it. *Seed: the fight ends here; silence the drum and
  the forming legions falter.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Herald's Gate.** The great muster-gate
  itself, the stair-lift beyond it. *Seed: only Malgrim's fall opens it — the
  gate to the throne-tiers, 91 through 100.*

## The people — who you are freeing
Muster-captives — the re-enslaved processed through the yards to be welded and
sent out, and the slave-clerks who tally them. They are the last great host of
the bound before the throne, and freeing them empties the King's muster. They
love a roll struck, a drum stilled, and a legion that never forms. They fear
Malgrim's tally and the welding-halls the gate feeds.
**The keeper:** Dara Lastmuster, muster-captain among the captives — keeps the
knack of striking the rolls and the grief of knowing Malgrim was once one of
them.

## The six
1. **Muster hound** (N) — *was:* a yard guard-dog. *now:* coursing the
   mustering-ranks, running down deserters. *Cured, it heels off the drum.*
2. **Gate-crow** (N) — *was:* a gatehouse crow. *now:* massing on the
   banners, crying the muster. *Cured, they lift off the gate.*
3. **Drummer imp** (P) — *was:* a captured soul collared to beat the muster-
   drum. *now:* an imp welded to a drum, beating men into soldiers. *Pressed —
   Break-collar frees it, and the drum stops.*
4. **The secretary** (P) — *was:* a captured clerk collared to keep the
   conscript-rolls. *now:* a quill-fisted functionary that re-musters the
   struck. *Pressed — freed, it strikes the rolls itself.*
5. **Gate-tender hulk** (P) — *was:* a great captured soul welded to work the
   muster-gate. *now:* a towering warder that bars and drives the ranks.
   *Pressed — the collar breaks; freed, it holds the gate for the rising.*
6. **The Marshal** (P) — *was:* Malgrim's second, a captured officer collared
   to marshal the yards. *now:* a hard armored figure ordering the legions.
   *Pressed — the last winnable officer before the Herald himself.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Disband — spell.** Dismisses and scatters a mustered force at a word — the
  capstone of the Made's freeing-arts, gathering Break-collar, Unseam,
  Countermand, and Recant into one command that empties an army. *Seed: learn
  it to disband the King's host at the throne.*
- **Muster-steel — material.** The gate's own ordnance-steel and herald-gold,
  reforged into the rising's command-gear and standards. *Seed: the Drum-
  Throne's hoard.*
- **Herald's-bane — potion.** A muster-captive's brew that deafens the drum's
  compulsion and steadies a soul against the call to fall in.

## The Warden — Malgrim, Herald of the King
Once a man, freed on some floor far below and then re-taken, welded, and
promoted through the Made until he came to *love* the King who unmade him —
Malgrim is the tower's masterpiece and its warning: the captive who became a
believer. He fights from the Drum-Throne, beating the muster to raise the yards
against the climber and hurling his marshaled legions. **The rising tries to
free him** — Break-collar, Recant, every mercy learned below — and Malgrim
*refuses,* clutching his chains as glory. He is the first captive who cannot be
saved, because he no longer wishes it; and so, grieving, the climb must put him
down as a *true enemy* — the King's willing voice silenced at last. The drum
stops; the muster falters; the Herald's Gate swings open on the throne-tiers.

## When it falls
Malgrim falls unfree, and it is the hardest victory of the climb — proof that
the King can make a captive love his cage, and that not every soul can be won
back. Lastmuster strikes the conscript-rolls; the drum goes silent; the King's
great muster empties. The freed muster-captives climb as the host's own
marshals and roll-breakers, carrying **Disband** — the word that will empty
the throne itself. The ninth tier is freed. Above lie the last ten floors: the
throne-tiers, and the Demon King at their summit.
