# Floor 43 — The Bone Garths (Men)

> *"Every beast that fed the realm came here at the end, for bonemeal and glue and char. The heaps never rot. Lately they never rest, either." — Bone-warden Sef Garthend*

**Cut from:** the rendering-country of Men — the walled bone-yards · **Gate-town:** Garthend · **Warden:** Garthbone

## The land
A country of walled bone-garths — great fenced yards where the realm's dead
beasts were rendered to bonemeal, glue, and char, the ground white with
splinter and the walls stacked house-high with clean bone. Picker-birds crowd
the yard-walls; the wind clatters through the stacks like dry rain. The fever
has soaked into the old bone, and the heaps have begun to shift and rise and
walk. It is a grim, useful, orderly place turned into a boneyard in the worst
sense — where the leavings of a thousand herds are standing up.

## Flora
- **Trees / canopy:** none — bone-stacks and yard-walls; one dead render-oak
  by the pit.
- **Ground & water plants:** bone-nettle in the splinter, glue-weed by the
  rendering-vats, and the pale char-moss on the burnt heaps.
- **Under the Yoke:** the bone-nettle grows needle-sharp; the glue-weed
  snares; the char-moss reeks a fear-stink that unnerves.

## Three places
- **The cave — the Rendering Pit.** The sunken vat-cellar beneath the garths
  where the bone was boiled and charred. *Seed: the bone-wardens' craft — the
  rite to lay a risen heap back down — is scratched on the vat-rim.*
- **The peak — the Great Stack.** The tallest bone-heap, an ossuary tower;
  the garths read from its top. *Seed: firing it in the old way sends the
  fear-stink over the whole floor and quiets the heaps.*
- **The gate in the mountain — the Garth Gate.** The stair-lift is set in a
  bone-walled scarp, its arch a rendered arch of horn. *Seed: Garthbone
  guards the pit before it.*

## The people
Bone-wardens — Men of the rendering-yards: knackers, char-burners, and glue-
boilers, unsqueamish folk who turn death into use and take pride in a clean
yard. They shelter in Garthend behind the garth-walls, laying back down the
heaps that rise. They love a clean-picked bone, a full char-kiln, and a heap
that lies still. They fear the walking stacks and the yard-ghouls in the
splinter.
**The keeper:** Sef Garthend, bone-warden — keeps the laying-rite and the
char-kilns, and reads a heap's mood by the way it clatters.

## The six
1. **Picker rat** (N) — *was:* a yard-scavenging rat. *now:* bone-gnawing and
   massed in the splinter. *Cured, it bolts into a heap-gap.*
2. **Garth-crow** (N) — *was:* a bone-yard crow. *now:* white-daubed and
   mobbing, roosting the stack-tops. *Cured, the flock lifts off the walls.*
3. **Bone-hound** (N) — *was:* a yard-guard dog. *now:* gaunt and rattling,
   coursing the garths in packs. *Cured, it drops its bone and heels.*
4. **Marrow-beetle** (N) — *was:* a bone-boring beetle. *now:* plate-sized,
   swarming out of hollow bone. *Cured, it burrows back into a stack.*
5. **Restless heap** (W) — *was:* nothing — aether pooled in a great pile of
   rendered bone. *now:* a shambling mound of splintered bone that reforms
   when scattered. *Broken (and laid by the rite), the pile falls still and
   drains into the ground.*
6. **Yard ghoul** (W) — *was:* a knacker who died in the rendering-pit,
   aether in the boiled bone. *now:* a bone-daubed feeder that drags the
   living to the vats. *Broken, it collapses into the pit and drains down.*

## Three finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Knit-bone — spell.** Mends shattered bone in a living body — or fuses
  loose bone into armor and bar; the wardens' craft, cut on the vat-rim.
  *Seed: learn it to lay the heaps and, above, to mend the host's broken and
  bar the tower's raised dead.*
- **Bonechar-steel — material.** Steel refined through the garths' bone-char,
  taking a black keen edge that bites the undead. *Seed: work the char-kilns.*
- **Marrow-tonic — potion.** A knacker's brew of rendered marrow; restores
  deep strength and knits fatigue out of the bones.

## The Warden — Garthbone
A bone-hound of the garths welded into a carapace of fused rendered bone — a
rattling giant that hurls bone-shards and reforms itself from any heap it
stands near. It fights among the stacks where its ammunition is endless.
Break it (and lay it with the rite) and the heaps go still across the floor;
the Garth Gate opens.

## When it falls
Garthend fires the Great Stack and the fear-stink settles the heaps for good;
the garths go quiet and clean. The bone-wardens climb as the host's bone-
menders and dead-layers, carrying **Knit-bone** and bonechar-steel — the folk
who set an army's broken bones and put down whatever the tower raises above.
