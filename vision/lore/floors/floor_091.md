# Floor 91 — The Obsidian Stair (Made)

> *"The stair is one long mirror of black glass, and it shows you climbing beside yourself. Don't look too long. Your reflection has been up there longer than you, and it's learned things." — Firststep captive Wend*

**The Made — the throne-tiers:** the King's inner ascent begins · **Gate-town:** Firststep (captives held on the landings) · **Warden:** Firstriser

## The land
The obsidian stair of the Made — the first floor of the throne-tiers, a
colossal grand staircase cut from a single sweep of black volcanic glass,
polished to a perfect mirror so that every climber ascends beside a flawless
dark reflection of themselves. The stair rises through a lightless hall, lit
only by the cold gleam off the glass. The reflections do not always copy; some
step out of the glass to climb ahead, or turn back down against you. It is the
threshold of the King's sanctum, and its first test is whether you can face
yourself. The stair-lift is set in the black glass at the First Landing atop
the stair; Firstriser bars the last treads before it.

## "Flora" — the Making's growth
- **Canopy:** none — mirror-glass and dark.
- **Ground growth:** glass-lichen in the stair-seams, reflection-moss on the
  treads, and the pale sheen-fungus that fruits where the glass has cracked.
- **How the Making twists it:** the reflection-moss deepens a reflection until
  it can step free; the glass-lichen shows a false step; the sheen-fungus
  lures the eye into the glass.

## Places of interest
- **The Understep.** The dark vault beneath the stair where the reflections
  are "sourced," on the silvered underside of the glass · *Seed: the mirror-
  lore — how to break a reflection free of its caster — is cut here.*
- **The Silvered Gallery.** A long mirror-walled turn of the stair where the
  reflections crowd thickest and step freely from the glass · *Seed: cross it
  without meeting your own eye and the copies never wake.*
- **The First Landing.** The top of the stair, the true threshold of the
  throne-tiers · *Seed: the fight ends here, where the last reflection must be
  faced.*

## The people — who you are freeing
Firststep captives — the innermost thralls, held on the stair's landings and
made to polish the glass that torments them, worn down by the reflections of
their own despair. They love a cracked mirror, a landing left un-polished, and
a reflection that stays put. They fear their own dark selves stepping out of
the glass.
**The keeper:** Wend of Firststep — keeps the mirror-lore and teaches the held
to look away before the glass learns their faces.

## The six
1. **Mirror moth** (N) — *was:* a glass-hall moth. *now:* dazzled and
   swarming its own reflections. *Cured, it drifts off into the dark.*
2. **Glass-rat** (N) — *was:* a stair rat. *now:* nesting in the seams,
   gnawing the silvering. *Cured, it bolts down a crack.*
3. **Obsidian sentinel** (W) — *was:* nothing — aether set in a carved glass
   guardian. *now:* a black-glass figure that steps off the balustrade to
   strike. *Broken, it shatters to dead glass.*
4. **Court page** (W) — *was:* a page who died on the stair, aether in his
   reflection. *now:* a grey figure in the glass that mimics a companion to
   mislead. *Broken, the reflection cracks and it drains away.*
5. **Stair-wight** (W) — *was:* a climber who fell on the glass, aether in the
   bloodied step. *now:* a figure that walks the treads to trip the living
   down the stair. *Broken, it slides into the Understep and drains down.*
6. **Your reflection** (W) — *was:* nothing — the climber's own image,
   aether-quickened by the stair. *now:* a flawless dark copy that fights with
   the climber's own skill and knows their every move. *Broken, the glass goes
   blank and the copy drains into the dark.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Mirrorbreak — spell.** Shatters reflections, glass-constructs, and mirror-
  tricks at a word · the captives' art, cut in the Understep · *Seed: break
  your reflection here, and the mirror-horrors of the tiers above.*
- **Obsidian glass — salvage.** The King's black mirror-glass, worked into
  blades that bite the unreal and shields that turn a reflection aside ·
  quarried from the stair · *Seed: arm the host against the throne-tiers'
  unliving.*
- **Selfward draught — brew.** A captive's draught against the stair's
  despair, warding the mind from doppelgängers and self-doubt · *Seed: face
  your own dark self and hold.*

## The Warden — Firstriser
Your own reflection grown vast and permanent — the first image the stair ever
freed, a dark perfect copy of the climber that has climbed and re-climbed the
glass for an age, learning every trick a hero learns. A construct of the
Making, it fights exactly as you do, only colder. Its end is an *eviction:*
Mirrorbreak it and break it, and the borrowed self drains back into the glass.
The stair-lift opens on the First Landing.

## When it falls
Wend's captives crack the great glass and the reflections go still; the climber
faces the last dark self and wins. This is the threshold-victory of the throne-
tiers — the proof that the climb can face what it fears most in itself. The
freed Firststep captives climb as the host's mirror-breakers, carrying
**Mirrorbreak** and obsidian glass into the King's hall of faces above.
