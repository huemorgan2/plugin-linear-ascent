# Floor 85 — The Imp Warrens (Made)

> *"Even a demon army has to sleep somewhere. This is the barracks-slum where the imps den between shifts — and where the press-gangs bring the next batch of us to be welded." — Warren-captive Wend Sootside*

**The Made:** the tower soldiery's barracks-warrens · **Gate-town:** Sootside (a captive warren-block) · **Warden:** Fluegrim

**Sky:** standard — three to five miles of soot-hung air over the tiers
**Veil:** thin — the warren's own smoke does the Warden's work
**Land:** Rimlands three miles — the soot-slum tiers; the deep middle holds
the deep cell-blocks
**Gates:** both doors at the rim, as written — the up-gate behind the iron
muster-gate on the top tier

## The land
The imp-warrens of the Made — a smoke-choked slum of iron cell-blocks and
flue-shafts stacked in reeking tiers, where the tower's off-duty imps and
hellknights den between garrison-shifts, gambling, brawling, and sharpening.
Soot hangs thick; flue-fires gutter in every cell; the din of a hundred
thousand soldiers at rest never quiets. The warren bends shallowly down
toward the tray's middle — soot-slum tiers stacked at the rim where the
smoke thins to a grey day, and the deep cell-blocks sunk at the dark center,
farther from the light the more the tower wants you kept. Press-gangs haul
fresh captives
through on their way to the welding-halls. It is the soldiery's home, and the
one floor where the Made's army can be met *out of order* — off-duty,
distracted, and, some of them, quietly sick of the King. The stair-lift stands
behind the block's iron muster-gate at the warren's top tier; Fluegrim keeps
order before it.

## "Flora" — the Making's growth
- **Canopy:** none — cell-tiers and flue-shafts.
- **Ground growth:** soot-moss on the flues, cell-lichen on the bars, and
  flue-fungus in the warm shafts.
- **How the Making twists it:** the flue-fungus fruits choking spores into
  the smoke-gloom; the soot-moss smothers a lamp; the cell-lichen greases
  the tier-ladders.

## Places of interest
- **The Deep Flue.** The soot-choked bottom of the warren's main flue-shaft,
  where the soot and the dead gather · *Seed: a network of the disaffected —
  imps and knights half-freed of the collar — meets in the smoke here.*
- **The Muster-Balcony.** The high assembly-tier where the shifts form up ·
  *Seed: call a false muster from it and empty a whole cell-block at once.*
- **The Cell-Tiers.** The stacked iron holding-blocks where fresh captives are
  penned on their way to the racks above · *Seed: unbar a tier and a hundred
  bound go free in a breath.*

## The people — who you are freeing
Warren-captives — the re-enslaved held in the deep cell-blocks at the
warren's dark middle, where the tower cells its captives farthest from the
rim's light, awaiting the welding-halls; and the warren's own drudge-
servants. They pass word, hide the half-freed, and wait. They love a shift
missed, a press-gang dodged, and a soldier who's started to remember. They
fear the muster-horn and the trip to the racks above. A stay-road leaves
this rim, and the disloyal know it — imps trading warren-gossip to the Wake
for word of the floors they were stolen from.
**The keeper:** Wend Sootside, warren-captive — runs the smoke-network and
knows which soldiers can still be reached with a whisper and a broken collar.

## The six
1. **Flue bat** (N) — *was:* a shaft roost-bat. *now:* soot-mad, fouling the
   cell-lamps. *Cured, it flits up a flue.*
2. **Warren vermin** (N) — *was:* barracks rats. *now:* massing in the
   middens. *Cured, they scatter into the tiers.*
3. **Soot-rat** (N) — *was:* a flue rat. *now:* fat on the warren's waste.
   *Cured, it bolts down a shaft.*
4. **Warren tough** (P) — *was:* a captured soul forged into a barracks-
   bruiser, collared. *now:* a brawling imp that fights for a cell and a
   ration. *Pressed — Break-collar frees it; off-duty, its collar is
   loosest here.*
5. **Press-gang imp** (P) — *was:* a captured soul made a slave-catcher,
   collared to it. *now:* a hook-fisted imp dragging captives to the racks.
   *Pressed — freed, it turns its hooks on the overseers.*
6. **Off-duty hellknight** (P) — *was:* a captured soul welded into plate,
   collared, resting between shifts. *now:* a knight caught out of armor and
   half-doubting. *Pressed — the most reachable knight on the climb: many
   will break their own collars if given the chance.*

## Finds  *(quest seeds)*
- **Passfor — spell.** Cloaks the caster as one of the Made's own, imp or
  knight, to pass unchallenged through the tower's ranks · the captives'
  survival-trick, taught in the Deep Flue · *Seed: walk the King's works unseen
  above.*
- **Warren-iron — salvage.** The rough cell-and-bar iron of the slum, cheap
  and everywhere · stripped from any block · *Seed: rank gear for the rising.*
- **A turned hellknight — companion.** An off-duty knight reached with a
  whisper and a broken collar wakes to who it was and joins · *Seed: the most
  reachable soldier on the climb, and an insider in the King's works.*

## The Warden — Fluegrim
A hellknight-champion, the warren's enforcer — a captured soul welded huge and
collared to keep the barracks in order. **He is Pressed, not a construct:**
under the plate is someone who was freed once, below, and re-taken. He fights
to hold his block — but his collar is old and cracked from a hundred half-
doubts. Break the collar (Break-collar, from floor 20) rather than the body,
and Fluegrim wakes, throws down the King's order, and turns his whole warren
to the rising. The stair-lift opens on his word.

## When it falls
Fluegrim's collar breaks and a barracks-slum of soldiers wakes to who they
were — the first great *defection* of the Made's own army to the rising. The
gloom unravels in a day with no one left minding it, and true light finds
the tiers through the thinning smoke; the warren learns what color it is.
The freed warren-folk and turned soldiers climb as the host's infiltrators and
insiders, carrying **Passfor** and the knowledge of the King's works — the
folk who walk the tower as its own and open it from within.
