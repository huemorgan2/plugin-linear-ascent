# 008-01 — Fast first fight

Floor-1 kills should take ~2–3 rounds (archer ~2), not 5–7.

## Steps

1. Fresh character (or existing floor-1 character), go to the gate town
   and pick **Hunt the wilds**.
2. Fight with **Attack** (or Treeline shot then Attack for an archer)
   until the kill. Count the action clicks from encounter card to
   victory card.
3. Repeat for 3 kills.

## Pass

- A common floor-1 animal dies in ≤ 3 player actions (≤ 2 for archer
  openers with Treeline shot) most of the time. A "tough"/"alpha"
  specimen may take more — that's fine and should be visibly labeled.
- The victory card pays gold and XP as before.
- HP lost per common kill is small (single digits for a fresh kit).

## Fail

- 5+ rounds on a common floor-1 animal, or unchanged monster HP (37).
