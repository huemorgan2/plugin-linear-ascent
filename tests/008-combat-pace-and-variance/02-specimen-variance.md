# 008-02 — Specimen variance

Some animals are runts (easy, poor), some are scarred (hard, richer),
rare alphas are dangerous and pay ~2×.

## Steps

1. Hunt the wilds ~10 times on floor 1–2.
2. Read every encounter card's prose and headline, and every victory
   card's gold line.

## Pass

- At least 2 different specimen flavors appear across 10 fights
  (gaunt/limping runt, scarred/heavy-set, or an alpha).
- The specimen is visible in the card (prose line or headline) BEFORE
  the player commits to fighting — running from an alpha must be an
  informed choice.
- Tough/alpha kills visibly pay more gold than runt kills on the same
  floor (compare victory cards).
- An alpha victory (if one appears) shows an extra "alpha spoils" loot
  line.

## Fail

- All 10 fights read identical (no specimen ever shown), or a runt
  pays more than a tough on the same floor consistently, or the
  specimen only becomes visible after the fight.
