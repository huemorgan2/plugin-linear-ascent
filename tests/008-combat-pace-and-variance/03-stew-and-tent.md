# 008-03 — Hunter's stew and the healer's tent

Cheap partial heal exists; full heal always costs less than one kill.

## Steps

1. Take some damage in the wilds, return to the gate town.
2. Verify two heal options: **Hunter's stew** (◈ 2 · +5 HP) and
   **The healer's tent** (◈ 2×floor, full).
3. Buy a stew — HP +5, gold −2. Buy another — repeatable.
4. Use the tent — HP to full, gold −2×floor.
5. Visit the Lodge in Roothollow — stew is offered there too, and the
   room copy mentions the night mends you (+20 HP at dawn).

## Pass

- Both options appear only when hurt (tent) / stew when hurt too.
- Meters update exactly (+5 HP per stew, capped at max; full for tent).
- Tent price (2×floor) is below one kill's average gold (8×floor+).
- Lodge copy mentions the +20 HP night heal; sleeping still buys PvP
  protection as before.

## Fail

- No stew option, wrong prices, stew heals past max HP, or lodge makes
  no mention of healing.
