# 008-04 — Wardens keep their weight

The fast-kill budget applies to wilds animals only. A warden must still
be a real boss fight (~12+ rounds), numerically identical to pre-008.

## Steps

1. On floor 1–2, enter **The Warden's keep** (3 ⚡).
2. Read the opener card's ATK/DEF (and HP once revealed in later
   rounds); fight a few rounds.

## Pass

- Warden stats match the pre-008 baseline (floor 1: ATK 14 / DEF 3 /
  HP 70; floor 5: 28/15/162 — spot-check whichever floor is played).
- The fight clearly takes many more rounds than a wilds kill.
- No specimen tag ever appears on a warden.

## Fail

- Warden HP shrank with the wilds retune (e.g. floor-1 warden dying in
  2–3 rounds), or a warden card reads "gaunt"/"scarred"/"alpha".
