# Dojo — 017 phase 003: the enemy [i] card

The real gate: a new player must answer "why is this fight bad for me"
from the card alone, in one glance. Local Luna (8765) + local worldd
(8600), tenant qa007. Class/encounter swaps via the DB technique from
the 001/002 retros (`doc.clazz`, `doc.gear.weapon`, inject
`doc.encounter`; fields are `clazz` and `gear.weapon`, NOT
`class`/`weapon`).

## A — armored (kings_guard, floor 10, as sorcerer)

Open the [i] badge. PASS when: dossier shows the plate shield icon with
"plate — Medium" and says spellwork ignores it; the always-on HP bar
sits under the headline from round 1; lore line renders in italics.
Screenshot.

## B — flying (glare_moth, floor 4, as warrior)

the fight note agrees ("cuts empty air"); the modifier row matches the
range state. Screenshot.

run-down warning; the at-range chip shows "its blows land at HALF";
once it closes, the mod chip flips to "your bow works at ×0.6 — open
distance to shoot full". Screenshot.

## E — glance test

With the [i] card open on any counter-monster, ask: can you name (1)
which of your damage types is wrong here, (2) whether you can outrun
it, (3) how much meat is left — without reading any combat prose?
