# 001 — baseline run (0.59.0 economy, sim v1)

Run: `python3 tools/sim046.py --max-floor 100 --tag baseline`,
personas optimal/casual/skinflint, seed 1. Runtime ~2 min. Raw:
`runs/baseline-*.jsonl`.

## What the current game measures as

- **Total climb: optimal 253 days, casual 564 days.** The pace curve
  is NOISY-FLAT: floor 60 costs 1 day while floor 50 costs 4; floor 90
  spikes to 6 (optimal) / 15 (casual). Roy's complaint is confirmed in
  the calendar, not just the stats: nothing grows steadily — deep
  floors are sometimes CHEAPER than mid floors.
- **Floor 2 is the first cliff, today**: 4.1 days optimal, 19.1 days
  casual — worse than any floor until the 80s. Cause: LEVELUP_BASE_GOLD
  (◈200) + xp_need at 1/3 energy vs floor-2 income. Pre-046 issue;
  goes on issues.md.
- **Tier-9/10 price wall for casual**: floors 80/90 cost 13–15 days
  (quadratic set prices vs ×1.2/band income). This is exactly the seam
  046 replaces with the two-exponent law.
- **The gear-starvation wall is real and sits at ~floor 17**:
  skinflint (never buys) hits level 30 by floor 17 and then NEVER
  reaches 75% win vs floor 18 — max level + tier-1 gear is a permanent
  dead end. Design-intended (steel carries the climb), but now the
  wall's location is measured. Gate G1's job under 046: the ESCAPE
  must always be affordable, and the wall must never be reachable by a
  player who is spending normally.
- **Win% erosion at depth (75–84% by floor 100)** for all personas —
  partly sim artifact: the buy policy is cheapest-step-first, which
  over-buys cheap hones and lags weapon rungs (rung 9.0 at floor 100
  vs reference 10). Fix before using win%% as a gate.

## Sim v1 limitations to fix in v2

1. Buy priority: cheapest-first → power-per-gold with weapon bias
   (matches how real players buy).
2. skinflint rule ("buy only under 60% win") is artificial — a player
   with 3650 days of banked income would buy everything at once.
   Redefine: buys only after 3 stuck days, then all at once (tests
   recovery, not starvation-by-stubbornness).
3. Floors > 30 warden gates counted as flat 1 day (coordination is
   scheduling, not pace) — fine for pace gates, blind to siege feel.
4. Deaths/mend ignored; single seed; specimen/profile gold premiums
   ignored (income slightly understated).

## Carry into the 046 implementation

- Recurring per-fight costs (healer tent) must ride the INCOME
  exponent, not the pillar — otherwise net income goes negative at
  depth (1.3 outgrows 1.25). Rule: running costs ride income;
  capital costs (gear, training) ride the pillar. Added to plan
  mentally; encode in economy.py.
- Hone must become multiplicative (×1.3 per step on the piece) — the
  additive HONE_WEIGHT can never produce within-band exponential
  growth; with lag 2 it reproduces the reference exactly.
- XP sync: xp_per_kill must be `2.4 · bar^1.5 / PACE^(bar−1)` (from
  xp_need = 24·L^1.5 and days(L) = d0·PACE^(L−1)) — a plain
  income-shaped xp curve would blow past level≈floor by floor 10.
