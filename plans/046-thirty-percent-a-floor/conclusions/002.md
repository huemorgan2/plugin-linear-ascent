  climb, as designed.

## The difficulty is now real

At the exact reference loadout: win 80–96% on your own floor, **0% one
floor up** (floors ≥ 5). One floor is a ×1.3 wall. Advancement requires
buying above reference (the 2-hone REFERENCE_HONE_LAG gap) — which is why
the sim shows 100% win at the moment it advances. Not a bug; it is the
out-gear-then-climb loop working.

## Significance gate — passes at all probes

- One hone step = +18–30% ATK at every probe floor (5→95).
- Hone cost in days grows 0.17 → 6.0 (floor 5 → 95): capital/income =
  pace_wedge, exactly the climb-time law. Night job stays 20% of a day's
  kill income at every floor (both ride the income pillar).

## Cliff gate — sawtooth, seams, and the floor-2 cliff

- The day-series alternates 1.0 / N ("gate-only" floors where readiness
  carried over, then a grind floor). Artifact of buy-ahead; amortized
  per-decade the curve is smooth. Sim v3 could smooth by reporting
  2-floor rolling sums.
- Decade seams (60, 70, 80, 90) run ×2.2–2.6 vs the prior floor: the new
  tier's capital price lands on the decade-start floor. Real, but bounded;
  acceptable — a "new decade, new steel" moment.
- **Floor 2 cliff persists** (optimal 4.2d, casual 20d vs ~0.3d design
  line). Cause unchanged from 001: LEVELUP_BASE_GOLD floor of ◈200 ≈ one
  full day of floor-1 income per level. Filed in issues.md — touching it
  changes new-player feel, Roy's call.
