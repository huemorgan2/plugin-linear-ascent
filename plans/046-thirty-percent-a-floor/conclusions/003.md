# 003 — 047 fast start (0.61.0): the first five floors advance faster

Roy's directive after playing 0.60.0 locally: the first levels should
advance faster; weapons a bit cheaper. Scope: the first 5 floors.

## What changed

1. **Training tutorial ramp** (`_train_soft`): the level fee rides
   ×0.25 → ×1.0 linearly across levels 1–8 (full law from 8 up).
   8, not 5, because the climber trains ~2–3 levels ahead of the floor —
   the fees paid ON floors 1–5 are levels 1–8's. `LEVELUP_BASE_GOLD`
   200 → 60. Fees: 60, 90, 140, 240, 380, 540, 840, then the one-day
   law untouched (1180, 1560, 2040, …).
2. **Early weapon discount** (`_EARLY_WEAPON_DISCOUNT = 0.8`,
   `_EARLY_WEAPON_FADE_RUNGS = 5`): the tier-1 weapon sticker opens 20%
   cheaper (250 → 200, all three class lines); sub-rungs fade the
   discount linearly to nothing by rung 1.5 (floor 5): 280, 370, 510,
   680, then 930 on — byte-identical to 046 from iron_sword up.

- The floor-2 cliff (issues.md #2) is resolved by the ramp: the ◈200
  fee floor was one full day of floor-1 income per level; ◈60 is a
  fifth of one.
- Floors 6–10 deliberately unchanged (Roy scoped the change to 5);
  they are XP-bound anyway (~900 xp/level vs ~1300 xp/day).
- Full-tower totals, the skinflint wall (27), win rates, and every
  warden number are untouched — no stat moved, only two prices.
