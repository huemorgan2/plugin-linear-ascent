# 049 — monster image remake

## Goal

Remake all creature images in a new style: **black-and-white
semi-realistic 3D render, dark arcade-stage look** — wide horizontal
landscape filled edge to edge with detail, dark and moody, the
creature standing in full side view, rim-lit against the backdrop —
replacing the current 1-bit Bayer-dither pipeline output in
`content/art/creatures/`.

## Scope

**525 images**: every encounter and every warden across the 100 shipped
floors. The full roster — each creature with its description, size
(from the body archetype trait), its floor's landscape, and the
original smaller animal it was before the fever (natives), or a note
that it is made/pressed and not an infected animal — is in
[MONSTERS.md](MONSTERS.md).

Origin key (world-lore.md §5):
- **Native** — the land's own animal grown wrong under the fever; the
  roster names the original smaller animal (`was:` in the floor YAML).
- **Pressed** — a conscripted person (goblins, kobolds, orcs, imps);
  not an infected animal.
- **Wrongmade** — manufactured by the tower (all wardens, the leaks);
  made, not infected.
- Floors 11–100 predate the 038 kind-tagging: marked *untagged*,
  defaulting to Native per world-lore §9, original animal not recorded.

## Style spec

- Black-and-white 3D render, semi-realistic, greyscale only.
- Dark arcade fighting-game stage look: wide horizontal landscape
  backdrop, moody, filled completely with detail edge to edge — no
  empty space.
- One creature standing in full side view (profile), feet on the
  ground, large in frame, sharp silhouette under strong dramatic rim
  light; high contrast, cinematic lighting, realistic textures.
- No color, text, borders, or watermarks.

## Pipeline

Same skeleton as `tools/generate_creatures.py` (prompts assembled from
floor YAML `name` + `prose` + floor landscape; nano-banana-pro via
`plugin_image_gen.providers`, key `LUNA_GEMINI_API_KEY` from
`luna/.env`), but the pencil style replaces the 1-bit STYLE preamble.
Open question before the full run: whether the game keeps the
320x112 white-ink-on-alpha post-process (re-dither the pencil art) or
ships greyscale pencil banners directly — decide after reviewing the
demo images.

## Demo

`demoimages/` holds the style proof: all floor 1 and floor 2
creatures (12 encounters + 2 wardens = 14 images), generated by
`gen_demoimages.py` in this folder.
