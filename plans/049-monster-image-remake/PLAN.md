# 049 — monster image remake

## Goal

Remake all creature images in a new style: **black-and-white
semi-realistic 3D render, dark arcade-stage look** — wide horizontal
landscape filled edge to edge with detail, dark and moody, the
creature standing in full side view, rim-lit against the backdrop —
replacing the current 1-bit Bayer-dither pipeline output in
`content/art/creatures/`.

## Scope

**525 images**: every encounter and every warden across the 100 shipped
floors. The full roster — each creature with its description, size
(from the body archetype trait), its floor's landscape, and the
original smaller animal it was before the fever (natives), or a note
that it is made/pressed and not an infected animal — is in
[MONSTERS.md](MONSTERS.md).

Origin key (world-lore.md §5):
- **Native** — the land's own animal grown wrong under the fever; the
  roster names the original smaller animal (`was:` in the floor YAML).
- **Pressed** — a conscripted person (goblins, kobolds, orcs, imps);
  not an infected animal.
- **Wrongmade** — manufactured by the tower (all wardens, the leaks);
  made, not infected.
- Floors 11–100 predate the 038 kind-tagging: marked *untagged*,
  defaulting to Native per world-lore §9, original animal not recorded.

## Style spec

- Black-and-white 3D render, semi-realistic, greyscale only.
- Dark arcade fighting-game stage look: wide horizontal landscape
  backdrop, moody, filled completely with detail edge to edge — no
  empty space.
- One creature standing in full side view (profile), feet on the
  ground, large in frame, sharp silhouette under strong dramatic rim
  light; high contrast, cinematic lighting, realistic textures.
- No color, text, borders, or watermarks.

## Pipeline

Same skeleton as `tools/generate_creatures.py` (prompts assembled from
floor YAML `name` + `prose` + floor landscape; nano-banana-pro via
`plugin_image_gen.providers`, key `LUNA_GEMINI_API_KEY` from
`luna/.env`), but the pencil style replaces the 1-bit STYLE preamble.
Open question before the full run: whether the game keeps the
320x112 white-ink-on-alpha post-process (re-dither the pencil art) or
ships greyscale pencil banners directly — decide after reviewing the
demo images.

## Demo

`demoimages/` holds the style proof: all floor 1 and floor 2
creatures (12 encounters + 2 wardens = 14 images), generated by
`gen_demoimages.py` in this folder. `demoimages2/` is the same set
zoomed out 2x with the creature at the far left/right edge.

`demo-players/` holds the player renders: 3 characters (woman
fighter, male elf, giant — bearded and wide, two heads taller than
the others) x 3 weapons (wand, bow, sword) = 9 images, same look on
a pure black background, generated by `gen_demoplayers.py`. Scale is
enforced after generation by `normalize_players.py` (humans one
common height, giant 1.3x, shared ground line) — rerun it after any
regeneration.

`demoimages3/` holds every player x weapon x monster matchup:
9 player renders x 14 floor 1–2 scenes = 126 composites, named
`{player}_{weapon}_vs_{floorN}_{monster}.jpg`. Built offline by
`gen_demoimages3.py` — no model calls: the figure is keyed off the
black player background (threshold + border flood-fill, so dark armor
interiors survive), scaled to 0.62 of its normalized size, and pasted
at the edge opposite the monster, mirrored to face it, feet on the
scene ground line. Rerun after any player or scene regeneration.
Rejected: the paste reads as glued-on — kept only as reference.

`demoimages4/` is the replacement tactic: the model itself re-renders
the scene with the player in it. `gen_demoimages4.py` sends
nano-banana-pro TWO reference images (the demoimages2/ scene + the
demo-players/ render) and asks it to recreate the scene exactly and
add the character at the opposite edge — grounded, contact shadow,
lit by the scene's light. Approved and scaled to the full grid:
14 monsters x 9 player-weapon combos = 126 images, named floor-first
(`floor{NNN}_{monster}_{player}_{weapon}.jpg`) so they sort by floor.

`demoimages4_1bit/` is the naive 1-bit convert of demoimages4
(320x112 crop, autocontrast, Bayer 8x8, white ink, saved 2x). It
drowns the figures in dither noise — kept as a baseline only.

`demoimages5/` is the readable 1-bit set, produced by
`gen_demoimages5.py`. The formula came out of an iterative lab
(20 tactics A-T over 4 rounds, judged visually on 3 test images):
median-3 denoise, autocontrast, then a thresholded rim-light boost
(high-pass vs gaussian-8, only values >=10 kept, x2.5) so the
rim-lit figure outlines survive while photoreal micro-texture drops,
then gamma-2.0 crush of the midtone landscape, then Bayer 8x8.
CLAHE, Floyd-Steinberg, posterize bands, and edge overlays all lost
to it. Busy mid-grey scenes (the floor-2 cave) remain the hardest
case — small dark creatures on textured rock.

`demoimages6/` is the inverted design (lab round 5, tactic V of
U-X, by `gen_demoimages6.py`): bright dither-shaded landscape, the
characters as solid dark silhouettes — gamma-0.4 lift of the
midtones plus a hard black floor (luma < 55) instead of the dark
crush. Same 126 files, same naming. Rejected: dark terrain became
solid black masses and broke the characters up.

`demoimages7/` fixes that with retinex illumination normalization
(lab rounds 6-7; `gen_demoimages7.py`): each pixel divided by a
gaussian-16 blur of the scene, so large dark terrain flattens into
the bright band (165..255 shades of white) while small locally-dark
blobs — the characters — drop to solid black. 10-image review
sample only (first 10 monsters, cycling all 9 player-weapon combos);
run with explicit names or lift SAMPLE_COUNT for more.

`demoimages8/` is the outlined-style probe (`gen_demoimages8.py`):
the demoimages4 composites re-rendered with bold toon/cel ink
contours. 10-image sample; strong in greyscale (the busy cave scene
finally reads) but the fine ink lines fight the 1-bit downscale.

`demoimages9/` is the single-image iteration pair
(`gen_demoimages9.py` + inline lab rounds): one original render plus
its 1-bit, iterated with roy. Ended at the winning approach — the
model designs the 1-bit dither itself (banner style), post-process
only enforces the grid. Full findings in
[../../vision/1bit-images.md](../../vision/1bit-images.md).

## Floor 1 into the game (production pass)

Everything for floor 1 only, made with the methods in
[../../vision/1bit-images.md](../../vision/1bit-images.md), then wired
into the game.

**Identity comes from the game, not the demos.** Races are
`economy.RACES`: human / elf / dwarf. The weapon is the equipped
weapon's line: blade / bow / staff (048: the weapon decides — damage
type and path both). Demo names map fighter→human, sword→blade,
wand→staff. The demo "giant" is NOT a game race — that third is
regenerated as a **dwarf** (players → two-ref composites → banner
re-render).

Assets (7 monsters: 6 encounters + warden_001):
1. Closeups — done (3D-dither-shaded, floor1/closeups/).
2. Scenes — {id} x {human,elf,dwarf} x {blade,bow,staff} = 63.
   human/elf done under old names; dwarf regenerated.
3. Movies — 63 Veo clips: the player kills the monster with the
   weapon of their line (sword slash / arrow / wand bolt), then the
   kind-correct ending — native: Liberation Dissolve to the `was:`
   animal at true size; pressed: the goblin falls, its sword left in
   the grass; wrongmade: evicted — collapses to dead brush / scrap
   plate, nothing living. Saved as .mp4 + .mpg + 1-bit gif.
4. Game formats: closeup → `creatures/{id}_320x112.png`; scene →
   `creatures/{id}_{race}_{line}_320x112.png`; movie →
   `events/{id}_{verb}_{race}_{line}_320x112.gif` (verb = freed /
   fall / evicted), white ink on alpha, play-once.

Code: `combat._fight_banner` prefers `{slug}_{race}_{line}` art;
`combat._kill_fx` kinded branch prefers `{id}_{verb}_{race}_{line}`.

**Triple check before the swap (must all pass):**
1. **Monster matches** — the creature in every image/movie is the
   yaml `name`/`prose` creature, and native endings show the `was:`
   animal.
2. **Weapon matches** — the kill art slug is keyed by the line of the
   weapon that landed the last blow, and the weapon visible in the
   clip is that weapon.
3. **Player matches** — the art slug is keyed by `p["race"]`, and the
   figure in the image is that race (human woman fighter / male elf /
   dwarf).
